FOB and CIF Options for Aloha Shirts

You decide whether to control the main freight leg for your aloha shirts or leave it to us.

With FOB, we deliver to the agreed port and you book the main freight through your own forwarder. With CIF, we arrange and pay for freight and insurance to the destination port you name. Every shirt is made in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Who Handles Import Clearance, Duties and Transit Risk?

  • Import clearance

    With FOB or CIF, you or your customs broker handle import clearance at the destination, and you pay the duties and import taxes.

  • DDP clearance

    With worldwide DDP, one all-in price covers delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

  • Choosing your term

    DDP suits first-time importers and buyers who don't have a regular customs broker. FOB or CIF suits buyers who import routinely and already work with an established broker.

  • Direct factory orders

    Our factory-owned B2B business handles both production and export. Your order comes directly through our team rather than through online shops or marketplaces, and our shipments have reached over 55 countries.

  • Developing your shirts

    With our OEM and ODM development, a new style starts from your design, a picture or an idea.

Under FOB and CIF, the risk of loss or damage passes to you once the goods are loaded aboard the vessel at the agreed loading port.

With FOB, you arrange cargo insurance from that port. With CIF, you claim any transit loss on the policy we provide. With DDP, transit risk stays with us until your shipment reaches your door, ready for unloading.

Payment Terms and Dispatch Timelines

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment. We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. With worldwide DDP, one all-in price covers delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

If your launch date is close, ask about expedited Bulk Production; we confirm the rush timeline in the quote. Sample rushing depends on schedule availability, and the surcharge is quoted as a percentage of order value before you confirm and is paid with your deposit.

Hawaiian Shirt Shipping: Freight Methods and Transit Times

  • Air express

    Timing and selection
    DHL, FedEx, UPS, EMS or TNT delivers in 5 to 7 days. For shipments under 100 kg, express is the quickest choice for small or urgent orders.

  • Sea freight

    Timing and selection
    Once a shipment goes over roughly 300 kg, sea freight costs less and suits larger bulk orders.

  • Air and rail

    Timing and selection
    We also arrange air freight and rail freight to Europe. When your deadline allows, sea or rail suits larger orders.

  • Route and schedule

    Timing and selection
    We ship regularly to the US, EU, UK, Australia and other markets. The option you choose determines the shipping time.

  • Transport selection

    Timing and selection
    We choose transport based on total shipment weight and your deadline, and we pick the most sustainable method whenever your schedule allows.

How Order Size and Packing Affect Freight Costs

  • Destination requirements

    Your freight cost depends on where the shirts are going and that country's customs rules. It also depends on the product category and how fast you need delivery.

  • Carton measurements

    We calculate freight from the shipment measurements. These come from the number of cartons and each carton's dimensions and weight.

  • Firm freight figures

    We give you a firm freight figure once you confirm final quantities, destination and delivery urgency. Any figure before that is an estimate.

FCL sea freight is priced per container; smaller LCL orders pay per cubic metre plus handling at both ports. Air freight bills the higher of actual or volumetric weight (L×W×H cm ÷ 6,000; couriers like DHL use ÷ 5,000).

We fold shirts to shrink cartons, because volume sets LCL and air charges on light, bulky loads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What loss or damage does the cargo insurance you arrange under CIF cover?

Our standard CIF insurance covers 110% of the invoice value under Institute Cargo Clauses (C). That protects you against fire, explosion, the vessel sinking, stranding or colliding, jettison and general average. It doesn't include the extra cover for theft, non-delivery, water damage and handling damage that Clauses (A) give you. If you want those risks covered, ask us for all-risks Clauses (A) cover before shipment and we'll arrange it.

Roughly how many aloha shirts fit in one export carton, and what are the usual carton dimensions and weight I should use to estimate air or sea freight?

A standard 60 × 40 × 40 cm carton holds about 50 to 60 folded aloha shirts, each in its own polybag. It weighs around 12 to 15 kg gross and takes up about 0.096 m³. For air freight, its volumetric weight comes to about 16 kg. Before shipment, our packing list confirms the exact carton counts, dimensions and weights.

Does choosing rayon, cotton, polyester or a blend, or a men's versus women's cut, change the import duty and the DDP price for my aloha shirts?

Yes. Men’s woven aloha shirts come under HS heading 6205 and women’s under 6206. The subheading follows whichever fibre makes up most of the weight, whether that's cotton, a man-made fibre such as polyester or rayon, linen or silk. Your fabric and cut set the duty rate that applies in your country. We work out DDP duty from your exact fabric and cut, so changing either one changes the price, and we confirm the code with you before bulk production.
