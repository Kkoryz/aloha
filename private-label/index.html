Private Label Hawaiian Shirts Made for Your Brand

You don't have to build a design from scratch to launch under your own brand.

Start from one of our base aloha shirt styles, then change the fit details, print, trims and branding. We make every shirt ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

For your private label order, we supply care labels, size labels, woven labels, printed neck labels and hang tags, along with custom buttons, packaging and other trims.

Order Minimums and Production Schedules

You can order from 50 pieces per style in each colour, which gives small and growing labels a way in.

We agree first-trial quantities with you case by case, and production requirements set how far we can flex. Your order then goes through the development, approval and delivery steps below.

With our Product Development service, our in-house design team works with you on fit, pattern, grading, print, fabric and trim development. That applies whether you bring an OEM design or need us to develop an ODM collection from your requirements and brand positioning.

Our factory and export business has been running since 2011. We work directly with business buyers worldwide, not through online shops or marketplaces, and we produce for more than 350 independent labels.

Before you order, we can show you case examples where we have permission, but we never disclose unreleased designs covered by an NDA. For fabric options, you can choose cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk or wool.

Blends include 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton. Our Customisation service puts your company logo on the shirts and matches your brand colours to Pantone references. Our Embroidery service covers intricate embroidery prepared by a designer, plus patches and labels.

Embroidered logos stand up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying. Our Garment Dyeing service includes Pantone matching at no extra charge, plus one round of lab dips for you to approve before bulk production.

Under our Custom Print Design service, unchanged base shirts and library prints stay non-exclusive. A new or adapted print, meaning one recoloured, rescaled or reworked from a library design or developed from your artwork, becomes exclusive to your label under NDA once you pay its design fee.

The unchanged original stays in our library.

Branded Buttons, Trims, and Buyer-Supplied Materials

We plan your branding together with the garment order.

  • Order-specific branding

    We prepare labels, tags and packaging for each style and colour you order; your garment minimum stays at 50 pieces, while printed packaging keeps its own minimums.

  • Custom button minimums

    Through our Custom Buttons service, you can get custom-dyed or logo-engraved buttons, including coconut, wood and natural shell, at the button maker's minimum of 1,000 pieces per design in each colour. We store the spares for your reorders.

  • Tooling and smaller runs

    For Custom Buttons, we quote moulds separately before you approve and keep them for 3 years so they can be reused. Colour-matched stock buttons have no separate minimum and work at the 50-piece minimum per style in each colour.

  • Your supplied materials

    We accept your own buttons, labels, hang tags, other trims, fabric and packaging, including deliveries from a supplier you nominate. We then sew, attach or pack them into your shirts.

  • Delivery quantities and timing

    When you supply materials, they have to arrive before cutting, with 3% spare trims and labels and 5–8% extra fabric beyond the consumption we calculate. We report any shortages or defects within 2 business days of receiving them.

  • Button compatibility checks

    For Custom Buttons, the buttons you supply have to be 14L to 24L, with two or four holes at least 1.5 mm wide. We attach them to your sample before bulk to confirm they fit the buttonholes and sew on securely.

  • Delicate shirt tagging

    With our Packaging service, rayon and silk shirt bodies stay free of holes and marks. We put fasteners only through the underarm seam or inner neck label, or tie cotton string through a buttonhole.

  • Linen and tag guns

    For Packaging, linen gets a string loop through a buttonhole or woven label, or a small safety pin through the label. Tag-gun pins go only into seam allowances or care labels, never into the shirt body.

Label Specifications and Related Resources

Our Trims & Accessories specifications list the size and detail limits for each label type.

Woven labels run from 10 × 30 mm to 60 × 100 mm with lettering of at least 1.5 mm, or 1 mm lettering and 0.3 mm lines on high-definition damask.

Printed satin and taffeta labels run from 15 × 30 mm to 50 × 150 mm with 1 mm text, roughly 5 pt. Printed cotton takes lettering from 1.5 mm and lines from 0.2 mm.

Heat-transfer neck labels go up to 60 × 60 mm, with lettering no smaller than 1.5 mm and lines of 0.2 mm or more so they stay readable through washing.

If your artwork goes past these detail limits, it has to be simplified or moved to a larger label, and you approve its legibility on a proof before production.

From Label Artwork to Approved Samples

We agree the price and artwork with you first, then make the samples you approve for production.

  1. Prepare your brief

    In your quote request, list your confirmed logo files, repeat artwork, label artwork, care wording, packaging references and retail requirements apart from the fabric, printing, trim and fit decisions that are still open.

  2. Agree the quote

    Under our process, we quote before any sampling. We then coordinate fabric, sewing, embroidery, printing and finishing on one timeline with one contact, and you can go straight to our production team with technical questions.

  3. Approve label development

    For custom labels and hang tags, we create any missing artwork free of charge and send digital proofs in 3-5 days for you to approve before production. We then make physical samples in 7-15 days, alongside your shirt sampling.

  4. Review shirt samples

    A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per pattern or print and covers all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  5. Arrange sample delivery

    For sampling, courier delivery costs you USD 50 on top of the sample fee.

  6. Discuss your decisions

    You can contact our team in English by phone or video call at every stage, and we reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Label Production, Finishing, and Packing

Before production starts, we need your final artwork, the content for your care and size labels, and your folding and bagging instructions.

  1. Confirm production funding

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order. Bulk production starts once you've approved the sample and we've received that deposit.

  2. Produce and attach labels

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, and order size and season decide where in that range your order falls. We make the labels alongside your shirts and sew the neck and care labels into the seams during assembly.

  3. Avoid late label changes

    Adding labels after assembly means opening finished seams or adding an extra, visible attachment step, which lowers quality and delays shipment. We deliver your shirts tagged, labelled, folded and bagged the way you specify.

  4. Check production quality

    Our quality control covers fabric, cutting, sewing, random checks and final inspection. OEKO-TEX Standard 100 covers the tested fabrics and trims, along with GOTS organic cotton, BCI cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 management and WRAP social compliance. You're welcome to bring in SGS or BV inspections.

  5. Settle and dispatch

    You pay the remaining 50% before shipment, on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  6. Clear branded goods together

    With our DDP Shipping service, the cartons carry none of our branding, and your shirts and packaging clear customs together on one invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where on the shirt can my labels be placed, can I add an outside flag label, and which label folds can I choose?

Your labels can go at the inside neck or back yoke, in the left side seam or at the inside hem, and you can add an outside flag label on the chest pocket, side hem or sleeve. Woven labels come centre-folded, end-folded, loop-folded or straight-cut, and we also make hem tabs and sleeve flags in damask, satin or taffeta.

How do you keep sewn or heat-transfer labels from puckering, itching or showing through on lightweight rayon shirts?

On lightweight rayon and polyester we use soft satin or fine damask labels with heat-sealed edges, sewn only along the top edge into the back neck seam with a fine needle and eased thread tension so the fabric does not pucker and no stiff edge rubs the skin, and on pale or sheer prints we choose a label base colour close to the ground so it does not show through. Heat-transfer labels go on at about 150 °C for 10 to 12 seconds with a low-temperature adhesive film suited to rayon and polyester, and we wash-test them on your fabric at sampling so they stay intact for more than 50 washes.

Which of my label, tag, button and packaging choices will push up the cost per shirt?

Woven damask labels cost more than printed satin or cotton labels. Cost also rises with label size and with each extra thread colour, and a denser weave for finer detail needs more time on the loom. End-fold, loop-fold and mitre-fold labels need an extra folding step that straight-cut labels do not. A label sewn down on all four edges takes longer to attach than one caught in a seam. Your price per shirt already includes a standard printed neck label and care label. Woven or embossed labels are not part of that standard and increase the cost per piece. Tagless heat-transfer labels also add to the cost per shirt, as do hang tags with foil stamping, spot UV, embossing, die-cut shapes or thicker card. If your custom branded buttons need a mould, that charge appears as its own line on your quote, and you see it before you approve.

Can you develop care-label instructions that account for the shirt's fabric, print and applied branding rather than using generic washing instructions?

Yes: we base care instructions on wash tests of your fabric and print together with any applied branding, such as embroidered logos, heat-transfer labels or printed artwork, and add lines like "wash inside out" or "do not iron on decoration" where that branding needs it. You confirm the final care wording before production, and our QC checks it on every private-label order.
