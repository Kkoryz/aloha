Fabrics Matched to Your Brand

Before any swatches go out to you, we check each fabric against your tech pack for GSM, hand feel, stretch and intended end use.

When you need Hawaiian fabric sourcing for your aloha shirts, we source the fabric and make the shirts in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We source knits, wovens, performance and technical fabrics, sustainable and specialty materials, and the trims and accessories to match.

Every material is checked for quality, consistency and compliance before production starts.

Fabric Blends, Weaves & Finishes

  • Blends and construction

    What it means for your shirts
    We develop blends such as 55% linen 45% rayon or 70% rayon 30% linen. They come in plain, twill, dobby, seersucker or jacquard weaves, at weights from 80 to 200 GSM. Custom development starts at 1,000 metres per quality, and we confirm the exact minimum for your fabric type at sampling.

  • Finishes and cost

    What it means for your shirts
    Peach, enzyme and silicone softening, easy-care wrinkle resistance and UV finishes add cost. The price per metre also goes up with finer combed or long-staple yarns, denser or more complex weaves and heavier GSM.

  • Comparing suitable options

    What it means for your shirts
    When a lower-cost fabric and a higher-spec fabric both meet your spec, we show you them side by side. An upgrade is worth paying for where wearers see or feel it, such as softening on a resort shirt. The lower-cost choice gives up some softness, drape or easy-care performance for a lower shirt cost.

  • Printed fabric choices

    What it means for your shirts
    Our Hawaiian Fabrics range covers cotton poplin at 110–140 GSM; cotton lawn at 80–110 GSM; rayon challis, rayon crepe and other viscose at 100–130 GSM; silk crepe de chine at 60–90 GSM; linen, linen-cotton and linen-rayon at 140–180 GSM; and polyester or poly-cotton peachskin and other light polyester blends at 100–130 GSM. For lighter or heavier specifications, we source from 60 to 200 GSM.

  • Fabric-only printed orders

    What it means for your shirts
    When you order printed fabric only through Hawaiian Fabrics, existing-library and custom prints both start at 300 metres per colourway. We supply the fabric in rolls of about 50 to 100 metres. Each roll is inspected for shade, defects and width, then packed on a core in sealed export film.

  • Factory accountability

    What it means for your shirts
    With factory ownership, production and export stay inside one company, so we control quality, cost and delivery directly.

Custom Colours & Yarn-Dyed Patterns

  • Yarn-dyed patterns

    Development and approval
    We develop stripes, checks and jacquards, and one round of yarn-colour lab dips is included. Before the yarn is dyed for weaving, we compare each dip with your physical colour standard under daylight and under store lighting. After weaving, the colour is fixed and can't be corrected the way a print can.

  • Custom-dyed colours

    Development and approval
    Custom colours start at 300 metres per colour. We confirm the exact minimum at sampling based on fabric type and dye method. One lab-dip round is included, and in-stock colour-card shades have no fabric minimum.

  • Colours across sets

    Development and approval
    For Matching Family & Group Sets from toddler sizes to 5XL, polyester swim fabric, rayon and cotton each look different against the same Pantone reference. So we match each base separately for you to approve side by side, scale the artwork by size and sample the whole set together.

Library Fabrics vs. Custom Material Development

We start with our fabric library. When no library quality meets the drape, stretch or fibre content your design needs, we develop custom material.

  • Resort fabric options

    Beyond our core range, we offer drapey rayon and performance stretch fabrics, so you have different options for how a resort shirt moves and feels.

  • Ready for sampling

    Library fabrics are already tested, so we sample and produce them straight away with no separate material-development round.

  • Custom material development

    Developing your own quality takes its own sampling round and a larger fabric commitment than picking an existing library fabric.

  • Exclusivity trade-off

    When you choose a library quality over custom development, you give up having a cloth exclusive to your brand.

Sustainable Fabrics & Printing Options

Start with your claim

First we ask what sustainability claim you plan to print on your labels or hang tags. Then we choose a fabric whose certification backs that claim up.

Certified purchasing route

Certification documents only come with fabric bought through a certified supply chain. They can't be added after the fabric has been bought.

Sustainable material choices

We use sustainable fabrics wherever we can, including organic cotton, recycled polyester, certified recycled nylon and Better Cotton (BCI). Eco-friendly dyes and sustainable packaging are also available.

Recycled polyester documentation

We hold GRS (Global Recycled Standard) certification for recycled polyester and send the documentation with your order. Other certified fabrics are supplied on request, and the fabric you select decides which certificates you get.

Plain-colour dye selection

Our partner mills use OEKO-TEX certified dyes for plain colours. We choose the fabric and its supporting certification to meet your collection's requirements.

Printing limits and consumption

With our Custom Printing, we use sublimation only on light-coloured polyester or on blends with at least 60% polyester. Screen-print repeats go up to 64 cm and digital/sublimation repeats go up to 150 cm.

Buttons, Labels & Other Material Details

  • Trims & Accessories

    With our Trims & Accessories service, we order trims along with your fabric and allow 7 to 15 days for them, so they arrive at least 3 days before cutting. We only start cutting once the trims have been checked against the approved card and every material has arrived.

  • Custom Buttons

    Through Custom Buttons, we supply resin, urea, plastic, shell, coconut, wood, metal and fabric-covered buttons, matched to your design, fabric and order.

  • Custom Labels & Hang Tags

    With Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we write the fibre, wash-tested care and origin information from the materials you're actually using. We add any required RN or CA numbers, follow the rules of the country you're shipping to and get your approval on the artwork.

Materials Only or Finished Shirts

Finished-shirt orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Full-package responsibility

    With Bulk Production, we source the materials and do the cutting and sewing in our workshops. That means one team is responsible for any shrinkage or shade problems. We also offer design and development.

  • Sourcing-only orders

    If you already trust another sewing factory, we can source just the fabric and trims. Stock and custom-dyed fabrics start at 300 metres per colour, and trims have the same minimums as garment orders.

  • Separate sourcing fee

    We charge separately for finding and coordinating your materials. Fabric, trims, labels and shipping each get their own line on the same itemised quote, so the fee isn't hidden in the material prices.

  • Inspection and delivery

    We inspect sourcing-only materials and send you reports and photos when we deliver them to your factory. We ship to any country by express, air or sea on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

  • Development and trial orders

    Through Product Development, we support OEM and ODM working from your design, a picture or just an idea. We agree first-trial quantities case by case, within what production allows.

  • Your supplied materials

    For Cut & Sew, your materials have to arrive before cutting, with 5–8% extra fabric and 3% spare trims and labels. We inspect everything on arrival and report any shortages or defects within 2 business days, before anything is used.

  • Your design ownership

    Under our NDA, Custom Print Design keeps prints developed from your artwork exclusive to you. You own the finished design once it's paid for, and the tech packs are yours to keep. Paid print files are available on request, and we release stored pattern files for a charge per pattern.

  • Package selection

    If we sew your shirts too, the full package keeps all material corrections in one place. With sourcing-only, your existing factory does the cutting and sewing.

From Your Brief to Fabric Options & Pricing

We narrow your shortlist by technical specification, target retail price and how the shirts will be worn.

  1. Send your brief

    Send your tech pack, reference samples and fabric requirements through our enquiry page. We reply within one business day and sort out any questions before sourcing starts.

  2. Receive your shortlist

    Within 5 business days of getting your brief, we send you 3 to 5 fabric options that meet your specification. Physical swatches reach you by courier 5 to 7 days after that.

  3. Compare fabric specifications

    Each option lists fibre composition, GSM, usable width, weave or knit construction, available colours, and the mill's shrinkage and colourfastness data.

  4. Calculate material requirements

    Within 2 business days of receiving your patterns, we send you the fabric consumption per size and for the total order quantity, plus an extra 3% cutting allowance.

  5. Agree itemised pricing

    Our quotation includes fabric recommendations, a timeline and line-item pricing. You get a full itemised quote before production, with no hidden fees.

  6. Approve and track

    Under Our Process, we agree the price before sampling and agree timing before production, based on fabric availability, artwork readiness, trims and shipping. Then we place the order and track it.

Samples, Approval & Production Timelines

If you settle on the final fabric before you approve the sample, bulk production uses exactly the material you signed off.

  1. Confirm fabric availability

    Stock fabrics reach our factory in 3 to 5 days. Custom-dyed or custom-developed fabrics add 15 to 20 days before bulk production starts.

  2. Make your sample

    A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days, so you have the garment in hand to review before you approve production.

  3. Account for development fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per design and covers its pattern or print and all its colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  4. Arrange sample delivery

    Sample courier delivery costs USD 50, which you pay on top of the sample fee.

  5. Release bulk production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season set the exact schedule.

  6. Control changes and shipment

    If you switch to custom-dyed or custom-developed fabric after approval, the fabric lead time starts again and bulk production is delayed. Every completed order goes through a full final inspection before it ships.

Fabric Certifications & Quality Checks

We check your fabric when it arrives, check the cutting and sewing, and carry out random checks during production.

  • Verify certificate coverage

    Before we order, we get the supplier’s current certificate and check that it covers your specific fabric and the production site, not just the mill in general.

  • Check certificate validity

    Certificates expire and cover only the products and sites named on them. Where it applies, we work with suppliers holding recognised organic-textile and chemical-safety certifications.

  • Confirm style-specific evidence

    Through Quality Control, we confirm that the certificates cover your fabric, trims, dyes and inks, children’s styles included, and we send you current copies on request.

  • Support UPF claims

    We state a rating such as UPF 50+ only when a fabric report to AS/NZS 4399 or EN 13758-1 backs it up, and we supply that report with your order.

  • Test shrinkage before sampling

    Our preshrunk printed rayon and cotton must stay within 3% shrinkage in length and width after three home washes, and we check colour bleed before sampling.

  • Check finished measurements

    Your garments stay within ±1 cm at chest, length and waist, and within ±0.5 cm at the collar, cuff and other small measurement points.

  • Measure colourfastness performance

    Printed and dyed fabrics, high-twist chiffon included, reach Grade 4 or above for washing and Grade 3–4 or above for rubbing and light.

  • Arrange independent testing

    You’re welcome to bring in SGS or BV for inspection. If you ask for testing at an accredited lab, including US flammability testing to 16 CFR Part 1610, you approve the booking and prepay the lab fee for each item.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you source fabric from a specific origin I ask for, such as Japanese or Korean mills, and give me proof of where it came from?

Yes. We source from the country you ask for, Japanese rayon or Korean polyester included, and give you the mill’s commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin for the lot. Imported fabrics add 10 to 20 days to the fabric lead time before cutting.

Can you supply sample-length fabric cuts for my factory to make trial shirts before I commit to bulk fabric?

Yes. We courier cuts of 3 to 5 metres per fabric to your factory within 7 to 10 days of your request. That’s enough for one or two trial shirts.

Can you source our specified fabric directly from a mill we nominate rather than asking us to purchase and supply it?

Yes. Send us the mill’s contact, the article number and the minimum. We order directly, track the delivery so it arrives before cutting, and inspect the fabric when it arrives.

If our approved fabric is discontinued before a repeat collection, can you source an alternative that preserves its drape, weight and print appearance?

Yes. We let you know when you reorder, then source the closest replacement against the reference swatch we keep with your stored pattern, your approved sample or a previous shirt. We keep the same fibre type and stay within 10 GSM of the original weight. Matching the original Pantone reference doesn’t guarantee the same colour or hand feel on the new base, so you approve a swatch, a strike-off and, when needed, a new sample before bulk. We never substitute without your sign-off.

Which fabric choices push the price up, for example custom-dyed versus stock fabric, or certified versus conventional materials?

Stock fabrics in existing colours cost the least. Custom-dyed and custom-developed fabrics cost more, and so do finer combed or long-staple yarns, heavier GSM, denser or more complex weaves and extra finishing treatments.

Does the 50-piece minimum apply to swimwear, and do jacquard, lurex or recycled swim fabrics need larger quantities?

Yes. Swimwear in jacquard, lurex or recycled swim fabrics from our existing range starts at 50 pieces per style in each colour. Custom-developed jacquard or lurex starts at 1,000 metres per quality, and we confirm the exact minimum at sampling. We don’t custom-dye swim fabrics; the colours come from printing or from existing shades.

How do usable fabric width, one-way tropical prints and large repeats affect fabric consumption and material cost per shirt?

A short-sleeve aloha shirt uses about 1.6–1.8 m at 140–150 cm usable width, against around 2.2–2.4 m at 110 cm, so wider cloth brings down your material cost per shirt. Large repeats or pattern matching take 10–15% more fabric, and we build that into your unit price.
