Swim Trunks From Our Factory in China

You choose the fabric, print, length, liner, label and overall design, and we make your swim trunks ourselves in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

If you need an OEM swim trunks manufacturer for custom branded men's and women's styles, we make them in your colours with your logos and prints, and we can coordinate them with rash guards and tech tees into a matching line.

Our trunks go to surf and resort retail, hotel and pool shops, swim teams and water sports clubs, and we also make hybrid shorts that go from beach to street.

Wholesale Swim Trunks: Minimums & Trial Orders

Wholesale swim trunks start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can order, and we agree your first trial run case by case.

Under our Bulk Production service, you can split each style's quantity across 3 to 4 sizes. We've run our own factory, with production and export in one company, since 2011, and you agree every order directly with our team.

A standard swim trunk sample is ready in 10 to 15 days, and we make it once you've agreed the quoted price.

  • Sample and design fees

    Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design in all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against the bulk order.

  • Sample courier delivery

    Courier delivery of samples costs USD 50, which you pay on top of the sample fee.

  • Itemised quote in 24 hours

    Within 24 hours you get an itemised quote with no obligation, priced on quantity, construction, fabric, lining, print method, labels, packaging and lead time.

  • Launch timing

    Plan to confirm your samples and pay the deposit at least 90 days before you need the goods in your warehouse, or earlier for January–April orders.

  • Production and sea transit

    Those 90 days cover 35 to 40 days of bulk production and 25 to 35 days by sea to the US or Europe, so approve by mid-January for May stock.

  • Base price inclusions

    Our base price covers two side pockets, one back flap pocket, three eyelets, 2–3 wash labels, 1–2 hangtags and one standard drawcord.

  • Priced upgrades

    Our boardshorts page lists each upgrade that raises your unit price, such as four-way stretch, a compression liner or full sublimation.

Starting from your brief or tech pack, your order goes through sampling, your approval, bulk production and a full final inspection before it ships. You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Swim Fabrics, Custom Qualities & Bulk Print Consistency for Your Trunks

  • Stock swim fabrics

    Through our Fabric Sourcing service, you can pick jacquard, lurex and recycled swim fabrics from our existing range at the same minimum of 50 pieces per style in each colour.

  • Custom-developed fabric qualities

    If you want a custom jacquard or lurex quality developed through Fabric Sourcing, it starts from 1,000 metres per quality, which we confirm at sampling. We plan its colours around printing or existing shades.

  • Print consistency in bulk

    The sample you approve sets the print standard for bulk. Colour stays within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm, and nothing changes unless you approve it in writing.

Custom Swim Trunks: Prints, Patterns & Colours

  • Floral prints and design library

    Send us your own floral artwork in any colour palette and we'll print it with full sublimation. You can also pick a ready-made swim trunk design from our library and customise it.

  • Camo in any scheme

    We make camo in any colour scheme you want, whether that's woodland, urban grey, digital camo or a colourway you specify for your line.

  • Ombre and face prints

    For ombre trunks, you choose the start and end colours and we blend from one to the other with sublimation. For face prints, we tile the photo you send and print it with high-resolution sublimation.

  • Solid base colours

    Our standard solid range has more than 10 base colours: black, white, navy, royal blue, sky blue, red, coral, yellow, kelly green, olive, grey and orange.

  • Fabric strike-off first

    Before bulk printing starts, we send you a sublimated strike-off on the actual swim fabric so you can approve the colours. Colours on polyester swim fabric come out differently from how they look on a screen or a paper print.

  • Exclusive resort prints

    Through our Custom Print Design service, we turn your sketches, moodboards or references into repeat artwork that's ready for production, with colour separation, scaled repeats, refined placement and 3D mockups.

  • Matching sets across fabrics

    Through Matching Family & Group Sets, we match every fabric base to a single Pantone reference. You approve the strike-offs side by side, and we scale the artwork from toddler sizes up to 5XL.

  • Your prints stay yours

    Through our Private Label service, any print we develop from your own artwork stays your intellectual property under our NDA, and we never sell it to another client.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference in construction between your elastic-waist swim trunks and your fixed-waist board shorts, and which one should my line use?

Our elastic-waist swim trunks have a fully elastic waistband with a drawcord inside. Our fixed-waist board shorts have a flat or half-elastic waistband that closes with a lace-up, hook-and-loop or mock fly. An elastic waist stretches to fit a wide range of waist sizes. A fixed waistband barely gives, so each size fits a narrower range of wearers. Choose elastic-waist trunks for pool, resort and casual swim lines, where every size has to fit lots of wearers. Choose fixed-waist board shorts for surf and active lines, where the waist has to stay in place in the waves.

Which rust-resistant zips, eyelets and drawcord tips do you use, and can the aglets or cords carry my branding?

For salt water, we use plastic coil or moulded zips, silicone or plastic drawcord tips that don't rust, and eyelets in coated rustproof metal or embroidered thread. Your logo can go on the aglets, and we can also weave or print your branding along the drawcord itself. A branded cord is priced separately from our standard drawcord.

Which logo methods do you use on swim trunks, and which last best through repeated swimming and washing?

We apply logos with sublimation, silicone transfer, embroidery and patches. Sublimated logos and silicone transfers hold up longest through repeated swimming and washing, because they bond into the fabric or flex with it. Embroidery and patches work best on woven board-short fabric and dry a little more slowly.

How do your standard mesh and compression liners differ in support, ventilation, drying and thigh coverage, and where do sport mesh and power mesh fit among these choices?

Our standard mesh liner is a brief-cut lining with light support. It ventilates freely and dries fastest. Our compression liner is a stretch short with firmer support that covers the upper thigh, but it dries a little more slowly. Sport mesh is the open, breathable fabric we use for brief liners. Power mesh is the firmer stretch mesh we use when you want compression-level support with more airflow.
