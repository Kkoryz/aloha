Beach Towels Made in China

We make your beach towels in cotton, microfibre, velour or recycled PET (RPET), including RPET blends, so you can choose the material that suits your programme.

Our partner towel mill weaves the terry, and we print, finish and check your towels in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Materials for Custom Beach Towels

Material or serviceWhat it means for your towels
Cotton loop terryWith pile on both sides, the towel is absorbent and soft. The trade-off is that the looped surface prints less smoothly.
Sheared velourOur partner towel mill shears one terry face smooth and leaves loops on the back. Your print comes out sharper, but the sheared side is less absorbent and less soft than full loop terry. You can have it in 100% cotton or a 65% polyester 35% cotton blend.
MicrofibreFine split yarn in 80% polyester and 20% polyamide is knitted into flat suede or waffle structures. That gives you towels that are light, dry quickly and pack down easily.
RPET constructionsYou can choose 100% recycled polyester or a 50% RPET and 50% cotton blend. The recycled yarn makes up either the knitted microfibre base or the blend's pile, with cotton loops on the other side.
Factory ownershipOur factory-owned business has been running since 2011. It handles both production and export, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. Orders are agreed only through our team, never through online shops or third-party marketplaces.
Fabric sourcingThrough our Fabric Sourcing service, you can pick from our fabric library or have a custom material developed.

Private Label Beach Towels With Your Logo

  • Smooth-face printing

    On smooth microfibre and velour faces we use sublimation or fine screen printing. Text stays sharp down to 6 pt and lines down to 0.3 mm, and screen-printed designs keep their bold look through repeated washing.

  • Loop-terry artwork

    Loops break up fine detail, so your screen prints need bold shapes, text of at least 12 pt and lines of at least 1 mm. Jacquard lettering has to be at least 10 mm high.

  • Reverse-side limits

    Double-sided microfibre and RPET towels can carry a different design on each side. Cotton and velour backs stay plain terry and take only a woven label or a screen-printed logo of up to 2 colours, no larger than 30 x 30 cm.

  • Woven and stitched branding

    With jacquard and embroidery, your branding becomes part of the towel and won't fade or crack. Neither one can reproduce full-colour photographic artwork. We also offer woven or rubber patches and woven or printed labels at the hems or corners.

  • Retail and resort channels

    We make printed towels for your shop, summer promotions or outdoor lifestyle brand, and heavier cotton terry for hotel pools. You choose the material, GSM, size and branding for each channel.

  • Custom digital printing

    Our Custom Printing service reproduces your all-over or placement artwork, gradients and shading included, for a small digital-printing surcharge. Polyester is printed by sublimation and nylon by digital pigment printing.

  • Matching collection colours

    For Matching Family & Group Sets, keep in mind that each fibre reproduces Pantone colours differently. We match every base to one reference and get your approval on a side-by-side sample. Then we scale the artwork across sizes from toddler up to 5XL.

  • Private label options

    Our Private Label service is open at every order quantity. It covers labels, hang tags, branded metal tags, logo application and silicone branding, and we make the woven labels and heat-transfer artwork in-house.

OEM and ODMWith our Product Development service, your OEM or ODM towel can start from your design, a picture or an idea.
Custom dyeingOur Garment Dyeing service custom-dyes cotton and rayon to Grade 4 or above for washing colourfastness and Grade 3–4 or above for rubbing and light. It uses reactive dyes, a fixing after-treatment and lab-dip fastness checks for dark brand colours before bulk approval.

Towel Production, Quality Checks and Shipping

  1. 01

    Release your order

    Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, your order goes into bulk production against the agreed specification.

  2. 02

    Produce your towels

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Your order size and the season decide where it falls in that range.

  3. 03

    Check the goods

    Our Quality Control runs from incoming fabric to a full final inspection. It confirms the certification for each style, including OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and GRS documentation for certified recycled goods, and you're welcome to bring in independent SGS or BV inspection.

  4. 04

    Prepare retail packs

    Our Packaging service offers rolled towels with branded bands, retail folds with paper bands or header cards, and printed boxes with hang tags. Folded towels go into individual polybags.

  5. 05

    Select your transport

    With our Shipping service, air express takes 5 to 7 days and is the quickest choice below 100 kg. Above roughly 300 kg, sea freight is more economical. Full export paperwork comes with either option.

  6. 06

    Deliver your shipment

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you print my beach towels with the same artwork as my aloha shirts so they sell as a matching resort set?

Yes. We print your beach towels with the same artwork as your aloha shirts, so you get a print-matched resort set.

What dimensional tolerance can you maintain against the ordered towel length and width?

For adult, oversized and round towels, finished length and width stay within -2 to 2 cm of the dimensions you order. For kids’ towels, they stay within -1 to 1 cm. Your approved sample sets the target size, and at final inspection we check every towel against it.

Which towel specifications, such as material, GSM, size or print method, move the price most?

Material, GSM and size move the unit price most, because heavier cotton terry, velour and larger towels use more yarn per piece. Print method and the number of screen colours come next, followed by jacquard or embroidery, edge finishes, fringe, loops, carry accessories and retail packaging.

What wash shrinkage should I expect from each towel construction, and can you supply preshrunk options?

Microfibre and RPET towels shrink less than 2%. Every towel construction uses preshrunk fabric as standard, so there's no extra step for you.

Which towel specifications are suitable for repeated pool exposure and commercial laundering in hotel or resort programmes?

For guest comfort and commercial laundering up to 60°C, we specify 100% cotton loop terry up to 500 GSM, yarn-dyed or solid dyed, with double-stitched dobby or bound edges and a hanging loop. Where towels face heavy chlorine and sun, 80% polyester 20% polyamide microfibre or RPET at 200 to 300 GSM holds its colour longer and dries faster, but you give up the heavy, plush feel of cotton. Commercial laundering, chlorine and strong sun wear towels out faster than retail use does, so your washing and exposure conditions decide the construction.

What colourfastness performance can your towel options meet under sunlight, seawater and chlorinated pool exposure?

Against seawater and chlorinated pool water, our sublimated microfibre and RPET towels reach Grade 3–4 or above to ISO 105-E02 and ISO 105-E03. Cotton terry reaches Grade 3 or above.
