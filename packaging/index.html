Hawaiian Shirt Packaging Options

We make your complete aloha shirt packaging set to your specifications, from poly bags, hang tags and woven labels to rigid boxes and mailers.

The shirts come from our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, where we make them ourselves. Tissue, boxes, bags, labels and tags all go through one coordinated production run, so you work with one contact and receive everything in one delivery.

Coordinated Packaging Sets for Retail-Ready Shirts

For retail-ready orders, our packing line folds and finishes each shirt with tissue, brand cards, hang tags, barcode labels, paper inserts and collar support.

The outer packaging goes on after that. For tissue, you can pick 17 gsm or 22 gsm acid-free logo tissue, recycled tissue or translucent glassine-style tissue. It comes in white, ivory, black, kraft brown and about 30 stock colours, and you can also choose Pantone-dyed options.

We print your logo as a repeat in 1 to 4 colours, and metallic gold or silver ink is one of the choices. We answer every enquiry within one business day.

Minimum Orders and Trial Runs

  • Garment minimums

    Garment orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which works for small and growing labels.

  • Packaging minimums

    Rigid boxes and complex packaging start at 50 units. Labelling minimums depend on the technique.

  • Trial quantities

    We agree your first trial quantity with you case by case, within production limits.

  • Focused customisation

    On a trial, you can customise one or two brand-critical pieces and use stock components for everything else, so the custom work stays concentrated.

Sample Pricing and Turnaround

  • Quote first

    Under our process, we send you a quote first, and sampling starts once you agree the price.

  • Sample timing

    We have your standard sample ready in 10 to 15 days.

  • Sample charges

    Each sample costs USD 50. This charge is not refunded and is not credited against bulk orders.

  • Design charges

    Each design costs USD 50, covering its pattern or print in all its colourways. This fee is not refunded or credited.

From Your Brief to the Finished Order

  1. Brief and sample

    We build the sample you review from your brief or tech pack.

  2. Approval and production

    Once you approve the sample and pay the deposit, your order moves out of development and into bulk production.

  3. Inspection and dispatch

    We carry out a full final inspection before your finished order goes on to shipment.

Production Lead Times and Rush Packaging

  • Packaging proposal

    We send you a packaging proposal with digital mockups and clear pricing within 24 hours.

  • Artwork timing

    Packaging lead time starts when you approve the artwork, so signing off during garment sampling starts that clock before sampling ends.

  • Standard and rush

    Standard packaging is ready 7–12 business days after artwork approval, and rush packaging is available for time-sensitive projects.

  • Bulk production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, with order size and season setting where your order falls in that range.

Export Cartons Ready for Warehouse Receiving

We pack your goods in 5-ply export cartons that meet shipping standards. Every carton carries shipping marks and a breakdown by style, colour and size, so your 3PL warehouse has the information it needs to receive it directly.

Freight Packing Plans and Buyer-Supplied Materials

Buyer-supplied materials

For Cut & Sew, your materials arrive before cutting with 5–8% fabric above the calculated consumption and 3% spare trims and labels; we inspect what arrives and report any shortages or defects within 2 business days, before anything is used.

Written packing specification

Before production, we agree the label content, packing method, delivery plan and carton marks with you, then put the specification in writing and pack to it.

Freight identification

Your bulk cartons carry custom PO numbers and destination notes, and each packing list shows style, quantity, size ratio and destination.

Long sea transits

For long sea journeys, we use stronger cartons and properly sealed bags, and we add moisture-control packing where the transit requires it.

Hang Tag Materials, Label Finishes and Fastenings

  • Brand specifications

    With Private Label, you decide the materials, finishes, attachment methods and designs for your hang tags and labels.

  • Fabric-safe fastening

    Our tag attachment methods go through seams, labels or buttonholes instead of piercing the shirt body, and we match the fastening to your fabric and packing.

  • Tag stock weights

    Trims & Accessories carries resort-wear tag stock from 300 to 800 gsm, which is roughly 0.4–1.2 mm thick. Laminated double-layer card goes up to 1,200 gsm.

Printed Shirt Bags and Shipping Mailers

Core-line bags

Our peel-and-seal bags hold folded shirts securely without extra tape. Each bag is ready for your size, SKU or barcode stickers.

Resealable shirt bags

For premium shirts, samples and bundles, zip bags leave room for inserts or small accessories, and you can open and reseal them.

Shirt bag films

Individual bags, including frosted zip bags, are made from CPE, EVA or GRS-recycled PE at 60 to 140 microns. Lightweight clear OPP self-seal bags are 40 to 60 microns.

Shipping mailer films

For e-commerce, we supply mailers in several grades and opacity levels, made from co-extruded LDPE/HDPE film at 60 to 120 microns.

Custom bag printing

We print your logo, colours and artwork on poly bags and mailers, and both can take full-surface coverage.

Printed component minimums

Custom-printed poly bags and mailers each start at 500 pieces per design. Custom-printed tissue starts at 500 sheets per design.

Collar Inserts, Curved Bands and Clips

Structured collars get firm inserts, and soft rayon or silk camp collars get curved card bands. The curved band follows the open collar, so the lapel keeps its natural roll and isn't held upright.

Clips go only on the inner collar stand, with folded tissue between the clip and the fabric so the clip leaves no marks.

Rigid Gift Boxes with Custom Finishes

  • Dimensions from packing

    We size your rigid box to fit the folded shirt along with its tissue, inserts and collar support, not just the bare garment.

  • Board and finish

    We build to your exact dimensions, using greyboard wrapped in premium decorative paper with a soft-touch, gloss or matte finish.

  • Closure and inserts

    For your rigid box, you can choose ribbon pulls, foam inserts and magnetic closures.

  • Printed decoration

    We offer full-colour lithography and debossing, plus foil stamping in copper, silver or gold.

Recycled Materials and Lower-Plastic Packaging Choices

Recycled film content

You can have your bags and mailers made with 30%, 50%, 70% or 100% recycled PE content, and GRS-certified options are available.

Resin appearance trade-off

Post-consumer resin is our default because it gives the strongest recycled-content claim. Pre-consumer post-industrial film gives you a clearer, more even-looking bag.

Industrial composting option

We also offer biodegradable bags for industrial composting. They go through a separate disposal route from the recycled-film packaging choices.

Recyclable kraft formats

Uncoated recycled kraft works for tags, tissue, mailers and folding boxes. When these are printed with water-based inks and no lamination, they stay recyclable as paper.

Rigid box restrictions

A rigid box with laminated wraps, foil, soft-touch coating, magnets or foam inserts can't go into paper recycling as supplied.

Paper-recyclable box construction

When you want a fully recyclable pack, we build it with uncoated wraps, blind debossing in place of foil, card inserts, and paper ribbon or tab closures.

Pantone-Matched Packaging and Patterned Tissue

  • Shared brand references

    We match your tags, woven labels, bags, tissue and rigid boxes to your Pantone palette, choosing materials and finishes that fit your brand.

  • Material-specific colour checks

    We check each material against a physical Pantone swatch under the same light, and we adjust the inks separately for tissue, poly film and coated board.

  • Tropical repeat tissue

    Working from your print file, we print your shirt pattern across the tissue sheet in water-based inks, using up to 4 spot colours. Artwork with many colours is simplified to its key colours.

Packaging Dielines and Artwork Proofs

  1. Separate confirmed requirements

    In your quote request, show which artwork, wording and retail requirements are confirmed and which fabric, printing, trim and fit decisions are still open.

  2. Send vector artwork

    Send your logos as vector files with the fonts outlined, and we'll place the artwork clear of folds, glue flaps and closures.

  3. Check supplied dielines

    We give you an AI or PDF dieline for every box, mailer, bag, tag and insert. Each one marks the 3 mm bleed, a 3 mm internal safe area, folds and glue lines.

  4. Approve the layout

    We fit your artwork to each dieline and send you digital proofs, so print problems are caught before you approve production.

Shirt Sorting, Retail Packing and Barcode Labels

Unit and sorting rules

We pack by the unit or by the dozen. Confirm your sorting before bulk production, whether you want packing by style, size, colour, name list, department or branch.

Standard folded packing

Your rayon and linen shirts ship steam-pressed and folded, each in its own polybag with collar support and clips. We fix the method in the PP packing reference before bulk.

Retail display alternatives

Folded polybags work for e-commerce and warehouses. Direct retail orders can have flat packing or garments-on-hanger. Hanger packing is charged per piece and adds carton volume and freight.

Labels before packing

Send us your label details or approved artwork before packing, and we'll prepare your SKU labels, size stickers, carton labels and barcode stickers.

Amazon FBA preparation

Working from the FNSKU files you supply, we place polybag labels where scanners can read them, seal the bags, add suffocation warnings and label the cartons to Amazon FBA requirements. UPC or EAN labels are available instead.

Checks and certification

Our Quality Control team checks order and packing details against your approvals. Before you order, we confirm which certificates apply, including OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and ISO 9001, and we send current copies when you ask for them.

We welcome SGS or BV inspection.

Delivery Terms, Payment and Split Destinations

  • Carton size ratios

    Mixing 3 to 4 sizes within one style and colour costs nothing extra. A typical ratio is S:M:L:XL at 1:2:2:1. Stores get mixed-size cartons and central warehouses get single-size cartons.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Payment schedule

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Sample courier charge

    Sample delivery by courier costs USD 50, which you pay on top of the sample fee.

  • Group order destinations

    For corporate and event batches, Matching Family & Group Sets offers individual size labels and shipping to multiple destinations.

  • DDP customs handling

    When you use our DDP Shipping service, your cartons carry none of our branding, and the shirts and packaging clear customs together on one invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order hang tags, labels or boxes from you without a shirt order, for example to restock packaging for a reorder?

Yes. We supply packaging on its own, whether you're restocking or your shirts were made elsewhere, and the item minimums are the same as for orders that include garments. We ship it straight to your factory on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

How do you prevent coloured tissue and printed packaging from transferring colour onto the shirts?

We use acid-free, colourfast tissue and fully cured water-based inks. Before bulk, we press damp coloured tissue and printed inserts against white cotton for 24 hours. We print bags on the outside only, and plain white tissue keeps dark printed cards apart from light shirts.

Which packaging choices raise or lower my cost per shirt, such as box type, print coverage, foil, inserts or eco materials?

Rigid boxes cost more than bags or mailers. Your unit cost goes up with full-surface printing, extra colours, foil, debossing, extra pieces in the pack and retail-ready kitting. It comes down with plain or one-colour bags, fewer inserts and larger quantities per design. Recycled and biodegradable materials cost more than standard film.

Can your rigid gift boxes serve as the outer parcel for direct-to-customer shipping, or do they need a protective mailer or carton?

For direct-to-customer delivery, our rigid gift boxes need a protective poly mailer or corrugated shipping carton around them, and we can print your logo on that outer packaging. If you want a box that ships on its own, we make printed E-flute corrugated mailers that take the shipping label directly.

Can you supply a fully plastic-free shirt packaging set, including closures, collar supports and protective inserts?

Yes. Shirts go into glassine or kraft bags closed with paper tape or printed paper stickers, with card collar bands in place of plastic clips, plus paper inserts, tissue and cotton tag string. Boxes and mailers are made from recycled kraft board or paper, with water-based inks and no lamination.

Besides digital proofs, can I get a physical prototype of my box or printed bag to check before bulk production?

Yes. A physical prototype of your rigid box or printed bag comes after digital approval and before bulk production. Check it with your actual folded garment sample instead of approving the fit from dimensions alone.

What are your biodegradable bags made from, and which documented degradation or compostability standard and disposal conditions apply to them?

Our bags are made from a PBAT and PLA blend with corn starch, and we send the film supplier's test report with your order. They break down under industrial composting conditions, not in home compost or landfill. They don't break down in landfill or ordinary plastic recycling. We can add a compost-only disposal note to each bag.
