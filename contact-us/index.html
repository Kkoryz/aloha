Frequently Asked Questions

What are your working hours and time zone, and do you close for any long holidays?

We work Monday to Saturday, 9:00-18:00 China Standard Time (UTC+8). We close for about 15 days around Chinese New Year and for 7 days over the National Day holiday in early October. We post the exact closing dates at least 30 days ahead so you can plan samples and bulk orders around them. Confirm your samples and bulk orders well before these closures so confirmation stays clear of the shutdown, especially at Chinese New Year. Fabric mills, trim suppliers and logistics partners close around the same holidays and restart slowly afterwards. When a late-confirmed order is held up by those shutdowns and restarts, its delay runs beyond our own closing days.

Can I write to you in a language other than English?

Yes. Besides English, you can write to us in Chinese, Spanish, French or German, and we answer in those languages. Every quotation, tech pack and order confirmation we send you is in English, so both of us work from one shared reference.

How can I send large design files that are too big for an email attachment?

For files over 20 MB, send us a download link through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer and keep it active for at least 7 days. We accept artwork as PNG, JPG, JPEG, PSD, PDF, AI, EPS and 300 dpi TIFF. Once every file has downloaded and opened correctly, we confirm by email.

How soon will I get a reply after I send a message?

We answer every enquiry within one business day.
