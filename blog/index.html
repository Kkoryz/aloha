Guides for clothing brands on developing, sampling, making and shipping aloha shirts.

  • Care label requirements for US apparel

    17 November 2026

    For ordinary US apparel that can be cleaned safely, care label requirements call for a supported washing or dry-cleaning method, necessary warnings, and instructions that stay attached and readable throughout the garment’s useful life. Fiber content, country of origin and business identification are separate garment-labeling obligations. FTC care labeling guide

  • Garment quality control: what should a buyer check?

    17 November 2026

    Garment quality control checks clothing against agreed requirements for materials, measurements, construction, performance, labels, and packing. For a buyer, the useful result is evidence that connects the approved specification to inspection findings and a shipment decision. QIMA’s garment inspection procedures describe these checks.
