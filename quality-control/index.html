How We Plan Quality Checks for Aloha Shirts

Quality control on your aloha shirts starts before bulk production. We work through sampling and a review of corrections so we can establish exactly what needs to change before the main run begins. We make the shirts ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Inspection planning, sampling and correction review.

  1. Set inspection points

    Define the inspection points before making the sample.

  2. Make the sample

    Produce the sample for review.

  3. Review the sample

    Review fit, fabric, hand feel, motif scale and color, seam alignment, trims, label placement, shrinkage risk and packaging.

  4. Record corrections

    Write correction notes for anything that needs to change.

Hawaiian Shirt Quality Inspection Stages and Independent Checks

We check your order at every stage of production, from the moment material comes in, through manufacturing, to final inspection. If you want an independent inspection before the goods ship, you can arrange one with SGS, BV or another inspector.

  5. Full final inspection

What We Check in Fabric, Stitching, Prints and Fit

  • Fabric condition

    We check incoming fabric for stains, holes and defects, and we test its colourfastness before it goes into production.

  • Shade grouping

    Before cutting, we check shade variation and sort the fabric by dye lot, so every panel of a single shirt comes from the same shade group.

  • Cutting alignment

    While cutting, we check pattern alignment so the cut panels follow the intended arrangement.

  • Sewing checks

    On the sewing line, we check stitching, seam strength and thread tension, then inspect the garments once they've been trimmed.

  • Production measurements

    During production, we compare key measurements against your confirmed size chart to check the shirt's fit dimensions.

  • Decoration quality

    We inspect print and embroidery quality for peeling, loose threads and stitch density, and we check logo size, colour and placement.

  • Labels and edges

    We check that labels sit in the right position and that edges lie flat and clean, with no flipping or waviness.

Inspection Standards, Records and Corrections

Before bulk production starts, we agree with you that your approved sample and acceptance level will be the inspection reference.

Our in-house QC team checks the bulk against that sample. We check materials on arrival and production in-line, inspect every finished piece at final inspection, and only then draw the pre-shipment AQL sample. Inline print verification has to meet ΔE ≤ 2.0.

We keep a detailed measurement log for each run and send it to you with photos of the finished goods before dispatch. If a piece fails, we reprint or rework it before shipment. We don't ship it and give you a credit later.

On Custom Printing orders, we reprint any substandard printed fabric.

What We Inspect on Every Finished Shirt

Before packing, we inspect every shirt one by one against your approved spec sheet and sample.

We check finished measurements, sizing, symmetry, construction and seam alignment. We also check for skipped stitches, thread trimming, fabric condition and stains, and we make sure the design is reproduced faithfully.

We inspect pressing, finishing and overall appearance too, including balanced collars and cuffs, neat edge stitching, trims and buttons. In our final audit, we check workmanship, measurements, labelling, folding and packaging, and we confirm that the shipment matches your order.

Before dispatch, we send you photos of the finished goods, so you can raise any concerns while the goods are still with us. No batch leaves us unchecked.

Certifications and Safety Testing for Aloha Shirts

Tested fabrics and trims carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100, organic cotton styles carry GOTS, cotton comes through Better Cotton (BCI), and our factory holds ISO 9001 and WRAP.

On kids’ aloha shirts, we test batches of buttons and snaps at 90 N for 10 seconds during sewing and again at final inspection, and we reject any piece that comes off or is damaged.

Nickel release is limited to 0.5 µg/cm²/week under EU REACH, and we check the applicable CPSIA, 16 CFR Part 1501, 16 CFR Part 1610 and EN 14682 requirements. Destination testing is excluded unless your quote lists it.

We arrange it only with your approval and advance payment, at the laboratory’s per-item rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What plus/minus tolerance do you accept on chest, body length, sleeve and collar measurements before a shirt fails inspection?

On bulk woven aloha shirts, chest width, body length and sleeve length have to fall within ±1 cm of your approved size chart. The collar, cuffs and other small points have to fall within ±0.5 cm. We compare your shirts against your approved sample and reject any shirt outside these limits. We then rework or replace it before packing. Before shipment, we also inspect random samples against your approved sample, at AQL 2.5 for major defects and 4.0 for minor defects. A single critical defect rejects the whole lot. Critical defects are a broken-needle fragment, a sharp or loose metal part, or a missing care or content label. Major defects include pattern mismatches of more than 0.5 cm at the collar, pocket or placket, prints out of registration, shade differences between panels of the same shirt, open seams and missing buttons. Minor defects include loose threads shorter than 1 cm, small marks that can be removed and light pressing creases.

Which colourfastness tests and minimum grades do your printed rayon, cotton and polyester aloha fabrics have to pass?

Our printed rayon, cotton and polyester aloha fabrics have to reach at least Grade 4 for washing fastness under ISO 105-C06. They have to reach Grade 3–4 for rubbing under ISO 105-X12, perspiration under ISO 105-E04 and light fastness under ISO 105-B02. For cotton and rayon, the rubbing test is done both dry and wet. We apply these washing, rubbing and perspiration standards to the rayon, cotton and polyester fabrics we print and dye, and to our printed silk. If your printed rayon is dark or strongly coloured, we advise you to ask for the wet rubbing and perspiration results as well as the washing result.
