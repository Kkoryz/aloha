Matching Hawaiian Shirts for Groups and Families

The same Pantone colour looks different on polyester swim fabric, rayon shirting and cotton, because each fibre takes dye and reflects light in its own way.

For your matching family and group aloha shirts, we match every fabric base to one Pantone reference. You then approve the strike-offs or samples side by side before bulk. We make the shirts ourselves in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Meet Our Factory Team and Explore Your Fabric Options

Controlled daylight checks

Before we send you the strike-offs, we check every fabric base under the same standard daylight lamp in our light booth.

Lighting makes a difference

Prints that look matched under shop or office lighting separate visually in daylight, so a fair comparison needs controlled lighting.

Your bulk colour reference

We keep your approved set of fabric strike-offs and use it as the colour reference for the bulk production run.

Direct factory relationship

We are a factory-owned B2B business that handles both production and export, with direct control over quality, cost and delivery.

Our website and our team are our only sales channels, every order is agreed with you directly, and we have no online shop or third-party marketplace store.

Fabric and blend options

You can choose from our fabric range of cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk and wool, plus blends such as 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton.

English-speaking factory team

At every stage you can contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call, and we answer every enquiry within one business day.

What Will Your Samples Cost, and When Will They Be Ready?

A standard sample costs you USD 50 and is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Make Your Shirt Design Work from Toddler Sizes to 5XL

One print across sizes

Your aloha print runs across matching sets from toddler sizes up to 5XL. We scale the artwork to each garment size and sample the whole set together.

Approve the graded proof

Before we print and cut, you approve a graded size proof showing the main motif on the smallest, middle and largest garments, so you can check placement across the range.

Develop your starting idea

Through Product Development we handle OEM and ODM from your design, picture or idea. Pattern design, grading and fit development all happen in-house, which cuts down on design back-and-forth.

Personalised and themed prints

Our Custom Print Design service covers high-resolution sublimation photo shirts with no pixelation, DTF or sublimation personalisation, coordinated his-and-hers prints, full-repeat pet patterns and 1950s–70s retro aloha looks.

Use your own patterns

With Cut & Sew, your DXF patterns go through two rounds of samples, then pattern and machine adjustments, then regrading of the full size range before production.

Embroidered details and branding

Our designers prepare your Embroidery, from intricate shirt decoration to patches and labels, alongside screen printing. Embroidered logos stand up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Production Time, Inspection and Door Delivery for Your Family Sets

Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days from your sample approval and our receipt of your deposit. Order size and season decide where in that range your order falls.

  • Available shipping terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and DDP is available worldwide.

  • Worldwide door delivery

    DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Standards and independent inspection

    Our Quality Control welcomes SGS and BV inspections and covers OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tested fabrics and trims, GOTS organic cotton, BCI cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 quality management and WRAP social compliance.

  • Packing method and costs

    Our Packaging follows the PP reference fixed before bulk. Your shirts are steam-pressed, then folded with collar supports and clips into individual polybags. Hanger packing is optional, is charged per piece and adds to carton volume and freight.

  • Agree the shipping plan

    Through our Shipping service, we agree the bulk quantities, the packing method and a combined shipping plan with you before shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

In what format should I send the list of people, sizes and garment types for each family or group?

Send us one Excel or CSV spreadsheet with a row for each person. Give the family or group name, the person’s name, garment type, size and colourway, and add a count of each size for every style and colour. Include the spreadsheet with your sample approval, and we'll check the quantities against it within 1 business day. Before cutting starts, we check every name row against the size counts for each style and colour and flag any mismatches to you. You can change names and sizes up to 5 days before cutting starts. If a size or colourway is wrong, the set ends up with a missing or incorrect piece, and that is costly to fix after cutting.

How do you keep the print matching across pieces made from different fabrics, like an adult rayon shirt and a baby cotton romper?

We match every fabric base to the same Pantone reference. Before bulk production, you approve a strike-off or sample of each base side by side. The same Pantone colour never looks identical on rayon shirting, cotton and polyester swim fabric, because each fibre takes dye and reflects light differently. Matching to one reference gives you a coordinated set. It does not make the colour look identical across different fibres.

How do you check that the colours and print scale match across every piece in a set before shipping?

You approve colour and print scale with us before bulk production.For matching sets from toddler sizes up to 5XL, we scale the artwork to each garment size and sample the whole set together, so you can judge the pieces as a set.
