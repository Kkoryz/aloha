Wholesale Sarongs and Pareos Made to Order

We make sarongs and pareos to your specifications, and we take white-label orders that you sell under your own brand. You choose the design, pattern, fabric and quantity.

If you're a designer or retailer, we develop pareos for you with traditional Polynesian motifs, contemporary patterns or your own brand identity.

Sarong Styles for Beach, Resort and Everyday Wear

Flat wraps and pareos

These tie in different ways for beach, pool and resort wear, and they double as scarves, dresses, throws or light travel blankets. The wearer's knot is what holds them, so they slip during active use.

Tubular sarongs

We sew these into a tube with an elastic or drawstring top, so they hold their fit without a knot. In exchange, you give up the versatility of a flat wrap.

Pre-tied styles

A fixed knot or sewn twist keeps the shape in place, so the wearer doesn't have to tie it each time.

Wrap skirts and fastenings

We make skirts with tie closures or built-in waistbands. We also make sarongs that fasten with coconut or shell buckles, buttons or hook-and-loop tabs instead of a knot the wearer ties.

Reversible and lined styles

We make two-layer reversible sarongs, including silk ones with a different face on each side. We also make lined versions with lightweight polyester or cotton voile stitched along all four edges.

  • Sample courier charge

  • Bulk production timing

Bring Sarongs to Life with Prints, Batik and Dye Effects

Screen printing

This suits bold, flat-colour designs of up to 3 or 4 colours, and every extra colour stage adds cost.

Digital printing

Photographic and multi-colour artwork works well with digital printing, which gives you unlimited colours with no extra charge per colour. What you give up is batik's handmade, through-dyed character.

Hand-painted batik

We use traditional wax-and-dye techniques to create modern patterns in vibrant colours. Hand-painted pieces and custom batik patterns start at 100 pieces per design.

Batik cost drivers

Each extra colour means another round of waxing, dyeing and wax removal. Fine canting lines, detailed painted areas, deliberate crackle and matching both sides all add hand work, and that adds cost.

Dye and wash effects

Tie-dye gives a handmade look that goes right through the cloth. Acid-wash, stone-wash and dip-and-wash treatments add colour and texture effects.

Matching Family & Group Sets

For Matching Family & Group Sets, different fibres take Pantone colours differently, so you approve each base side by side. We scale the artwork and sample the sets together, from toddler sizes up to 5XL.

Custom Printing service

With Custom Printing, on-roll screen repeats go up to 64 cm, and digital repeats run across the full 150 cm printable width on rayon, cotton, linen or silk. Sublimation needs light-coloured polyester or blends with at least 60% polyester.

Garment Dyeing service

Garment Dyeing reaches Grade 4 or above for washing fastness and Grade 3–4 or above for rubbing and light fastness on cotton and rayon. We fix dark reactive colours and lab-dip test them before bulk approval.

  • Plus-size sarongs

    Our plus-size sarong is 200 × 120 cm, and we confirm its finished dimensions during sampling.

  • Kids’ sarongs

    Our kids’ sarong is 100 × 70 cm, and we confirm its size on a washed sample in the same way.

  • Custom measurements

    You set your own length and width.

  • Hand-washable pieces

    Every sarong we make can be hand-washed, including plus-size, kids’ and custom-sized pieces.

  • Finished sample measurement

    We measure each finished sample after washing, so the dimensions we record are the ones the wearer actually gets.

  • Fabric movement

    Dyeing, finishing and hand washing make lightweight fabrics shrink or relax, so we use the finished measurements as the sizing reference.

  • Recorded size chart

    At sampling, we record your confirmed measurements in the size chart before bulk cutting starts.

  • Quality Control service

    Our Quality Control checks that preshrunk printed rayon and cotton stay within 3% shrinkage in both length and width after three home washes. Wash tests on each fabric and print set the care instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my brand label or logo go on a sarong, and how are sarongs folded and packed for retail?

We sew your woven brand label and care label into one corner, print your logo into the design, or add a hang tag at the corner.

How wide can you make a sarong from one continuous fabric panel before a joining seam is necessary?

We make sarongs from a single panel up to 150 cm wide, which is the full printable width for digital and sublimation prints. Beyond 150 cm, we join two panels with a French or flat-felled seam and line the print up across it.

Will my design show on the reverse side of the sarong, and can you make a double-sided sarong?

How the reverse looks depends on the technique. For a fully double-sided sarong, we sew two printed layers together around the edge. Digital and sublimation prints on lightweight polyester show through lighter on the back, and screen prints on cotton and rayon show through faintly. Batik and tie-dye go right through the cloth, so both sides look nearly the same. Two-layer versions carry either matching designs or a different design on each side.

How long do batik or hand-painted sarongs take compared with printed sarongs?

Batik and hand-painted samples take 15 to 20 days, compared with 10 to 15 days for printed samples, because we apply and dry every wax-and-dye stage by hand. Once you approve the sample and pay the deposit, printed bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Batik and hand-painted bulk takes 45 to 50 days, which is 10 days longer.

Will batik or tie-dye sarongs bleed or vary in colour from piece to piece, and how do you control this?

Handmade batik and tie-dye vary slightly in shade and pattern. We fix the dyes, rinse each batch until the water runs clear and sort the pieces by dye lot against your approved sample. You approve an acceptable range of shade and pattern, not an exact repeat. When each retail delivery comes from a single dye lot, you avoid the stronger differences that show when pieces from different lots are displayed together.

Which of your sarong constructions suit beach cover-ups, and which suit spa wraps that need to stay secure during movement?

For beach cover-ups, flat wraps and pareos in chiffon, voile or rayon are the right choice: they tie several ways, pack small and dry fast. For spa wear that has to stay secure while the wearer moves, we make cotton tubes with elastic top bands, or cotton wraps with built-in waistbands and hook-and-loop or button closures.
