How We Handle Your Information

This page explains what information Tapa Works collects through this website and how we use it.

When you send an enquiry or request a quote, we collect your name, company, email, any phone number and your message. We use them only to reply, prepare your quote and samples, and run your order.

We do not sell your details or share them outside our team, except with couriers and inspection agencies an order needs. Artwork you send stays confidential under our Terms and Conditions. To see, correct or delete your information, email our team.

What this site collects

WhatWhat is recordedWhy
The enquiry formYour name, email and message; your company and phone if you give them; the details of your request if you give them (what it is about, how many, by when, a reference link), and that you agreed to this useTo answer your enquiry
With each enquiryThe page you sent it from, the page that linked you here (referrer) and any campaign tags in the link you followed (UTM)To know which pages and campaigns bring enquiries

How long we keep what you send us: 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted.
