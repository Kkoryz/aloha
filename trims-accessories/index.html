Apparel Trims That Complete Your Shirts

We fit every trim on your aloha shirts ourselves, in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Your garment trims start at the collar, where we put in collar stays, either sewn in place or slide-out, in plastic or metal. When your design has roll-up sleeves, we add button tabs to hold the rolled sleeve, and the tab buttons can match your main front buttons.

Before we go ahead, you approve the tab length and the height where the tab sits.

Buttons, Zippers & Shape-Supporting Trims

For pockets and other fashion details, you can choose metal, nylon coil or concealed zippers. Wherever the construction needs it, we reinforce the area around the zipper.

  • Nylon coil zipper, size #3

    How we use it on your shirts
    The lightest and most flexible option. It bends with soft rayon or cotton pocket fabric, so the pocket lies flat. That's why we recommend it for most lightweight aloha-shirt pockets.

  • Concealed zipper

    How we use it on your shirts
    The same fine coil, hidden behind the seam to give a clean, flat finish.

  • Metal zipper, size #3 or #4

    How we use it on your shirts
    Heavier and stiffer, so it can drag a thin pocket out of shape. We recommend it only when you want a deliberately rugged look with a stiffer pocket. On lightweight fabric we back it with a 2 cm fusible interlining strip, and you confirm the drape on your sample.

  • Buttons

    How we use it on your shirts
    Through our Custom Buttons service, you get resin, urea, plastic, shell, coconut, wood, metal and fabric-covered buttons from 14L to 24L. Custom-dyed or engraved designs start at 1,000 pieces per design in each colour. Stock buttons matched to your colour have no separate minimum.

  • Collar and placket interlining

    How we use it on your shirts
    When we make your shirts through our Cut & Sew service, camp collars and plackets get a lightweight fusible interlining as standard. At sampling you can switch to a soft sewn-in interlining or leave it out. Soft interlining suits drapey rayon, and light fusible suits crisp cotton.

Checks for Trim Strength, Comfort & Consistency

On lightweight aloha fabric, buttons can work loose and snaps can tear through thin plackets, so we build strength into every trim.

  1. Reinforce each trim

    We lock-stitch buttons with a thread shank and back snaps with interlining and a reinforcing washer. Zippers go onto edges stabilised with stay tape, so the seam doesn't pucker.

  2. Fit soft-edged labels

    Neck labels on thin fabric itch against the skin, so we use soft-edged ones.

  3. Wear the sample

    We judge comfort on a sample that someone has actually worn, and we check that the inner trims stay flat and smooth, with no bulk or rubbing.

  4. Pull-test the hold

    We test how well each functional trim holds up to pulling and daily wear, first on samples and again during production.

  5. Match the approved standard

    Throughout your order we check logo details, colour, finish, label position and packing style, so every shirt matches the approved standard.

  6. Inspect and test

    Our quality control covers every stage, up to a full final inspection. You're welcome to bring in SGS or BV inspectors, and you can request paid lab tests.

Nickel-Safe Metal Snaps & Hardware

Our metal hardware meets the EU skin-contact limit for nickel:

  • Metal snaps, buttons and other hardware

    They meet the EU REACH nickel-release limit of 0.5 µg/cm²/week for parts in prolonged skin contact.

  • Nickel-free plating

    You can request nickel-free plating on metal snaps and metal buttons.

  • When to request it

    If you sell into markets with strict skin-contact rules, or to customers with sensitive skin, ask for nickel-free plating when you confirm your trims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which trim choices change production cost, including button material and finish, zipper construction, trim quantity per shirt and attachment reinforcement?

Compared with stock resin or coconut buttons, each of these raises your cost per shirt: genuine shell, custom-dyed colours, engraving, custom-moulded buttons and hidden snaps. Metal and concealed zippers cost more than nylon coil. Every trim you add, such as sleeve tabs, spare buttons, collar stays or concealed snaps, adds to the cost. So does every reinforcement, such as fusible patches, snap washers or bar-tacks. Each of these items is listed in your quote. Custom-moulded buttons and other custom hardware carry a one-time mould or tooling charge for each design. The charge has its own line in your quote. You pay it with the deposit, and you don't pay it again when you reorder. To keep this cost down, use one design across several styles and keep using it when you reorder.

Which recycled or plastic-free button and label materials can you supply for an eco-focused aloha-shirt collection?

For an eco-focused aloha-shirt collection, we supply plastic-free buttons in coconut shell, wood, corozo and genuine shell, and we also supply buttons made from recycled polyester. We make woven or printed labels in recycled polyester or organic cotton too.

How long does trim sourcing take, and how do you make sure buttons, labels and interlinings arrive together with the fabric?

We order your trims at the same time as your fabric, and we check in each trim before cutting. We don't cut anything until the fabric and every trim have arrived.

How do you determine whether the selected buttons, stays, labels and zippers require restrictions on washing, tumble drying or ironing?

When we source each button, collar stay, label and zipper, we check how well it handles washing, tumble drying and ironing. Before bulk finishing, we wash, dry and press your pre-production sample with every trim attached. This brings any limit to light early. For example, you can't tumble dry natural shell buttons, and you need a cool iron near resin buttons.

Can you install concealed snaps in lightweight rayon or silk plackets without visible reinforcement or puckering, and which constructions are unsuitable?

Yes. On lightweight rayon and silk plackets, we fit concealed snaps over a small, lightweight fusible patch pressed at a controlled temperature. We sew with fine needles and put a reinforcing washer behind each snap. You confirm the drape on your sample. Snaps aren't suitable for single-layer plackets without a facing, for sheer chiffon or georgette, or for any spot within 5 mm of a seam or edge. In those cases we recommend sewn buttons instead.

How we get trims and fabric ready for cutting at the same time.

  1. Trims ordered with fabric

    Trim orders are placed on the same day as the fabric order; stock buttons, labels and interlinings usually take 7 to 15 days.

  2. Trims arrive before cutting

    Every trim is scheduled to reach our factory at least 3 days before cutting.

  3. Checked against trim card

    Arriving trims are checked against your approved trim card.

  4. Cutting starts when complete

    Cutting starts only when fabric and trims are all in.
