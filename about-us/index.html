Meet Tapa Works: Your Direct Factory Partner

We make your aloha shirts ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, which has been running since 2011.

You buy straight from the maker at factory-direct prices, whether you need wholesale, private label, OEM or ODM services. We work only with business buyers, through this website and our team.

We don't run an online shop or a store on any third-party marketplace, so you agree every order directly with us.

Shirt Programmes for Brands and Teams

Before we quote, we ask what your shirts are for: resale, staff uniforms, promotion or an event. Then we match the labels, sizes and packaging to that use.

Collections for resale

For fashion brands, resort brands, boutiques, wholesalers, distributors, retailers and e-commerce sellers, we develop collections built around the fabrics, prints, sizes, labels and packaging you choose.

Staff and branded apparel

For hotels, resorts, companies and schools, we make corporate shirts, uniforms and branded apparel, with specifications set around your programme.

Events and groups

We've made tens of thousands of custom shirts worldwide for military units, clubs, corporate teams, events, communities and other groups, as well as for family programmes.

Our Brand Experience and Confidential Case Studies

Right now we produce for more than 350 independent labels around the world. If you'd like to see evidence of comparable work before you order, we'll share released case examples, but only with those labels' permission.

Because of our NDA commitments, we never show you another client's unreleased designs.

Orders Outside Our Production Scope

We spot requests that fall outside our production scope at the enquiry stage, so we don't accept an order that isn't a good fit.

  • Commodity pricing

    If you're buying ultra-cheap commodity shirts, we're not the right maker for you.

  • Sample coverage

    One sample doesn't cover every fabric and fit you use in later production.

  • Exact colour claims

    We don't accept orders that require an exact colour guarantee before you've approved a sample.

  • Heavy embellishment

    We don't accept heavily embellished programmes unless the timeline leaves room for sampling.

  • Structured formalwear

    Structured formalwear falls outside our resort-wear production scope, so we don't take those orders.

Our People and Production Under One Roof

Our team is more than 290 people strong, with over 110 garment sewers on the production floor.

Sampling happens in a separate, dedicated studio with 62 staff: 7 pattern makers, 20 sample sewists, 4 graphic artists, 11 product development coordinators and 20 assistants. During development, a coordinator puts you in touch with the pattern makers and sample sewists.

Our experienced workforce runs modern production equipment and works with buyers every day on manufacturing and quality control. Production and export both sit within one company that owns the factory, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

We handle everything in-house: fabric selection and sourcing, artwork and logo work, design development, sampling, pattern making, cutting, sublimation printing, bulk sewing, finishing, inspection and packing, and we arrange export shipping with our forwarding partners.

We do not subcontract garment production to outside workshops; partner mills weave and dye the fabric, button makers produce custom buttons and forwarders carry the freight, with our team coordinating each of them.

Since printing and sewing share one roof, we plan your print layouts against the cut patterns, so motifs line up across the front placket, pocket and seams.

Development Support, Samples and Order Minimums

Full-package development

Our full-package service gives you help with your tech pack and fit development, and we get your approval on the fit sample before we grade sizes or start bulk production.

Programme finishing options

Full-package programmes can include sustainable production, private label branding and eco-friendly packaging, and we support your logistics through global shipping.

Bulk production

Bulk Production starts at 50 pieces per style per colour, and trial quantities depend on the production. Our 5,800-square-metre floor has 13 production lines, 6 automatic cutting lines and over 580 sewing machines, and it runs 24/7 when required.

Production takes 35–40 days after sample approval and deposit, depending on size and season.

Sampling costs and timing

Sampling takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50 per sample plus USD 50 per design for its pattern or print, covering all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

Your design ownership

Under our confidentiality terms, we sign an NDA that protects your designs and business information, you keep ownership of aloha patterns developed from your artwork, and we're prohibited from selling your exclusive prints to other clients.

Production Certifications and Social Compliance

  • Certified materials

    Our production is GRS certified for recycled polyester and OCS certified for organic content.

  • Transaction Certificates

    When you sell recycled polyester or organic styles with a certified claim, we apply to our certification body, which issues the Transaction Certificate for those goods.

  • Social compliance

    Our production is BSCI approved for social compliance and has passed a SMETA ethical audit.

Check Our Certificates and Their Scope

  • Style-specific coverage

    Before you order, we match every fabric, trim, dye and ink in your style to the certificate that actually covers it.

  • Documents for verification

    On request, we email your compliance team current certificate PDFs, certificate numbers and scope annexes, so they can check validity with the issuing bodies.

  • Company and factory identity

    Our certificates cover our company and its single factory, and its registered name and full address appear on every quotation and proforma invoice.

  • Tested fabrics and trims

    Our OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification covers tested fabrics and trims, and we check that coverage against your style.

  • Organic cotton styles

    For organic cotton styles, we hold GOTS certification and confirm that it applies before you place your order.

  • Additional management standards

    We also hold WRAP for social compliance and ISO 9001 for quality management.

  • Cotton sourcing

    For cotton sourcing, we source Better Cotton (BCI) and give you the supporting documents for your compliance review.

Factory Visits and Independent Audits

An on-site assessment lets you inspect the production floor and materials in person.

You can visit the factory or run your own independent social or quality audit. Either one needs at least 5 business days' notice. For a faster first look, a remote assessment gives you a live video walkthrough or a 3D virtual tour.

For formal supplier evaluations, we share our SMETA audit records.

Payment Terms, Production Timelines and Worldwide Delivery

  • Worldwide markets

    We have shipped to more than 55 countries so far, including markets across North America, Western Europe, Australia, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Japan.

  • Destination-based quotations

    We build your export quotation around your destination and the delivery terms you prefer.

  • Order confirmation payment

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order, before bulk production starts.

  • Balance before shipment

    You pay the remaining 50% before shipment, which completes payment for your confirmed order.

  • Order timing

    Our order process takes your brief or tech pack through sampling (10 to 15 days), approval and deposit, bulk production (35 to 40 days, depending on size and season), a full final inspection and shipment.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Since what year have you been making aloha shirts in your own factory?

We've been making aloha shirts in our own factory since 2011.

Can I visit the factory or get a live video tour before I place an order?

Yes. With at least 5 business days' notice, you can visit our Guangzhou factory or bring in your own on-site social or quality auditor. For a faster first look, we offer a live video walkthrough or a 3D virtual tour. As you move toward a production order, an on-site assessment lets you inspect the floor and materials yourself.

Do you do every step in-house, or do you subcontract any printing or sewing?

We handle your order in-house, from fabric sourcing and development through sampling, cutting, sublimation printing, sewing, inspection and packing, and we arrange export shipping with our forwarding partners. We don't subcontract garment production to outside workshops. The only exception is holiday colour programmes, where printing, embroidery, dyeing or washing may go to a partner, and we tell you about it before the order is confirmed.

Will you sign an NDA to protect my designs before I send them?

We sign an NDA that protects your designs and business information. Aloha patterns developed from your artwork stay your intellectual property, and we never sell prints made exclusively for you to another client.
