Hawaiian Uniform Shirts Made in China

If you're looking for an OEM Hawaiian uniform shirt manufacturer, we make your Hawaiian uniform shirts to your own design and size specifications in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

You pick the cuts, styles and colours, and we apply your artwork with custom printing or embroidery. With sublimated Hawaiian uniform shirts, you choose the colours and design for every area: the front, back, collar and short sleeves. We make the shirts ourselves, all in one factory.

Private Label Uniform Shirts With Your Prints and Logos

Your logo stays readable only when it contrasts with what's directly around it, and dense, multicoloured prints take that contrast away faster than thread colour can bring it back.

Logos against tropical prints

On dense prints, go for solid patches, sublimated 10 x 10 cm plain zones on the chest or back, or contrast-thread chest embroidery. Pantone-matched logos on calmer patterns lose readability at a distance.

Design and print development

Product Development starts your OEM/ODM styles from a design, a picture or an idea, and Custom Print Design gives you 3 mock-ups within 48 hours.

Coordinated team sizing

With Matching Family & Group Sets, we coordinate Pantone-matched shirts, size labels and multiple destinations. Our aloha shirt sizing runs from adult XXS to 5XL, plus youth, children and seniors.

Patterns and fine seams

Our Cut & Sew service keeps your patterns on file (releasing one costs USD 580) and tests 1 cm overlocked or French seams on lightweight rayon to ISO 13936-2.

Printing limits and allowances

Custom Printing gives you up to 12 colours. Screen repeats go up to 64 cm, digital and sublimation repeats up to 150 cm, and extra fabric for large repeats is already in your price.

Your brand, direct ordering

Our factory-owned business has been running since 2011, and you agree orders directly with us. With Private Label, only your brand appears on labels, trims and packaging.

Buttons and repeat production

Custom Buttons tooling is quoted separately, and we keep the moulds for 3 years. Bulk Production remakes approved styles from archived specifications.

Distribution to your destinations

For one order split across staff or branch addresses, our aloha work uniform programmes set out how we ship to each.

Collar and Placket Interlining

We match the interlining to your shirt fabric, so the collar and placket hold the shape they're meant to have without the shirt becoming stiffer than it needs to be.

Construction pointWhat happens on your shirts
Standard fusible interliningYour collar and placket get lightweight fusible interlining, heat-bonded at 130–150°C so it stays fully attached.
Actual-fabric testingBefore bulk cutting, we test-fuse a swatch of your actual printed fabric and match the interlining weight to that shell fabric. We run the test on the material going into your order, not on a substitute fabric.
Interlining that is too heavyOn light rayon or cotton, interlining that's too heavy shows through or stiffens the fabric. Adding weight doesn't make a better collar or placket.
Interlining that is too lightWithout enough support, the collar curls and the placket goes wavy. We pick the weight to suit the fabric instead of using the lightest option no matter how the shirt is built.
Home-wash performanceWith our standard lightweight fusible construction, the collar keeps its shape through at least 30 home washes at 40°C, with no bubbling, peeling or stiffening.

Uniform Shirt Fabrics and Performance Options

We choose fabric for where your staff work and how their shirts get washed, rather than judging the uniform only by how it looks on day one.

Uniform requirementFabric choice and what follows
Front-of-house comfortRayon or cotton suits front-of-house work, where comfort and drape matter. Your choice has to account for how the shirt is laundered as well as how it hangs on the wearer.
Frequent heavy washingPolyester or cotton-poly blends suit frequent heavy washing. Rayon gives you a softer, more classic Hawaiian drape than a cotton-poly blend, but it gives up some wrinkle resistance and toughness in the wash.
Active and outdoor rolesFor active or outdoor staff, we make shirts in moisture-wicking material. That way, performance is built into your fabric choice instead of being assumed from the shirt's cut or print.
Fabric weights and hot weatherThrough Hawaiian Fabrics, you can choose cotton poplin at 110–140 gsm, rayon at 100–130 gsm, polyester microfibre at 100–130 gsm, silk at 60–90 gsm, cotton-polyester at 120–150 gsm and moisture-wicking polyester at 110–140 gsm. For all-day wear in hot weather, rayon and moisture-wicking polyester are the light, breathable, quick-drying options.
Specialist sourcingWith our Fabric Sourcing service, we get moisture-wicking, UV-protective, anti-odour and sustainable fabrics from textile suppliers and certified mills we've already found to be reliable.
Wear and laundering limitsFabric that can't stand up to the job's daily wear and laundering fades, wrinkles or loses its shape before the uniform is due for replacement. Choosing for the work and the wash puts service life ahead of first-day looks.

Reinforced Seams and Finishing for Daily Wear

We reinforce pocket corners, pocket openings and buttons, because that's where staff movement and handling put the most strain.

Both top corners of each patch pocket are bar-tacked or reinforced with small stitched triangles, and we double-turn and topstitch the opening so it doesn't fray. Buttons are lock-stitched with 8 to 12 passes through each hole and pull-tested to 90 N during in-line checks.

Our Quality Control service verifies that uniform fabrics keep residual shrinkage within 3% in length and width after three home washes, and you're welcome to bring in SGS or BV inspectors before shipment.

With Garment Dyeing, custom-dyed cotton and rayon reach Grade 4 or above for washing colourfastness and Grade 3–4 or above for rubbing and light. Our Embroidery logos stand up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can individual shirts carry different employee names or job titles while keeping the same Hawaiian print and company branding?

Yes. Every shirt keeps the shared Hawaiian print and company branding, and our aloha work uniform programmes add an employee name, job title, initials or number in embroidery or heat transfer vinyl from your spreadsheet, packed by department or branch.

Can you add back pleats, underarm gussets or side vents to improve movement during service work?

Yes. We add a 3 cm centre-back box pleat or two side-back pleats, diamond underarm gussets and 5 to 8 cm side vents at the hem, on their own or combined. They give staff more room to reach and bend during service. Our in-house design team builds them into your pattern and grading for every size, so you test the movement on the sample before bulk production.

Can the fabric be given a stain-release or easy-care finish for food-service and hotel staff?

Yes. We apply stain-resistant, soil-release or easy-care finishes to uniform fabric for food-service and hotel staff, and the fabric stays breathable and colourfast. The finish goes on before cutting. These finishes work best on polyester and cotton-poly blends, and you check how they perform on your sample before bulk production.
