Aloha Work Uniforms Made in China

If you're looking for a custom aloha work uniform manufacturer that small and growing labels can order from, our minimum is 50 pieces per style in each colour.

We make every shirt ourselves in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree each first trial run with you case by case and keep its quantity as flexible as production allows. A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days.

Once you've approved it and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

OEM Aloha Work Uniforms: Sample Costs and Worldwide Delivery

Each sample of your OEM aloha work uniform costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering its pattern or print in all colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against the bulk order. Courier delivery of samples is a further USD 50, paid on top of the sample fee. We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

You're working with a business-to-business manufacturer that owns its factory. That factory has been running since 2011, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. You agree every order directly with our team, never through an online shop or marketplace.

With our Product Development service, an OEM or ODM style can start from your design, a picture or an idea, and our in-house team grades each silhouette from XS to 3XL on US, EU and UK charts, with extended sizes available.

We quote first and begin your sample once the price is agreed. Through our Shipping service we have blind-shipped to more than 55 countries, and for business customers multi-destination DDP splits one bulk order across your employee or branch addresses, for example 500 shirts to 500 addresses.

A typical order goes from your brief or tech pack through sampling, approval and deposit, then bulk production and a full final inspection, and finally shipment.

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

With our Quality Control service, we check every order from the moment the fabric arrives through to final inspection, and we keep finished shirts within ±1 cm of your size chart on chest, length and waist.

We also offer UV-protective polyester uniform fabrics, lab-tested for UPF 30 to UPF 50+ in each fabric and colour you pick. Our Hawaiian Fabrics range for uniform shirts includes:

- cotton poplin at 110–140 gsm - rayon at 100–130 gsm - polyester microfibre at 100–130 gsm - silk at 60–90 gsm - cotton-polyester blends at 120–150 gsm - moisture-wicking polyester at 110–140 gsm For all-day wear in hot weather, we recommend 100–130 gsm rayon or moisture-wicking polyester.

With our Bulk Production service, repeat runs scale from a small launch quantity up to replenishment orders of 10,000 units.

We build each run from your approved sample and archived files, and it matches earlier runs most closely when the fabric base, dyeing method and supplier stay the same. Our Customisation service lets you add placement printing, extra pockets or hi-vis tape.

With our Custom Print Design service, our designers turn your logo and ideas into 3 mock-ups within 48 hours. Through our Custom Printing service, we put logos on your aloha shirts by screen printing, DTF or embroidery.

Our Fabric Sourcing team buys moisture-wicking, UV-protective, anti-odour and sustainable fabrics from mills we've vetted. Our Embroidery service handles detailed logos, patches and labels that hold up to repeated washing without fraying.

With our Garment Dyeing service, we dye dark brand colours such as navy, black and deep red with reactive dyes and a colour-fixing after-treatment, so they reach Grade 4 or above for washing colourfastness.

Our Private Label service puts only your brand on the shirts and pulls hang tags, care labels, spare buttons, size stickers and branded packaging together as one set for uniform programmes.

With our Custom Buttons service, any mould for branded buttons shows as its own line on your quote, and we keep it on file for 3 years for reorders.

Through our Custom Labels & Hang Tags service, we draft fibre content, care symbols and country of origin to your destination's rules and send you the artwork to sign off before production.

Each shirt can carry an employee name, job title, initials or a number from your spreadsheet in embroidery or heat transfer vinyl; this adds 3 to 5 days to production, and we pack the shirts sorted by department, branch or name list.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the uniform prints and colours hold up to daily commercial laundering without fading?

Yes. For uniforms that go through commercial laundry every day, we suggest a polyester-blend or reactive-dyed cotton base, and we wash-test your sample fabric and print for 20 cycles at 60°C before you approve bulk. Industrial laundries use strong alkaline detergents, bleach and high-heat tunnel drying, and these fade and shrink fabric much faster than washing at home. If keeping colour and shape matters most, go for a polyester blend, because it stands up to fading and shrinking better. The trade-off is that you lose some of cotton's breathability, moisture absorption and soft hand, and staff notice that on long shifts in warm kitchens or workshops. If comfort in the heat matters more, go for reactive-dyed cotton. It breathes better, but it holds up less well to industrial laundry than a polyester blend.

Do you offer women's cuts and extended sizes so the whole team can wear the same uniform?

Yes. Every uniform style comes in a men's cut, a unisex cut or a women's fitted cut with shaped side seams, a narrower shoulder and a shorter sleeve. All three share the same fabric, print and placement, so the whole team matches, and we sample the women's and men's cuts side by side before bulk.

If we hire new staff next year, can you remake the same print in the same colour for a small top-up order?

Yes. Reorders of approved styles skip sampling and are made in 35 to 40 days after deposit, with the same minimum of 50 pieces per style in each colour. For every reorder, we reprint the fabric from your archived print files and colour profiles and match it to your original approved sample within ΔE ≤ 2.0.

Can you embroider or print our logo and staff names on the uniform shirts?

Yes. Through our Custom Printing service, we put your logo on your aloha shirts by screen printing in up to 12 colours, by DTF transfer with unlimited colours and a soft hand feel, or by embroidery.
