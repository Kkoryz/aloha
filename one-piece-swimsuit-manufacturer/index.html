One Piece Swimsuit Production in China

You set your one-piece swimsuit's neckline, back, cut-outs and leg line separately.

For the neckline, choose scoop, square, plunge, halter, one-shoulder or high-neck. For the back, choose open, cross-back, racerback, zip-back or low-back. Cut-outs go at the side, front or underbust, and leg lines come in classic, high-cut, French-cut or sport.

We make your one-piece swimsuits ourselves, in a single factory of our own in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Custom Swimsuit Styles and Design Details

  • Base silhouettes

    Pick from tank or maillot, plunge, cut-out monokini, asymmetric one-shoulder, long-sleeve or rash-guard, sport racerback, swimdress and skirted styles. Our in-house team works out the pattern, grading and fit from your tech pack, a picture or just an idea.

  • Rear coverage

    Every leg height works with full, moderate, cheeky, Brazilian or thong coverage. We shape the seat to the fit reference you've approved, and you check it on the sample.

  • Pattern approval

    We settle the silhouette and coverage first and add details after that. If you change the base silhouette later, every detail on its pattern lines has to be reworked and the fit check done again.

  • Decorative details

    We add ruching at the sides, front or centre back, self-fabric ties at the shoulders, neck or hips, wrap fronts and contrast panels.

  • Colour-blocked panels

    In a colour-blocked swimsuit, we join contrasting fabrics, match each panel to your Pantone references and check the sample for puckering or pulling.

  • Fabric compatibility

    We only combine panel fabrics when their stretch differs by no more than 15% in each direction and their weights are within 30 gsm of each other.

  • Artwork ownership

    Through our Private Label service, prints developed from your artwork stay your intellectual property under our NDA, and we never sell them to another client.

  • Matching sets

    For Matching Family & Group Sets, polyester, rayon and cotton each render Pantone colours differently, so you approve every base side by side before bulk. We scale the artwork from toddler sizes up to 5XL and sample the sets together.

Wholesale Swimsuit Orders and Delivery

  • Order quantities

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We agree first-trial quantities case by case, and production requirements limit how flexible we can be.

  • Samples and fees

    A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design and covers its pattern or print and all its colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  • Production and payment

    Once you approve the sample and pay a 50% deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, with order size and season setting where it falls. You pay the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Direct factory orders

    Our company owns its factory, which has been running since 2011, and controls production, export, quality, cost and delivery directly for business buyers worldwide. You order through our website and team, never through an online shop or third-party marketplace.

  • Sample delivery

    Sample courier delivery to send your sample costs USD 50, which you pay separately from the sample fee.

  • Separate approvals

    Our process starts with an agreed quote before sampling, then moves through technical development, sourcing and sample revisions. Design approval is separate from your approval of bulk quantity, size split, quality checkpoints, packing and shipping, all of which happens before production.

  • Contact and response

    Contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call at every stage. We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Swimsuit Fabrics, Weights and Performance

Fabric choiceBuild and performance
Standard compositionUnless you specify something else, we use 80/20 nylon spandex, and recycled versions are available. You can specify UV protection, chlorine resistance and shape retention.
Regular lined stylesA 180–200 gsm nylon spandex shell with swim lining makes a light, soft, opaque suit. At around 160 gsm the fabric feels softer but shows through more in pale colours. At 200 gsm and above it feels firmer and gives more coverage and hold.
Layer weightsShell fabrics run from 180 to 220 gsm, linings from 100 to 130 gsm, and shaping power mesh from 150 to 180 gsm.
Sculpting stylesA 200–220 gsm shell is paired with a second shell or a power-mesh lining, for a combined build of about 350 to 440 gsm. This heavier double-layer construction gives up lightness, softness and quick drying in return for hold and smoothing.
Stretch and recoveryOur 80/20 nylon spandex stretches 130–170% lengthwise and 170–220% crosswise. It recovers at least 90% in both directions after repeated stretching, measured to EN 14704-1. If you need a tighter specification, we source fabric to match it and check your sample before bulk.
Frequent pool useWe use chlorine-resistant elastane tested to ISO 105-E03. We aim for Grade 4 colourfastness to chlorinated water and seawater.
Fabric optionsOur Hawaiian Fabrics service gives your collection soft, shape-recovering nylon/spandex, print-focused polyester/spandex and jacquard options.
Specialist sourcingThrough Fabric Sourcing, existing jacquard, lurex and recycled swim fabrics keep the 50-piece minimum per style per colour. Custom jacquard or lurex starts at 1,000 metres per quality, confirmed at sampling. Swim colours come from printing or existing shades, not from custom dyeing.

OEM Swimsuit Construction and Finishing

Edges and seams

Your price point decides whether we finish the edges with fold-over elastic or with picot elastic turned and coverstitched. Side seams are sewn flatlock, and so are the joins between colour-blocked panels.

Cut-outs and legs

Every stress point around a cut-out gets a bar tack, and high-cut leg openings are finished with silicone-backed elastic to keep the construction secure.

Metal hardware

For resort and fashion styles, we use lead-free zinc-alloy rings, sliders and clasps with gold or silver plating. You can have the plating nickel-free.

Heavy-use hardware

For training and heavy pool use, we fit acetal plastic rings and sliders, which don't corrode. You give up the polished gold or silver look, but long exposure to chlorine is the hardest test plated metal faces.

Crotch gusset

The gusset is a double layer of soft swim lining, overlocked into the front and back body seams and coverstitched flat against the skin. It covers the full crotch, and its side edges are enclosed in the leg elastic.

Pattern cutting

With our Cut & Sew service, we work from custom patterns and cut every panel of your swimsuit precisely.

Colour coordination

Our Customisation service matches print and thread colours to your Pantone references, so the finishing is coordinated across the whole swimsuit.

Retail packaging

Our Packaging service adds hygienic liners, hygiene stickers with your logo and wording, and individual self-seal or heat-sealed polybags closed with branded stickers, ready for hanging or shelf display in retail.

Bust Support, Fit and Extended Sizing

Fit decisionWhat we build
Support before strapsDecide on support first, then straps and sizing. Removable cups sit in sewn-in pockets, power-mesh shelves support the bust, and cup-sized styles use underwire channels with the strap placement adjusted to match. Deep-V styles get internal power-mesh support.
Strap compatibilityRemovable cups and shelf linings work with adjustable, convertible or removable straps. Convertible straps have 4 attachment points, so they can be worn straight, crossed or as a halter. Underwire cup-sized styles need fixed-anchor adjustable straps with up to 8 cm of adjustment, so you trade convertible and removable styling for structured lift.
Size rangesWe produce XS to 3XL, plus sizes 1X to 5X, and band-and-cup ranges from a 30 to a 40 band in A to G cups. You can choose any size run within these ranges.
Block adjustmentsWe re-grade blocks, add torso length or move the leg line to match your fit. Plus-size and extended-cup ranges get adjusted strap placement, wider back bands and reinforced underbust elastic.
Measurement chartsYour chart lists bust, waist, hip and torso girth for every size. It keeps body measurements separate from finished-garment measurements, which are taken flat and unstretched. We also grade to your fit model or size standard.
Wet movement checksWe cut neckline elastic to 85–90% of the edge length and put power mesh behind plunge and deep-scoop fronts so they don't gape when wet. On classic and sport styles, the back-leg elastic is set firmer so it doesn't ride up, and we check both in a wet movement sample test.
Padding choicesYou can choose removable round or triangle moulded foam cups, push-up cups, thin soft cups or sewn-in fixed padding. We scale the cups to each size in your run.

Swimsuit Builds for Different Uses and Care

We define each style's end use before we settle its build and care wording, because chlorine, sun and sunscreen are what wear a suit out.

Training and pool

For lap swimming and club training, we build sport-cut and racerback suits in chlorine-resistant four-way-stretch fabric, with compression panels for an ergonomic fit.

Resort and beach

For collections meant for resort and beach wear, we make fashion one-pieces with plunge necklines, cut-outs and shaping layers.

Surf and watersports

For surf and water sports, we use a high-neck or long-sleeve build with a zip back and UV-protective fabric.

Care instructions

Rinse in cool fresh water after every wear, then hand wash cold or machine wash cold on a gentle cycle. Don't bleach, tumble dry or iron, and dry flat in the shade.

Rinse sunscreen off promptly, because it stains the fabric and weakens the elastic.

Label approval

Our Custom Labels & Hang Tags service prepares the fibre content, origin, wash-tested care wording and any required RN or CA numbers, all based on the actual materials and the rules of your destination.

We send you the artwork to approve and check the labels on every private-label order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the suit lined, and will light colours stay opaque when wet?

We offer full or front-only lining in nude, white or black. For white and pale shades, we use full lining matched to the shell. A double layer or a heavier shell stops the suit turning see-through when wet, and we check this on your wet sample before bulk. Front-only lining works for darker shades or busier prints. You give up rear opacity and some hold, and in return the suit is lighter and dries faster.

Will the elastic hold its stretch after repeated chlorine and sunscreen exposure?

When it's rinsed in fresh water after each wear, our chlorine-resistant fold-over or picot elastic keeps its stretch and grip far longer than standard elastic. It's enclosed in fabric at every leg opening and edge channel, which cuts down its direct exposure to pool water, salt water and sunscreen.

How do you stop a one-piece from turning see-through, sagging or losing elastic grip after wear?

To stop sagging and loss of grip, we use 80/20 nylon spandex with at least 90% recovery, power mesh in the shaping zones and chlorine-resistant elastic on the edges. High-cut legs get silicone-backed elastic, and the stress points around cut-outs get bar tacks so the seams don't open.

Can you manufacture fully reversible one-pieces with clean finishing on both wearable sides?

Yes. We develop the pattern in-house with two finished faces that can both be worn, and we enclose every seam and all the elastic between the layers, so no stitching or raw edges show. You can pair two prints, a print with a solid, or two solids.

Can you make bonded or concealed-seam one-pieces instead of the listed elastic-edge and flatlock construction?

Yes. We heat-bond the seam allowances with tape instead of stitching them, which gives you smooth seams and edges with no stitches. Bonded construction suits minimal styles in plain or lightly printed fabric. We also turn seams inside a fully lined body to hide them.

What UPF ratings can you supply for these one-piece fabrics, and under which applicable standards are those ratings supported?

We supply fabrics rated from UPF 30 up to UPF 50+. We only state a rating when the fabric has a laboratory report to AS/NZS 4399 or EN 13758-1 that backs it up, and we send that report with your order.
