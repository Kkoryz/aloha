Women's Hawaiian Shirts Made in China, Ready for Your Brand

Our women's Hawaiian shirts are cut from a light, breathable, wrinkle-resistant comfort-stretch fabric that keeps a crisp, shirt-like drape.

You can pick 95% rayon 5% spandex, 95% cotton 5% spandex or 95% polyester 5% spandex. All three are woven rather than knitted, so you get the stretch without a knitted construction.

If you're looking for an OEM women's Hawaiian shirt manufacturer that makes the shirts itself, we produce yours in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Wholesale Women's Hawaiian Shirts With Stretch That Keeps Its Shape

Our gentle 4-way stretch moves with the body across and along its length, in a fitted women's cut.

The fabric gives about 15–20% in width and 10–15% in length and recovers to within 5% of its original shape, so the fit holds without bagging at the back or elbows.

Our Hawaiian Fabrics service covers blends such as 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton. Through Fabric Sourcing, you can also choose drapey rayon, recycled polyester (RPET), organic cotton or Better Cotton (BCI).

We've run our factory-owned business since 2011, and B2B orders are agreed with our team directly. You can contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call, and we reply within one business day.

Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can order with us.

We agree your first trial quantities case by case, within the limits of what production allows. Our Bulk Production service handles both small market tests and larger repeat orders.

For example, you can order a 5-print capsule of 250 units instead of a single SKU of 1,500 units or more. You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering its pattern or print and all colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order. Our Sampling service adds USD 50 for courier delivery, which you pay. Fit revisions and correction of our errors are included until you approve, and the approved fit, construction and branding are then fixed for bulk production.

Our process begins with a quote and an agreed price before sampling. It then takes your selections through development, separate sample and bulk approvals, production and delivery.

How Are Your Shirts Tested, Washed and Labelled?

Before production, we wash-test your actual bulk fabric and print and write the care label from those results, because stretch yarns and printed colours react to heat and agitation differently from plain cotton.

To keep the stretch and colour, your customers machine wash inside out at 30°C on a gentle cycle without bleach, then line dry in shade or tumble dry on low.

Our Quality Control service tests shrinkage and colour bleed before sampling, with a limit of 3% in length and width after three home washes, and welcomes SGS or BV inspection.

Through Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we prepare fibre content, tested care instructions, origin and any required RN or CA number for you to approve.

Under our terms and conditions, you report any quality problem within 7 days of delivery with photos or videos, and we offer a repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you prevent the comfort-stretch shirts from becoming permanently loose at the bust or elbows after repeated wear?

We keep spandex at 3–5% and use core-spun stretch yarns, so the fabric springs back and doesn't slowly loosen. We also leave enough room at the elbow to avoid constant strain.

Which lightweight fabric options keep pale or white-ground women's shirts opaque without a lining, including when stretched across the bust?

For an unlined shirt with a pale or white ground, we use the heavier end of our ranges. That means 120–130 gsm rayon or polyester, 130–140 gsm cotton poplin, or a tighter-weave comfort-stretch blend. Before bulk production, we check opacity on your sample with the fabric stretched across the bust.

How prone is the comfort-stretch fabric to pilling under arms or bag straps, and what fabric choices or finishes do you offer to reduce it?

Our comfort-stretch fabric is smooth and tightly woven, so it pills much less than knitted stretch fabrics. That holds where underarms and bag straps rub, too. For heavy-wear styles, you can cut surface fuzz further with compact-spun cotton yarns, an anti-pilling enzyme finish or a denser 130 gsm weave.

Can you make matching shorts, skirts or dresses in the same print as the women's shirt so I can sell co-ord sets?

Yes. We make shorts, skirts and dresses in the same print as your women's shirt, so you can sell them as co-ord sets.

How do you stop the buttons gaping at the bust on a fitted women's shirt?

We put one button level with the bust point and space the rest 6 to 7 cm apart. We also grade at least 8 cm of ease over your bust measurement. Before we lock the bulk pattern, we check button placement and bust ease on someone who is moving. Reaching and sitting show strain that a flat sample doesn't.

Which women's design details or fabric choices make the unit price go up compared with a basic camp-collar shirt?

Your unit price goes up with princess seams, bust and back darts, front ties, ruffles, puff sleeves, longer tunic or dress lengths, and stretch grades such as 95% cotton 5% spandex. Compared with a basic camp-collar shirt, each one adds fabric, sewing time or material cost.
