Meet the Team at Tapa Works

We cut and sew every garment in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

The team that develops your styles is the same team that cuts and sews your bulk order, so you talk directly to the people making your shirts, with no middlemen. Your dedicated account manager is your one contact from brief to shipment.

We plan embroidery, printing and finishing alongside fabric and sewing, all under one timeline and one production management and quality control process. You can also speak directly with our production team about sizing, fabric, labels, logo work, packing and timing.

We quote first, make your sample once you've agreed the price, and use your sample approval to set the quality standard before your bulk order.

Approve the Details and Confirm Your Order

Our bulk orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, and we agree first-trial quantities case by case within production limits.

  1. Technical preparation

    We start with a consultation and technical review, then build the tech pack that defines your project.

  2. Style and materials

    You choose the silhouette, sleeve, length, size range, fabric, print method, artwork, labels, buttons and packaging, and we source the materials.

  3. Print confirmation

    We confirm the scale of your artwork and check the print on a real garment before you approve the sample.

  4. Sample approval

    You review the sample and any revisions until the design, fabric, fit and branding are approved, so changes are settled before they reach the whole run.

  5. Separate bulk approval

    You then sign off, separately, the quantity, size split, quality checkpoints, carton packing and shipping terms on the final design. We don't start bulk cutting until both approvals are complete.

  6. Order payment

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Plan Your Production and Delivery Timeline

We plan backwards from your stock date. The deadline that sets how much time is left for production and transit is sample approval, not shipment.

  • Schedule allowance

    Once you've approved the sample and paid the deposit, production and transit leave no spare time in the schedule, so your allowance sits before approval.

  • Launch deadline

    For a summer or resort launch, approve samples and pay the deposit at least 90 days before you need the goods in your warehouse.

  • Peak production season

    If you order during the January–April peak, you need to approve the sample and pay the deposit earlier than that minimum lead time.

  • Bulk production window

    Once we have your sample approval and deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  • Sea transit allowance

    Sea delivery to the US or Europe takes about 25 to 35 days, plus another 1 to 2 weeks in peak shipping months.

  • May store stock

    To have stock in your stores in May, approve samples by mid-January. That gives you the planning allowance for production and delivery.

  • Timeline agreement

    We go through timing with you before you confirm the order and agree your production timeline before manufacturing starts.

  • Project timing factors

    The time your project needs depends on fabric availability, how ready your artwork is, the trims you choose and the shipping method.

Track Production, Quality Checks and Shipment

We look after your custom shirts from design and material sourcing, through garment production, to packing and dispatch, and we keep you informed the whole way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an extra charge for rush production or a rushed sample?

A close launch date can move your order onto an expedited lane, whose ship date we fix with you when we quote, and we rush samples when our schedule allows. The rush surcharge is quoted as a percentage of the order value before you confirm and is paid with the deposit.

How can I pay the deposit and balance (bank transfer or other methods), and which currencies do you invoice in?

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment, and sample and design fees are paid before sampling starts. We invoice in USD and accept T/T bank transfer, and you can also pay sample fees by PayPal.

When do I need to place and ship my order so the aloha shirts reach my stores before the summer resort season?

For a summer or resort launch, confirm your samples and pay the deposit at least 90 days before you need the goods in your warehouse, and earlier if you order in the January–April peak. That covers 35 to 40 days of bulk production plus about 25 to 35 days by sea to the US or Europe, which can stretch by 1 to 2 weeks in peak shipping months, so for May stock in your stores we suggest approving samples by mid-January.
