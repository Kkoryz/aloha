Resort Dress Production in China

Our resort dress range includes printed maxis, vacation midis, sundresses, slip, halter, wrap, backless and tiered styles, light shirt dresses, cover-ups and dress-led sets.

We sort every style by how its fabric behaves, its neckline risk, its print layout and its production route, which keeps your development predictable. We make every dress ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Wholesale Resort Dresses: Stock and Minimum Orders

  • Available stock

    We keep selected printed maxis, sundresses and wraps in stock in tropical and floral prints, in sizes S to XL. Ask us for our current availability list.

  • Order minimum

    Your order starts at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which suits small and growing labels. In-stock orders let you mix sizes.

  • Stock dispatch

    If speed matters most, choose stock. We dispatch within 3 business days once payment is complete.

  • Custom requirements

    Your own prints, colours or fit need custom production. You can't get them by choosing from our current stock list.

  • Trial quantities

    We agree your first trial quantities case by case, and what production allows limits how flexible we can be.

OEM Resort Dress Development for Global Brands

Business buyers

We supply private-label brands, resort and beach boutiques, hotel and cruise-ship shops, and vacation-wear e-commerce labels around the world.

Sales channel requirements

Before development we ask where you sell, because your sales channel decides the size spread, labelling and packing your order needs.

Factory ownership

Our factory-owned business has been running since 2011 and keeps production and export under our direct control. You order through our website and team, not through an online shop or a third-party marketplace.

Design development

Our Product Development service handles OEM and ODM from your designs, pictures or ideas. Patterns, grading and fit are done in-house, and the service also covers quarterly capsule planning, reorder forecasting, fabric reservation and changes based on your sales.

Multi-style launches

When you launch 10–30 styles under one brand programme, our Bulk Production service groups the work by print route, fabric, construction complexity and delivery window.

International shipping

Our Shipping service covers the United States, Hawaii, Canada, Australia, the Caribbean, the UK and Europe. We choose express, air, sea or door-to-door delivery to suit your quantity and timeline.

Production Timelines for Resort Collection Launches

We take your brief through sampling and approval, then into bulk production, inspection and shipment. Under our launch-planning process, sample approval and deposit must be in place at least 90 days before warehouse delivery, and earlier during the January–April peak.

If you want stock in store for May, approve your samples by mid-January.

  1. Brief or tech pack

    The order starts with your brief or tech pack.

  2. Sample

    A sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

  3. Approval and deposit

    Approve the sample and pay the deposit before bulk production.

  4. Bulk production

    Usually 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  5. Final inspection

    A full final inspection takes place before shipment.

  6. Shipment

    Shipment on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. Sea transit to the US or Europe takes about 25 to 35 days and can stretch by 1 to 2 weeks in peak shipping months.

Resort Dress Fabrics and Lining Choices

Core and premium fabricsYou can choose from smooth lightweight wovens, rayon, viscose-feel and chiffon-like fabrics, cotton blends, stretch poplin, knitted blends, satin, lightweight crepe, mesh, lace trims and prints. For a premium line, there are linen, silk blends and embroidery.

Colour Selection, Fastness Tests and Fabric Standards

Colour or standardWhat we confirm
Colour approvalYou pick from more than 45 colours, and we approve each colour on the actual dress fabric before bulk, never from a screen or a different fabric.
Testing and tolerancesOur Quality Control standards test washing to ISO 105-C06, rubbing to ISO 105-X12 and light to ISO 105-B02. Washing fastness reaches Grade 4 or above, high-twist chiffon included, and rubbing and light reach Grade 3–4 or above. Finished chest, length and waist stay within ±1 cm and small points within ±0.5 cm. Residual shrinkage in length and width stays within 3% after three home washes. Tested fabrics and trims carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100, organic cotton styles carry GOTS, cotton comes through Better Cotton (BCI), and our factory holds ISO 9001 and WRAP.
Custom shadesOur Garment Dyeing service works from Pantone Coated or stock colour cards and develops custom shades. The fabric and dye method set the minimum, we confirm feasibility during sampling, and we coordinate colour across shell, lining and trims.

Custom Resort Dress Prints and Placement Alignment

We develop floral, tropical, border, gradient and digital prints, or a solid-colour resort story, and we approve a strike-off on your actual dress fabric.

Our Custom Print Design service turns your references into exclusive, production-ready repeats or placement layouts, and it handles colour separation, garment-scaled artwork, alignment and 3D mockups.

Through our Custom Printing service, we screen-print or digitally print rayon, cotton and linen on the roll before cutting, which gives saturated colour and a natural hand.

Sublimation is limited to light-coloured polyester or blends with at least 60% polyester, and it gives sharper brights and a slightly crisper fall.

All-over repeats use less fabric and cost less.

Engineered placement and border prints make the print the defining feature of your dress, but they use more fabric on long or tiered skirts.

Matched tiers need extra allowance, one-way layouts can't be nested head to tail, wide panels occupy the roll width, and each size needs its own print file.

For placement prints, we use high-precision digital printing and cut by hand so floral motifs line up symmetrically across seams and around waist cut-outs.

Fit, Movement and Finishing Checks Before Shipment

  • Actual production materials

    We check fit in your real production fabric and lining, never a substitute cloth, and correct the fit, length, armholes, collar, drape and print scale.

  • Light and opacity

    Before PP approval, we hold lightweight fabrics up to the light to check opacity, lining colour and length, and whether seam allowances show through.

  • Movement and stability

    Before PP approval, we check the sample in motion to see that the straps stay in place and the hem stays balanced.

  • Packing and edges

    We review the sample after it's packed and inspect the edge finishing before we confirm PP approval for production.

  • Free-moving lining

    We leave 0.5–1 cm of extra lining ease at the hem and add French tacks at the side seams, so the lining doesn't pucker or show below the hem.

Frequently Asked Questions

What bust, waist, hip and finished-length measurements define your adult and girls' dress sizes, and how much wearing ease does each silhouette allow?

Dresses keep their own chart, XS to 4XL, covering bust 82–126 cm, waist 64–108 cm and hip 88–132 cm. Girls' sizes go from 2T with a 53 cm chest up to 14 with an 80 cm chest. Finished lengths are about 90 cm for minis, 110–115 cm for midis and 135–145 cm for maxis. Slip and fitted cuts get about 2–4 cm of bust ease, wrap and shirt dresses 6–10 cm, and tiered and kaftan styles 12 cm or more. We can also work to your own size chart.

Can I take one of your dress silhouettes and change its length, neckline or sleeves, or add pockets or tiers?

Yes. We can change the length, neckline, sleeves, fabric or colour, and add pockets, tiers or ruffles. Our in-house team revises the existing pattern, we quote you before sampling, and your new sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Which design choices on a resort dress raise the price most, for example a maxi length, tiers, a full lining or a placement print?

Fabric use has the biggest effect on price. Maxis, multiple tiers, ruffles and matched placement or border prints cost more than minis or midis with all-over repeats. Full lining, embroidery and built-in cups add materials and sewing time, and we itemise each option you choose.

Which of your fabrics suit close-fitting slip dresses, gathered tiered dresses and structured shirt dresses without compromising their intended shape?

For close-fitting slips we use fluid silk blends, rayon, chiffon and satin. Light cotton voile and lawn hold the volume of gathered tiers without adding bulk. Cotton poplin, stretch poplin, linen and viscose shirting keep the collars, plackets and bodies of shirt dresses crisp.

Can you make bias-cut satin or silk-blend slip dresses, and what fabric or design restrictions apply?

Yes. We cut polyester, viscose and silk-blend satin at 70–120 GSM on the true bias, one layer at a time, then hang each dress for 24 hours before we level the hem. Bias cutting uses about 20–30% more fabric than straight-grain cutting, and it suits all-over or small-repeat prints, not borders or placements. Fabrics under 60 GSM or with more than 5% spandex don't work for bias cutting.

Do you offer both adjustable true-wrap dresses and fixed faux-wrap dresses, and which fastening options are available for each?

Yes. True wraps fit a wide size range, with an adjustable self-tie that passes through a side-seam opening and an internal snap or hook-and-eye at the inner crossover. Faux wraps have a fixed neckline and are sewn closed at the waist. They pull on or use a side zip, and you can add a hidden press stud at the bust overlap.

Can you incorporate sewn-in cups or an internal support layer into backless and low-neckline resort dresses, and what support constructions fall outside your capability?

Yes. We offer fixed or removable moulded cups, hidden power-mesh support, clear stabilising tape at the neckline, bodice lining and light spiral boning, and we pick what suits your design. We don't make fully corseted bodices, underwired cups or structured bra frames, so a dress that needs that level of support has to be worn with a separate strapless bra.

Can you combine a knitted bodice with a woven skirt or add mesh and lace panels, and what material combinations are outside your production capabilities?

Yes. We join knitted bodices to woven skirts with stretch stay tape at the waist. We also add mesh overlays, sleeves, cut-out fills, ruching or illusion panels, as well as lace trims and inserts. We don't combine fabrics with conflicting care needs, such as dry-clean-only silk or wool with washable knits, and we don't work with leather, sequinned panels or bonded seams.
