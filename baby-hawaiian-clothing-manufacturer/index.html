Baby Hawaiian Clothing Made in China

You can order from 50 pieces per style in each colour, which gives small and growing labels a starting quantity for baby Hawaiian clothing.

We make the baby clothing ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. For a first trial, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within our production limits. Your standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days after you approve the sample and we receive your deposit. Order size and season set the exact timing.

OEM Baby Hawaiian Clothing: Quotes and Sample Costs

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering all its colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order. Sample courier delivery costs you another USD 50. Our quality-control programme checks every stage of production and includes OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims.

For Garment Dyeing, our dyes meet OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I requirements and are free from heavy metals, formaldehyde and banned azo compounds. Our metal snaps and buttons meet the EU REACH nickel-release limit of 0.5 µg/cm²/week, and nickel-free plating is standard on baby Hawaiian clothing.

Matching Family & Group Sets run from toddler sizes to 5XL, with artwork scaled to each size. Our Private Label branding includes in-house woven labels and heat-transfer artwork. Your quote request needs product categories, style IDs, units per style and colour, and sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which baby styles can you make in Hawaiian prints, like bodysuits, rompers, bibs or headbands?

We make Hawaiian-print bodysuits, rompers, bibs, headbands, baby aloha shirts, shorts, shirt-and-shorts sets, sundresses, bloomers and bucket hats. Soft cotton or cotton-spandex suits everyday bodysuits, rompers and bibs because it holds its shape and keeps the print clear through repeated washing. Modal or Tencel gives sundresses and matching sets for hot climates a cooler, silkier drape and a smoother feel on sensitive skin. Compared with cotton, you give up lower cost and sturdier shape after many washes to get that softer, cooler feel.

Can you use organic or GOTS-certified cotton and skin-safe, water-based inks for baby pieces?

Yes. We make GOTS-certified organic cotton styles and print baby pieces with soft-hand, water-based inks that are free of PVC and phthalates. The fabrics and trims we test for baby pieces carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

Are your snaps nickel-free, and how do you test that they stay on?

Yes, nickel-free plating is standard on the snaps for our baby Hawaiian clothing. We set the snaps on reinforced backing and pull-test every batch at 90 N for 10 seconds. The snaps have to stay attached without damage. Every US baby order also needs CPSIA tests at a CPSC-accepted lab, and we sew nothing detachable onto newborn to 24-month pieces.

Which baby sizes do you cover, from newborn to 24 months, and can you add extras like fold-over mitten cuffs?

We cover newborn, 0–3, 3–6, 6–12, 12–18 and 18–24 months, and you can add fold-over mitten cuffs at sampling. Our in-house team grades each style from your size chart or from our baby base pattern. At sampling you can also choose fold-over footies, envelope shoulders and snap-crotch openings.

Which safety rules apply to baby pieces sold in the US and the EU?

US baby pieces need the same CPSIA tests and Children's Product Certificate as the rest of our kids' clothing, booked at the laboratory's rate. For the EU, cords and ties follow EN 14682, and on newborn to 24-month pieces we leave out detachable small parts.

How do you set care labels and pack baby pieces?

We wash-test your bulk fabric and print, then write the care label from those results, so the instructions match how each piece behaves in the wash. Every piece is folded and sealed in its own poly bag with your insert card, and our Packaging service adds barcodes or branded bags.
