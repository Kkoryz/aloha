Hawaiian Shirt Print Design for Your Label

You can start with 50 pieces per style in each colour, so our aloha shirts are open to small and growing labels.

We make them ourselves at our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. For a first trial run, we agree the quantity with you case by case, and we are as flexible as production allows.

When you come back for bulk reorders, we keep your print's colour profiles and pattern specifications on file and use those records to match your original run.

From Your Brief to Samples and Delivery

  • Price agreement

    What happens
    Our order process begins with a quote. Once you agree the price, we move to sampling so you can judge the quality, and bulk production follows when you approve the sample.

  • Quote preparation

    What happens
    To get an itemised quote within 24 hours with no obligation, list your confirmed logos, repeats, label artwork, care wording, packaging references and retail requirements separately from any fabric, print, trim and fit decisions that are still open. We then review your design and fabric choice and price the quantity, construction, design complexity, fabric type and availability, lining, print method, logo application, labels, branding, packaging, size ratio and lead time.

  • Sampling

    What happens
    A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

  • Sample timing and courier

    What happens
    With our Sampling service, the lead time starts once your artwork and fabric direction are confirmed. Courier delivery of the sample costs USD 50 on top of the sample fee.

  • Payment and production

    What happens
    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order. Once you've approved the sample and we've received that deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, with the exact timing set by order size and season. You pay the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Quality assurance

    What happens
    Our quality control covers incoming fabric, cutting, sewing, random checks and a full final inspection, and you're welcome to bring in independent pre-shipment inspectors such as SGS or BV. It's backed by OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GOTS for organic cotton styles, BCI for cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

  • Shipment

    What happens
    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Communication

    What happens
    Contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call at any step. We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Need Help Turning Your Ideas into Shirt Prints?

Yes. We can turn your ideas into production-ready shirt prints, or we can support your own designer with templates and artwork guidance.

  • Design support

    Our graphic designers create new prints every day, develop custom patterns and forecast trends, and sample turnaround is fast. We take your sketches, moodboards and references and create exclusive resort prints for your collection.

  • Repeat and placement development

    We separate colours, build repeats, scale the artwork to the garment and refine placement. Before any strike-off, you approve both the flat repeat tile and the 3D placement on the collar, placket and pocket. Changing a screen costs less than reprinting a sample.

  • Your first previews

    Send us your logo and ideas and you get 3 mock-ups within 48 hours. If you supply the artwork, we send a 3D mockup within 48 hours. A flat tile can't do this, but the mockup shows where the key motifs land after cutting and sewing.

  • Tropical and novelty themes

    You can choose florals with hibiscus, plumeria and tropical blooms; monstera, palm fronds and jungle foliage; beach scenes with sunsets, waves, surfboards and coastline; or quirky novelty prints.

  • Faces, logos and solids

    Besides tropical and custom artwork, we offer face prints, logo prints and solid-colour styles. A face-print shirt can show two, three, five or more faces.

  • Personalised photo shirts

    We print photo shirts with high-resolution sublimation, with no pixelation. Personalised photo designs are printed with either DTF or sublimation.

  • Coordinated and themed shirts

    Couple shirts have coordinated his-and-hers prints. Pet shirts turn your pet into a full-repeat aloha pattern. Retro shirts recreate vintage aloha looks from the 1950s to the 70s.

  • Style development support

    Our OEM and ODM product development can start from your design, a picture or just an idea. Pattern design, grading and fit development all happen in-house, which means fewer rounds of back-and-forth on the design.

Which Fabric and Print Method Fit Your Design and Budget?

Your fabric, ground colour and repeat size decide which print method works.

  • Fabric selection and fabric-only orders

    What we supply and how we produce it
    Our Hawaiian Fabrics service offers cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk and wool, including 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton blends, as finished shirts or as fabric by the metre or roll. Library and custom fabric prints start at 300 metres per colourway, in rolls of about 50 to 100 metres. We inspect each roll for shade, defects and width, then pack it on a core in sealed film for export.

  • Very large sublimated repeats

    What we supply and how we produce it
    Sublimation handles repeats across the full 150 cm printable width and up to 150 cm long. We plan matching at the marker stage and cut each panel from its matching point on the printed roll instead of nesting panels freely. That extra fabric gets you unbroken matching across the collar, front placket, pocket and hem.

  • Print production and artwork requirements

    What we supply and how we produce it
    Our Custom Printing service prints rayon, cotton, linen and silk on the roll by screen or digital printing before cutting for all-over patterns. Sublimation is limited to light-coloured polyester and blends with at least 60% polyester. Engineered or placement designs are printed on cut panels before sewing, so the motifs land in the same position on every shirt. We make reverse prints on cotton. For dark grounds, we either dye the fabric before screen printing or print the full ground digitally. Screen repeats go up to 64 cm and digital repeats up to 150 cm wide. Large repeats or pattern matching need 10–15% extra fabric, which is built into your unit price. We don't change the approved sample's method, fabric base or colour reference unless you approve it in writing. Colour stays within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm. Send 300 DPI PNG, JPG, JPEG, PSD, PDF, AI or EPS artwork or a reference sample. Vector EPS or AI with outlined text and expanded strokes is best, or else high-quality JPG or PDF. We check files for clarity, durability and compliance, and you confirm the layout before production.

  • Embroidered details

    What we supply and how we produce it
    Alongside screen printing, our Embroidery service offers intricate custom work, patches and labels made by skilled fashion designers. Our embroidered logos hold up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Prepare Your Artwork for All-Over and Border Prints

If your artwork is built on repeating motifs, it suits an all-over resort-shirt layout printed on the roll.

If it has a natural band or edge, it suits a border along the hem or a design that frames the placket. We set the layout for your artwork based on that difference. Send us the repeat tile and any border element as separate layered files.

Our designers then build a seamless repeat and place the border on each size’s panels without redrawing your artwork. With a border print, you give up the relaxed, continuous look across the whole shirt and get a framed focal design instead.

Design Fees and Artwork Rights: What You Need to Know

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering all its colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk orders. Before development, we need your written confirmation that you own or are licensed to use the designs, logos or pictures you supply.

When you send someone else's reference, we use it only for its mood and draw an original print.

Adapt a Library Print and Make the New Design Yours

Our library prints and base aloha shirt styles are non-exclusive as they stand: another brand can order the same print or shirt unless you change its print, fit or trims.

For a look of your own, we adapt a library print by adding your logo and branding, recolouring or rescaling it, or swapping or reworking its motifs and layout.

An adapted library print gives you a new finished design, but it doesn't give you a completely original starting concept, because the unchanged base stays in our library. If the print is central to your brand identity, we develop a fully custom print from your own artwork instead.

Once you pay the design fee for an adapted or fully custom print, the finished design belongs to your label alone under our NDA, and we never sell it to another client. That protection doesn't cover the unchanged library original.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I receive the editable repeat artwork and colour specifications for my archived print for use by my own team or another manufacturer?

Yes. When you ask for the files for your paid designs, we send them so your own team or another manufacturer can use them. Within 2 business days of your request, you get the editable layered repeat artwork, the Pantone references and the colour profile from your first run.

How many rounds of changes to my custom print artwork are included in the design fee?

Your design fee covers 3 revision rounds per design, including changes to motifs, scale, layout and colourways. We quote each extra round separately and start it only after you approve it. For each round, send us one consolidated, marked-up list that your whole team has agreed, and we confirm it with you before we redraw. Scattered or contradictory comments use up your included rounds without bringing the artwork any closer to approval.

How long does it take you to create a fully original print design from my brief until the artwork is ready for sampling?

After you send us your brief, moodboard, references or sketches, we deliver fully original repeat artwork within 5 to 7 business days. It's ready for production and goes straight to a strike-off or sample.
