Your Muumuu Dress Factory in China

We make muumuu dresses for resort shops, boutiques, hotel and gift stores, online retailers and other business buyers around the world. You can also build a coordinated print collection with us for hula groups, wedding parties, luau and cruise events, or matching families.

We make muumuu dresses ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Wholesale Muumuu Dresses, Order Minimums and Payment Terms

  • Order minimum

    Every order, in-stock dresses included, starts at 50 pieces per style in each colour, and stock orders can mix sizes. That keeps ordering within reach for small and growing labels.

  • Available stock

    We keep muumuu styles in stock for wholesale buyers. Ask us and we send you the current stock list so you can check which designs are available.

  • Faster dispatch

    In-stock dresses are already made and only need packing once you've paid in full. We dispatch them within 3 business days, so restocks stay quick.

  • Stock or custom

    Going with stock means giving up your own print, fabric and fit details in exchange for faster dispatch. Custom production gives you those choices, but sampling and bulk production have to come first.

  • First trial run

    We agree your first trial quantity case by case, and how flexible we can be depends on what production allows.

  • Payment terms

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Custom Muumuu Dress Fabrics, Colours and Prints

Fabric or print decisionWhat we supply and check
Main fabric and colourWe make your dresses in cotton, linen or chiffon, and we also work with wool blends and performance fabrics. You choose from a broad colour range to suit your collection.
Lining requirementChiffon, voile, and white or pastel cotton and rayon muumuus need lining. Weight and shade decide how sheer a fabric is: a light or pale fabric that looks opaque on the roll shows through once it's sewn. We check the actual fabric in daylight, then agree the lining type and shade with you at sampling, along with the main fabric and colour.
Lining coverageYou choose full, bodice-only or skirt lining. Skirt lining stops 5 cm above the hem. Options are cotton voile, polyester habotai or rayon in matching or skin-tone shades. Full lining gives complete coverage but makes the dress heavier and warmer in resort heat.
Colour coordinationWith our Customisation service, we match print, thread and fabric dye colours to your Pantone references.
Print themesOur Custom Print Design service creates florals for your group with hibiscus, plumeria and tropical blooms. It also does foliage with monstera, palm fronds and jungle leaves, beach scenes with sunsets, waves, surfboards and coastlines, and novelty prints.
Printing and consistencyOur Custom Printing service screen-prints or digitally prints rayon, cotton and linen on the roll before cutting. Rayon and cotton get saturated colour and a soft hand, and rayon keeps its fluid drape. Sublimation works only on light-coloured polyester and on blends of at least 60% polyester. It gives the sharpest brights and a smoother, crisper fall. Bulk uses your approved sample's method, fabric base and colour reference, with no changes unless you approve them in writing. Printed colour stays within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm. Repeats line up across the placket, pocket and hem with no visible break.
Weights and stretchOur Hawaiian Fabrics service offers 100% polyester chiffon and high-twist chiffon at 60–90 GSM, rayon challis at 100–130 GSM and cotton voile and lawn at 80–110 GSM. It also offers performance knits such as 95% polyester 5% spandex and 90% polyester 10% spandex at 140–180 GSM. Non-stretch chiffon, challis and voile give a flowing drape. Knits with 2-way or 4-way stretch of about 20–40% give an easy pull-on fit.
Dark coloursFor navy, black and deep red, our Garment Dyeing service uses reactive dyes and a colour-fixing after-treatment. We check washing fastness and wet and dry rubbing fastness on the lab dip before you approve bulk.

Muumuu Dress Sizing, Grading and Quality Standards

Sizing or quality requirementHow we handle it
Your size chartWe grade from your approved base sample to your own US, EU or UK size chart, using our muumuu range of XS to 3XL, plus sizes as on our Hawaiian dresses. Each style gets its own grading rules. Bust goes up 5 cm per size up to XL and 6 cm per size above XL. Length goes up 1 to 1.5 cm per size.
Size-range checksChecking samples in the smallest and largest sizes before bulk shows up grading problems. Those sizes are where the size steps add up the most, especially on loose or tiered cuts.
Quality ControlOur Quality Control service checks fabric, print, fit, length, finish and packing against your brand's project standards. Checks run at incoming materials, cutting and sewing, then random checks and a full final inspection before dispatch. SGS or BV inspection is welcome. Coverage includes OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GOTS for organic cotton styles, BCI for cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance. Finished chest, length and waist measurements stay within ±1 cm of your chart, and collar, cuff and other small points stay within ±0.5 cm. Preshrunk fabrics shrink no more than 3% in length and width after three home washes. Printed and dyed fabrics, high-twist chiffon included, reach Grade 4 or above for washing colourfastness and Grade 3–4 or above for rubbing and light. Rayon care is a cold or 30°C gentle wash, no tumble drying and a cool iron on the reverse side.
After-delivery issuesUnder our terms and conditions, we stay in touch after delivery. If you report a quality problem within 7 days with photos or videos, we review it and come back with a solution within 24 hours. That solution is a repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit.
Label complianceOur Custom Labels & Hang Tags service drafts fibre content, care symbols and country of origin from the actual materials, and adds your RN or CA number where needed. Care instructions come from fabric and print wash tests. Labels follow the rules of the destination market, including US FTC, EU and UK fibre-name rules and Australian care-labelling standards. We send you the artwork to approve before production and check the labels on every private-label order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which dress lengths can you make, and can I set a custom length for each style?

We make mini, knee, midi, maxi and ankle lengths, and each style can have its own custom length. Mini and knee lengths suit casual daywear and resort boutiques. Midi suits brunch and garden events, and maxi and ankle lengths suit luau, wedding and evening looks. Give each length in centimetres from the high shoulder point on your size chart, for example from 85 cm for a mini to 140 cm for an ankle length on size M. We fix the length at sampling.

Which closures, waist treatments and pockets can I choose for my muumuu dresses?

You can pick elastic, smocked or drawstring waists, self-fabric tie belts, a back invisible zipper, front or back button plackets, or a pull-on design with no fastenings. We add side-seam pockets to almost any cut. We also make patch pockets in the main print or in a contrasting fabric.

Can you combine a contrasting yoke or bodice with a different skirt fabric, and what material combinations would you avoid?

Yes. We pair a contrasting yoke or bodice with a different skirt fabric once both fabrics have been preshrunk and wash-tested together for shrinkage and colour bleed. We avoid pairings with very different shrinkage or care needs. Examples we make include a solid cotton bodice with a printed rayon skirt, or a linen yoke over a cotton voile skirt. We avoid silk with cotton, rayon with heavy polyester, and dark prints that bleed next to white panels. Mismatched shrinkage puckers the seams, and bleeding stains the panels next to it after washing.

Which muumuu cuts and details sell best for resort retail, and which should I start my line with?

Our best sellers for resort retail are loose A-line and tiered maxi muumuus in printed rayon. Next come smocked-waist midi dresses and off-shoulder or puff-sleeve shapes with ruffle hems. A first line of 3 to 4 styles mixing maxi and midi cuts, in 2 to 3 prints each, gives you a focused retail test. Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. If you start with this small mix and reorder the styles that sell, you learn what your customers like without tying up money in slow-moving styles and colours.
