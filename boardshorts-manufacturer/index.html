Quick-Dry Boardshort Fabrics Made for Salt Water and Chlorine

Our boardshorts are made from lightweight, quick-drying performance polyester that stands up to heavy use and chlorine.

You can also choose recycled polyester or water-resistant or UV-resistant options. Polyester-spandex gives four-way stretch and resists salt water, chlorine and UV, and nylon-spandex gives more abrasion resistance. We pick fade-resistant fabrics, thread and stitching for pool and sea use.

For swim shorts we use 120GSM microfibre polyester, and for beach shorts we use 110GSM fabric with digital prints.

Custom Boardshorts: Your Prints, Patterns & Styles

We choose the print method to suit your artwork, fabric and order size.

Full sublimation

This is our main method. It handles all-over, partial or logo layouts with no colour limit, and on polyester-spandex your artwork runs edge to edge from waistband to hem.

Print durability

The dye goes into the polyester fibres, so the print moves with the fabric and doesn't crack.

Screen printing

For simple logos and designs with only a few colours, we use screen printing to suit the artwork.

Small-run DTF

DTF works on polyester-spandex and nylon-spandex with no screen setup, so it suits smaller runs and nylon fabrics.

Patterns and styles

We make solid, women's, floral and tropical boardshorts. We use digital printing or sublimation for all-over designs, and we control floral repeats and colour matching.

Exclusive print development

Our Custom Print Design service turns your sketches, moodboards or references into exclusive, production-ready repeats. It covers colour separation, scaling for each garment, placement refinement and 3D mockups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do your UV-resistant boardshort fabrics provide rated UPF protection for the wearer, and which ratings and standards can you supply?

A UV-resistant finish protects the fabric and the print. To protect the wearer, you need rated fabric, and we supply it at UPF 30, UPF 40 or UPF 50+. We only claim a rating after laboratory testing under AS/NZS 4399 or EN 13758-1, and the test report ships with your order. For pool and sea use, we offer chlorine-resistant polyester or nylon-spandex. Its colour fastness to sea water and chlorinated water targets Grade 4, a goal rather than a guaranteed grade.

Do you hold any board shorts in stock as blanks that I can order with just my logo added, and in which colours and sizes?

Yes. We keep blanks in black, navy, grey and white in sizes S to XXL. The stock list we send on request confirms what's available now. Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, and you can mix sizes. Once you've paid, plain-blank orders dispatch within 3 business days. Adding a screen-printed or DTF logo takes 5 to 7 days before dispatch. Stock blanks are for quick orders in basic colours. Specific fabrics, cuts or performance ratings need custom production.

Which of your boardshort configurations are suitable for sustained surfing rather than occasional swimming or beachwear?

For sustained surfing, we specify a four-way-stretch polyester/spandex shell with 8–10% spandex, a flat fixed waistband, a lace-up or hook-and-loop fly, a flat drawcord, a gusseted crotch, welded or triple-stitched seams and a zip back pocket with a key loop, all in a 17 to 20 inch outseam. For swimming, beachwear and group or promotional orders, we use non-stretch polyester or nylon microfibre shells with elastic or half-elastic waists and mesh brief liners instead.
