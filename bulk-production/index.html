Hawaiian Shirt Production for Business Buyers

We make your garments ourselves, in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Our in-house Product Development team works from your design, picture or idea and handles OEM and ODM pattern design, grading and fit development, so there's less back-and-forth on design.

Through Hawaiian Fabrics, you pick from more than 580 fabric bases, including rayon, cotton, polyester, linen, silk, blends and performance knits.

Wholesale Hawaiian Shirts: Minimum Quantities by Style and Colour

  • Order minimum

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can get into production.

  • First trial

    We agree your first trial quantity with you case by case, within production limits. That lets you check your core style's fit, fabric hand and customer response before you go to bulk.

  • Equal support

    Your small batch gets the same quality inspection, fabric choice and account support as enterprise programmes of 5,000+ units.

You can spread each style across 3 to 4 sizes.

For Garment Dyeing, one bath holds about 10 kg, or roughly 50 short-sleeve shirts, with no separate dye-bath minimum, and styles in the same colour share baths so shades match.

For Custom Buttons, custom-dyed or logo-engraved buttons need 1,000 pieces per design in each colour, while colour-matched stock buttons work at the garment minimum.

Planning Multi-Style and Event Orders

Multi-style planning

For resort launches of 10–30 styles, we group your programme by print route, fabric type, construction complexity and delivery window.

Grouped approvals

You finalise artwork and approvals one group at a time, starting with shared fabrics and print routes. Production gets under way while later groups are still in development.

Event orders

For corporate and event orders through Matching Family & Group Sets, we match each base to the same Pantone reference, label individual sizes and offer delivery to multiple destinations.

Keeping Repeat Runs Consistent

We support everything from small launches to replenishment orders of 10,000 units.

Every reorder follows your approved sample and fabric reference. Behind it are pattern records, print files, signed swatches, PP sample notes, trim cards, packing references and what we learned from earlier runs.

We archive your colour profiles, pattern specifications and label and barcode details, so fit, fabric, colour, logo placement and presentation stay the same from run to run. You get the most consistent results when the fabric base, dyeing method and supplier reference stay unchanged.

If a change can't be avoided, it needs a new swatch and a new colour approval.

How Size Breakdowns Work Within Your Reorder Minimum

Mixed sizes

You can mix sizes within the minimum quantity at no extra charge, and we work out the breakdown with you.

Wearer-led allocation

Instead of dividing quantities equally, we base your size split on staff rosters, past sales or pre-order results.

Pre-order support

We help you run pre-orders, which gives you wearer information to work from when you agree the size breakdown for production.

In-House Production Capacity

Once your sample and fabric are approved, we book line time.

Our 5,800 square metres house 13 production lines, 6 automatic cutting lines and more than 580 sewing machines. Cutting, sewing, print checking, inspection and packing all stay under our roof; fabric weaving and dyeing and custom buttons come from partner suppliers we check.

When your timeline demands it, we run 24/7. Output reaches up to 34,500 pieces a month, and we expand production for programmes above 57,500 units. Since 2011, our factory-owning B2B company has handled production, export and direct orders without marketplace stores.

More than 290 people support that work, including over 110 garment sewers and a 62-person sampling studio made up of 7 pattern makers, 20 sample sewists, 4 graphic artists, 11 product development coordinators and 20 assistants.

What Full-Package Production Includes

Full-package production

If you don't have an established fabric source, we source the fabric and trims and do the cutting and sewing in-house. Design and development are also available as part of the package.

Buyer-supplied fabric

If you already have a proven fabric, you send it to us for cut-and-sew instead of having us source it.

Direct communication

You can contact our English-speaking team by phone or video at any point in your order, and we reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Embroidery options

Alongside screen printing, our Embroidery service gives you intricate designs prepared by our fashion designers, along with patches and labels. Embroidered logos stand up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Pricing, Fees and Payment Terms

  • Quantity pricing

    We quote wholesale and custom-design orders in volume tiers. Your tier is set by your realistic seasonal quantity and by colourways grouped on a shared fabric, and higher volumes bring the unit cost down.

  • Sample and design fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. Each design costs USD 50, which covers its pattern or print and all its colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk.

  • Payment terms

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment. Sample and design fees are paid before sampling starts, and you can also pay sample fees by PayPal.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Sampling standard

    For Sampling, courier delivery costs an extra USD 50. Samples use the same fabric and print methods as bulk, so colour holds within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm.

  • Order updates

    Our order process starts with agreeing the price before sampling. Before dispatch, we send you photos of the finished goods, and the process ends with shipment tracking.

From Your Brief to Shipment

  1. Brief and sample

    You send us your brief or tech pack, and we have a standard sample ready in 10 to 15 days.

  2. Approval and production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  3. Inspection and shipment

    We carry out a full final inspection, then ship your finished order on the delivery terms we agreed with you.

Rush Orders and Material Lead Times

  • Rush confirmation

    Before you ask for expedited production, you pick in-stock fabrics and finish your artwork. We check that fabric and trims are available before we confirm your rush order.

  • Expedited timing

    With in-stock fabric and finished artwork, expedited bulk can ship within 20 days for the quantity your quote confirms. Rush sampling runs when our schedule has room for it.

  • Rush surcharge

    We quote the rush surcharge as a percentage of order value before we confirm, and you pay it with your deposit.

  • Fabric lead times

    Through Fabric Sourcing, stock fabrics take 3 to 5 days. Custom fabrics add 15 to 20 days and start at 300 metres per colour for dyeing or 1,000 metres per quality for development.

  • Trim readiness

    Trims & Accessories take 7 to 15 days. We order them with the fabric and check them against your trim card. They arrive at least 3 days before cutting, and cutting doesn't start until every material is in.

What You Approve Before Cutting

Before cutting, we need your written sign-off on every pre-production item, and bulk follows that approved record.

  1. Order confirmation

    Once you approve the sample, we confirm your quantity, specifications, colours, size chart, size ratio, packaging and production schedule.

  2. Technical record

    We put together the PP sample, bill of materials, fabric standard, print placement reference, measurement tolerances and construction notes.

  3. Fit and construction

    You approve the fabric, fit, collar, placket, cuff, size grading and size run, and the sample sets the stitching, seam appearance and stitch spacing.

  4. Branding and trims

    Along with your sample comments, you confirm logo position, final logo and artwork files, label dimensions, label positions and trims.

  5. Packing requirements

    You approve packaging samples, carton marks and requirements, and packing details and instructions. You send these requirements with your label artwork and sample comments.

  6. Quality and presentation

    We fix the QC focus points, pressing method and final presentation against your approved sample, and only then release the order for cutting.

How Your Shirts Are Made and Packed

  • Fabric preparation

    What happens to your shirts
    Before spreading, we relax and steam your printed fabric. Then we match the print across the collar, front placket, pocket and hem.

  • Cut and assembly

    What happens to your shirts
    Our Cut & Sew service cuts with size-specific templates and automated multi-layer cutting. We then assemble the panels, make buttonholes, attach buttons and neck labels, apply logos, trim threads and press your shirts, with inline checks along the way.

  • Print route

    What happens to your shirts
    With Custom Printing, all-over patterns are printed on the roll before cutting, and placement designs go onto cut panels before sewing. Any screen or plate setup charges that apply are quoted separately for you to approve.

  • Quality checks

    What happens to your shirts
    Our Quality Control team inspects every finished piece at final inspection and keeps a measurement log for each run. Any shirt outside ±1 cm at chest width, body length and sleeves, or outside ±0.5 cm at smaller points, is rejected and then reworked or replaced. A pre-shipment AQL sample follows, at 2.5 for major and 4.0 for minor defects. We also check that preshrunk rayon and cotton stay within 3% residual shrinkage after three home washes. Your care instructions are tested: cold or 30°C gentle washing, no tumble drying, and cool ironing on the reverse side for rayon. Tested fabrics and trims carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100, organic cotton styles carry GOTS, cotton comes through Better Cotton (BCI), and our factory holds ISO 9001 and WRAP. You're welcome to bring in independent SGS or BV inspections.

  • Private-label information

    What happens to your shirts
    Through Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we draft the fibre content, wash-tested care information, origin and any RN or CA details your destination's rules require, all for you to approve. We check the labels on every private-label order.

  • Packing

    What happens to your shirts
    Our Packaging service polybags each shirt individually as standard, or follows your own requirements. A PP reference fixes the folding, hangers, tissue, bags, label placement and carton pressure. On top of that come the sorting, stickers, carton marks and packing lists we've agreed with you.

  • Dispatch planning

    What happens to your shirts
    For Shipping, we agree the bulk quantities, packing method and a combined shipping plan with you before anything is dispatched.

Reorder timeline and colour matching for approved styles

When you reorder an approved style, there's no sampling, and the order takes 35 to 40 days after your deposit. We keep the original fabric base and print setup and check the new fabric against your retained sample before cutting.

Then we reprint from archived files and colour profiles so the colour matches that sample within ΔE ≤ 2.0.

New Colourways for Prints You Have Approved

New colour option

When you reorder, you can add a new colourway of an approved print. The same per-style, per-colour minimum applies.

Fabric-matched strike-off

We print the strike-off for your new colourway on the approved shirt's fabric base, so you're approving how it will look in bulk.

Approval time

Approving the strike-off adds 5 to 7 days. You don't need a new full sample, because the fit and construction are already approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

What GSM ranges can you supply in rayon, cotton and polyester for the fabric used in my approved aloha shirt sample?

We supply rayon and polyester at 100–130 gsm, cotton poplin at 110–140 gsm, cotton lawn at 80–110 gsm, and linen and linen blends at 140–180 gsm. Your bulk order uses the same fabric weight as your approved sample. Rayon gives you a soft, fluid drape and bright prints, cotton gives a crisper look, and linen gives more texture and structure.
