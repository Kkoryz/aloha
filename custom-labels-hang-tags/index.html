Garment Labels and Hang Tags, Direct From Our Factory

Aloha shirt orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, a minimum set for small and growing labels.

We put your custom labels and hang tags on shirts we make ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree first trial quantities with you case by case, within the limits production requirements set. A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Once you approve it and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

Label and Hang Tag Sample Fees and Payment

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, which covers its pattern or print and all its colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order. You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Our factory and company have been running since 2011, with production and export under one owner, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. We manufacture for businesses (B2B) and sell only through this website and our own team.

Every order is agreed with you directly, and there is no online shop or third-party marketplace store. Our product development service handles both OEM and ODM work, so your new style starts from your own design, a picture or an idea.

With our Sampling service, sample courier delivery costs USD 50 on top of the sample fee, and we also make mockups of your labels and packaging during sampling. Our ordering process begins with a quotation. Once you agree to the price, we make the sample.

When you contact our team, you hear back within one business day, in English, and we're available by phone or video call at every step. Our Packaging service covers hangtags, barcode stickers, logo cards, branded poly bags and printed cartons.

Alongside screen printing, our Embroidery service offers custom shirt embroidery, including intricate work, plus embroidered patches and labels. Our skilled fashion designers prepare the designs.

In your quote request, keep confirmed logo files, repeat artwork, label artwork, care-label wording, packaging references and retail requirements separate from open decisions about fabric, print method, trims and fit corrections. That shows us what is settled and what still needs development.

Our Private Label service is available on every order, and your packaging proposal confirms the minimum for each technique. It covers woven main and neck labels, printed labels, heat-transfer care and size labels, hang tags, branded metal tags, logo application and silicone branding.

We make woven labels and heat-transfer artwork in-house.

We develop your label artwork from the materials actually used in the garment, and you approve it before production.

If a fabric or trim is substituted, we redraft the artwork and send it back for your sign-off. That way the label describes what is really sewn into the garment, including any change to the content declaration or care symbols.

For our Custom Printing service, send your artwork at 300 DPI as PNG, JPG, JPEG, PSD, PDF, AI or EPS, or send us a reference sample.

Vector EPS or AI files with outlined text and expanded strokes work best for us, and we also accept high-quality JPG or PDF files. We check every file for clarity, durability and compliance, then show you the layout to confirm before production.

Your care instructions are set by the most delicate part of the garment, including its print or trims, not by the base fabric alone.

Washing or ironing that suits the fabric but damages the print causes cracking, fading or colour bleeding, so the instructions protect the whole garment.

We base them on wash tests of your fabric and print, and we word care and content labels to your destination's rules, including FTC care-labelling and textile rules for the US, EU fibre-name rules and Australian care-labelling standards.

You confirm the final wording before production, and our QC checks it on every private-label order. Our quality control follows your order through production to final inspection, and independent pre-shipment inspectors such as SGS or BV are welcome.

Tested fabrics and trims carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100, organic cotton styles carry GOTS, cotton comes through Better Cotton (BCI), and our factory holds ISO 9001 and WRAP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which label types can you do: woven, printed satin or tagless heat-transfer for kids' and baby pieces?

Our Private Label service offers woven labels, printed satin labels and heat-transfer care and size labels, and your packaging proposal confirms the minimum for each technique. Printed satin comes in white, black or cream, 15 to 40 mm wide, printed on one or both sides in up to 4 colours. We cut labels straight, or fold them at the centre or into a loop, for the neck, side seam or hem. You get a proof within 3 to 5 days, and we make the labels alongside your sample so they're ready before bulk cutting starts. For baby clothes, a soft printed satin loop at the side seam keeps the label away from the neck, where it rubs against the baby's skin, and it gives the hang tag's cotton string something to anchor to. The trade-off is that you lose the neck branding shoppers see when the garment is on a hanger or folded.

Can you print barcodes or UPCs on the hang tags, and how do you attach them safely to baby clothes?

Yes. We print UPC or EAN barcodes on your hang tags from the barcode files you send us before packing, and we also print and apply FNSKU or UPC/EAN barcode labels to each polybag. On baby clothes, we attach each hang tag with a soft cotton string loop tied through the label inside the side seam, with no plastic tag pins or metal safety pins. Nothing sharp touches the skin, and a single snip takes the tag off cleanly.

Can you write the care, fibre content and country-of-origin text for the markets I sell in, or do I have to supply it?

We draft the fibre content, care symbols and country of origin for each style from the fabric and trims actually used. We add your RN or CA number where it's needed and send you the artwork to sign off before production.

Which artwork file format and colour references do you need for labels and hang tags?

We work from your artwork file, a Pantone reference, a hex code or an approved colour sample. Before you approve the mockup, we match your colours as closely as the fabric allows. Once you approve the sample, it becomes the standard for bulk production.
