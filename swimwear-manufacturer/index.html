Custom Swimwear Made to Your Specifications

We make swimwear to your specifications, in the fabric, colours and patterns you choose, with bespoke embroidery too. You can choose from contemporary styles, bikinis, one-pieces, surf suits, rash guards, board shorts and neoprene pieces, and our range also covers cup-sized, plus-size and children's collections.

Custom Swimwear Styles, Sizes and Fit Options

Adult and children's sizes

Swimwear keeps its own chart: adult sizes XS to 3XL, plus sizes 1X to 5X, children's sizes 2T to 14 years and baby sizes 6 to 24 months.

Cup-sized options

Our cup-sized one-pieces come in bands 30 to 40 (EU 65 to 90) and cups A to G. Our underwire tops come in cups B through E. All of them are graded from the fit sample you approve.

Fit across your range

Once a mid-range fit sample is approved, we check the smallest and largest sizes separately before bulk.

Proportional grading changes how the cup, leg opening and straps fit, and that causes gaping or digging at the ends of the size range.

Pool and surf performance

For chlorine-heavy training, we use 100% polyester or polyester-spandex with chlorine-resistant elastic. For surf, we make rash guards, board shorts and neoprene pieces with flatlock or bonded seams.

Bikini cuts

Our bikini options cover low-, mid- and high-rise bottoms, fixed, folded or tie-side waistbands, and every level of coverage from full to thong. The sampling reference you agree with us keeps bulk coverage consistent.

Direct factory relationship

Our company has been operating since 2011, owns its factory and controls production and export directly. Every B2B order is agreed through our team, and we have no online shop or store on any third-party marketplace.

Wholesale Swimwear Orders, Samples and Lead Times

  • Minimum order

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We agree first-trial quantities case by case within production constraints.

  • Sample timing and cost

    A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. The sample fee is not refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  • Bulk timing and payment

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on size and season. The clock starts once the sample is approved and we've received your 50% confirmation deposit. You pay the remaining 50% before shipment, after a full final inspection.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Sampling service

    Our Sampling service gives you one sample per style, in size S or the fit size you choose, so you can approve fit, stretch and construction and ask for corrections. You pay for courier delivery, which costs an extra USD 50.

  • Separate approval stages

    Our process starts with a quote you agree to before sampling. Sample approval is kept separate from bulk approval, and production begins once you've approved the design, materials, fit, branding and order details.

  • Your itemised quote

    We review your design, fabric, quantity, construction, lining, printing, branding, packaging, size ratio and lead time, then send you a no-obligation itemised quote within 24 hours.

  • Contact our team

    Our English-speaking team replies to every enquiry within one business day, and you can reach us by phone or video call throughout your order.

Private Label Swimwear Design and Production Support

Fully custom development

We design and make your pieces entirely to your brief. Our in-house pattern makers and technical designers develop bikini, one-piece and swim-trunk silhouettes from sketch through sampling.

Fit decisions first

Send reference garments or fit preferences with your brief so we can fix the silhouette before cutting. A fit change after sampling needs new patterns and another sample round.

Full-package production

Our full-package service takes you from concept and design through fabric sourcing to finished production. Hygienic liners for bottoms are included, and we brand those liners on orders over 3,000 units.

Exclusive design ownership

Through our Private Label service, you get exclusive design rights to each design, including all its colourways, once you pay the USD 50 fee per design. That fee is non-refundable and non-creditable.

Our NDA protects prints based on your artwork from being resold.

Technical development support

If you're looking for an OEM swimwear manufacturer to build to your existing specifications and tech pack, our Product Development service does exactly that.

It also develops the tech pack for you, coordinates fabric mills, runs fit-sample rounds and prepares production markers for each size.

Swimwear Fabric Choices and Recycled Materials

Fabric choiceWhat it means for your swimwear
Nylon or polyester blendsNylon-spandex feels softer and drapes more smoothly. Polyester-spandex gives up some of that silky feel for better chlorine resistance and works better with sublimation. That's why you confirm your choice on a sewn sample under stretch, not on a flat swatch.
Stretch and weightOur bases include 80% nylon / 20% spandex and polyester-spandex at 180 GSM or more. We check stretch at 130–170% lengthwise and 170–220% crosswise. You approve recovery and print sharpness before we use that construction for bulk.
Texture and supportYou can choose ribbed knits, jacquards, lurex metallic weaves, UPF50+ fabrics and double-knit compression linings. Each one is selected for performance, stretch, UV resistance and service life.
Recycled materialsWe offer recycled nylon, ocean-regenerated nylon and regenerated elastane, and we cut with zero-waste pattern layouts.
Neoprene thicknessWe supply neoprene in 1.5 mm, 2 mm, 3 mm and 5 mm. 1.5–2 mm suits surf tops, rash vests and warm-water paddling. 3 mm suits spring suits for around 18–22°C. 5 mm with a plush lining suits water below 15°C.
Neoprene facesYou can specify nylon jersey on both faces, a smooth-skin outer face or a thermal plush lining.
Fabric sourcing minimumsThrough our Fabric Sourcing service, existing jacquard, lurex and recycled fabrics stay at the garment minimum. Custom-developed jacquard or lurex starts at 1,000 metres per quality, and we confirm the exact minimum at sampling.

Swimwear Prints and Colours That Suit Your Collection

Sublimation requirements

Our ultra-HD sublimation uses paper transfer film and starts at 50 pieces per print. It needs light-coloured polyester or a blend with at least 60% polyester. It can't print white or lighten a dark ground.

Prints under stretch

The dye goes into the polyester fibres, so the print moves with the fabric and doesn't crack. We stretch strike-offs over bust, hip and seat forms.

Wherever white shows through, we deepen the ground colour or rescale the artwork before bulk.

Existing solid colours

Custom dyeing isn't practical for swim fabrics, so you choose printing or stock shades.

The stock shades include navy, black, white, ocean blue, emerald green, pink, coral, lavender, earth tones, neutrals and summer brights, and they're coordinated across fabric, lining and trims.

Exclusive print development

Our Custom Print Design service turns your sketches, moodboards or references into exclusive resort repeats. It covers colour separation, scaling for each garment, refined placement and 3D mockups.

Matching across fabrics

For Matching Family & Group Sets, different fibres render Pantone colours differently. You approve each base side by side against one reference, and we scale the artwork and sample the sets together, from toddler sizes up to 5XL.

Production print adjustments

Our Custom Printing service adjusts repeat, scale and placement for each style and calibrates colours on stretch fabric to reduce deviation. It also gives you pre-production strike-offs to approve.

Precision Swimwear Construction, Lining and Finishing

  • Stitching and finishing

    We build swimwear with four-needle six-thread flatlock stitching, seamless bonding and lining. Then we mark details, attach custom hardware and clean up edges under magnifying lenses.

  • Lining choices

    You choose full, front-only or self-fabric lining at sampling. Front-only lining makes the suit lighter and cheaper, but the back has less opacity and body. Power mesh at the front or tummy adds support.

  • Bust support

    Removable foam pads suit triangle and bandeau pockets, but they shift or fold and give little lift. For larger cups, we specify power mesh and sewn-in moulded cups, which keep their support under heavier loads.

  • Swim-grade hardware

    Rings, sliders, clasps and adjusters come in 316 stainless steel, zinc alloy with anti-corrosion plating, or high-strength nylon. Every opening is finished with chlorine-resistant rubber elastic, which keeps its stretch through repeated pool and seawater use.

  • Embroidery placement

    We keep embroidery under 8 cm across and put it on lower-stretch hips, waistbands or strap ends. We use a stitch density that works with stretch and a soft mesh or wash-away backing. We also cover the reverse with lining so it doesn't touch the skin.

  • Buyer-supplied materials

    For Cut & Sew, your materials arrive before cutting, with 5–8% extra fabric and 3% spare trims and labels. We inspect everything when it arrives and report any shortages or defects within 2 business days, before we use it.

  • Retail-ready packaging

    Our Packaging service adds branded hygiene stickers to bottoms. It packs each piece in a self-seal or heat-sealed polybag with a branded closure sticker, ready for retail display.

  • Nickel-release requirements

    Our metal trims and accessories meet the EU REACH nickel-release limit of 0.5 µg/cm²/week for prolonged skin contact. Nickel-free plating is available on request.

Swimwear Performance Tests and Final Quality Checks

Dye lots and finishing vary between batches, so we repeat chlorine-resistance, colourfastness and elasticity tests on your bulk fabric lot and inspect every finished piece for stretch, print accuracy and size tolerance.

Light shades get nude or matching linings and a wet-opacity check at sampling. Before you order, our Quality Control service confirms the certification that applies, including OEKO-TEX Standard 100, ISO 9001 and GRS documentation for certified recycled goods.

We welcome independent inspectors and arrange paid tests at accredited laboratories, such as US flammability testing to 16 CFR Part 1610.

Our Custom Labels & Hang Tags service prepares compliant fibre, origin and care wording, adds your RN or CA number where required and sends you the artwork to approve.

Under our terms and conditions, you report quality problems within 7 days of delivery, and we offer a repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which choices change the price of a swimsuit most, such as recycled or speciality fabric, underwires, lining, hardware or bonded seams?

The biggest factors are how complex the style is, standard or speciality fabric, sublimation, underwires and moulded cups, hardware and packaging. Full lining costs more than front-only lining, and bonded seams cost more than stitched ones. Your quote lists each of these choices. If you remove bonded seams, moulded cups or full lining after sampling, the fit and construction change, so the style needs resampling and another wear test.

Does a UPF rating such as UPF 50+ cover only the fabric or the finished swimwear, and what standard and supporting evidence address protection when wet or stretched?

A UPF rating such as UPF 50+ applies to the fabric, not the finished swimsuit. We only state it when a laboratory report to AS/NZS 4399 or EN 13758-1 backs it up, and we send you that report with your order.

What care instructions do you put on swimwear care labels so my customers' suits last longer?

Your custom care labels can show fibre content along with instructions to rinse in cool fresh water after the pool or sea, hand wash, avoid the tumble dryer, dry flat in the shade and wash off sunscreen promptly so it doesn't stain.

Can you manufacture with cups, underwires or swim elastics that I supply, and what compatibility limits apply?

Yes. We sew with the components you supply as long as the underwires fit our 8–10 mm channelling, the cups match your pattern's cup size and shape, and the elastics are chlorine-resistant swim elastic 6 to 12 mm wide.

Which children's swimwear safety requirements can you meet for my destination market, particularly for cords, drawstrings and detachable small parts?

For the US, we arrange CPSIA testing for lead, phthalates and flammability at a CPSC-accepted laboratory so you can issue the Children's Product Certificate. For the EU, we make cords and drawstrings to EN 14682. Children's sizes have no hood or neck drawstrings and no toggles. Waist cords are bar-tacked at centre back, and their free ends are no longer than 7.5 cm. Baby sizes have no detachable decorative small parts.

Beyond checking measurements and seam tension, how do you assess whether cups, straps and leg openings stay secure during swimming?

Before bulk, a fit model wears each sample for 30 minutes of swimming and poolside movement. During that time we check that the cups stay secure, the straps don't slip and the leg openings don't ride up or gape. Fabric and elastic must recover at least 90% of their stretch after 50 stretch cycles and a soak in chlorinated and salt water. If they don't, we adjust the patterns or elastic tension before you approve.
