Hawaiian Shirt Product Development: From Idea to Collection Plan

Our starter kit helps you plan your first aloha shirt collection before you place an order.

We make the shirts ourselves, in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. In the kit you'll find fabric swatches with print notes, more than 65 base styles, advice on sampling, support for custom prints, and guidance on FOB and DDP shipping.

It also gives you access to our catalogue and a direct line to our team.

A Planning Kit for Your First Collection

Every new label gets our 12-page launch guide, which our in-house design team puts together. Work through it from start to finish, in the order it's laid out. That way your development decisions are settled before you move on to planning your bulk quantities.

Follow the launch guide in order.

  1. Choose fabric

    Settle the fabric direction for your first aloha shirt collection.

  2. Plan sampling

    Work through sampling before planning bulk quantities.

  3. Plan bulk orders

    Bulk orders start at 50 pieces per style per colour.

Is This Service Right for Your Brand?

Yes. This service is built for first collections, print-led brands, capsule resort drops and low-MOQ test runs.

First-time founders

If you have little factory experience, use the kit to plan your first resort line before sampling starts.

Undecided collection direction

If you're still comparing categories or custom print directions, use the materials before you narrow down your first capsule range.

Experienced brands

If you already have experience, you don't need the kit and can go straight to selecting styles and requesting a quote.

Design Support for OEM and ODM Projects

We support both OEM and ODM projects, whether you start from your own design, a picture or an idea.

For OEM work, we build to your specifications and tech pack. If you need development support, we prepare the tech pack, coordinate with the mills, run fit-sample rounds and prepare production markers for each size.

Our mockup and written-approval process means no fabric is cut until you've signed off on the design.

Digital review comes before cutting.

  1. Receive digital mockup

    We usually send a digital mockup within 48 hours.

  2. Approve every detail

    Sign off on every detail in writing.

  3. Begin fabric cutting

    We cut no fabric until every detail has been approved.

From Your Brief to a Technical Specification

You can go with fully custom development, pick one of our ready designs that's already graded to international sizes, or combine the two.

For a fast first launch, start from a ready design and add your own prints or details. You don't need a tech pack to get started or to receive a quote, though a complete pack does help.

In our consultation, we turn whatever you start with into a specification that's ready for sampling. Send change notes, artwork, reference photos, measurements, your target fit, fabric ideas, quantities and packing requests wherever you have them. Our designers will also advise you on logo placement and all-over prints.

A tech pack is not required to begin.

  1. Share your starting material

    Send whatever you have, from a tech pack or reference shirt to sketches, pictures or simply an idea.

  2. Review the brief

    We review your material before development begins and help fill in missing details before sampling.

  3. Settle design and sizing

    In a design consultation, we help settle styles, sizes, colours and patterns, and work out the pattern and sizing with you.

  4. Prepare the technical specification

    Our designers create technical sketches and a detailed specification covering every seam, the lining and all trims.

Shirt References, Fabrics and Finishing Details

  • Reference shirts

    What we provide
    You can start from our base block, your measurements or just a reference shirt. We examine its fit, collar, cuffs, pocket, stitching, fabric and finish, reverse-engineer the pattern, find the closest fabric available and prepare a sample plan. Then we make a counter sample for you to approve before we go any further.

  • Unsettled details

    What we provide
    If your construction, fit, fabric, artwork, trims, quantity, logo placement, labels or packaging aren't settled yet, we help you settle them before sampling.

  • Fabric sourcing

    What we provide
    Through our Fabric Sourcing service, stock fabrics reach our factory within 3 to 5 days. Custom-dyed colours start at 300 metres per colour, and custom yarn-dyed stripes, checks and jacquards start at 1,000 metres per quality. We confirm the exact minimum at sampling, based on fabric type and dye method. Custom fabric adds 15 to 20 days before bulk, and that time includes one yarn-colour lab-dip round for yarn-dyed development.

  • Colour and finish

    What we provide
    Through Garment Dyeing, we match your Pantone reference or physical swatch, get your lab-dip approval before bulk, and offer finishes and treatments.

  • Embroidered details

    What we provide
    Our Embroidery service covers intricate decoration, patches and labels prepared by our designers. The embroidered logos hold up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

  • Your branding

    What we provide
    Our Private Label service keeps your garments under your brand alone. It covers approved labels, branded trims and accessories, tags, and coordinated retail packaging, all packed to your specifications.

  • Branded buttons

    What we provide
    Through Custom Buttons, we develop logo buttons and hardware. We quote any tooling they need separately before you approve it, and we keep the moulds for 3 years so identical reorders reuse them.

Exclusive Prints Ready for Production

  1. Coordinate print development

    One team takes care of your fabrics, styles and prints, from your brand references through sampling and bulk. We engineer the repeats around the pattern pieces so plackets, pockets and every size line up.

  2. Choose fabric effects

    Hawaiian Fabrics gives you more than 580 bases. Reverse-print shirts are printed on the inward-facing side. For these we recommend 100% cotton poplin at 110–130 gsm, and you approve a strike-off of both faces plus a sample of the outer effect before bulk.

  3. Develop exclusive artwork

    With Custom Print Design, you get mock-ups within 48 hours, repeats within 5 to 7 business days and 3 revision rounds. Any extra rounds you approve cost more. New or adapted designs you pay for become exclusive to you, while unchanged originals from our library stay non-exclusive.

  4. Protect your designs

    Under our Terms and Conditions, we sign an NDA that protects your designs and business information. Patterns developed from your artwork stay your intellectual property, and we never resell exclusive prints.

  5. Coordinate matching sets

    For Matching Family & Group Sets, different fibres render the same Pantone differently. So we match the bases to one reference for you to approve side by side before bulk, scale the artwork from toddler sizes to 5XL, and sample the sets together.

Shirt Patterns, Fit and Size Grading

  • Digital pattern master

    Development specification
    Our pattern maker drafts and grades your pattern in CAD to your size specification, and checks the custom grading rules across the full run. We keep the graded master unchanged for your repeat orders.

  • Size coverage

    Development specification
    Our standard range runs from XS to 3XL on US, EU and UK charts, and extended sizes are available.

  • Base cuts

    Development specification
    Our aloha shirt range comes in four cuts: unisex/men’s, men’s slim, women’s and women’s vintage. Each one is graded over 47 measurement points, and the vintage cut has a mandarin collar and cap sleeves.

  • Construction choices

    Development specification
    Through Customisation, you choose camp, spread or notch collars; straight or curved hems; vents or closed sides; no pocket, or one or two chest pockets, plain or matched; and coconut, shell, wood or resin buttons. We record every choice in your tech pack and sample it for your approval before bulk.

  • Cutting and sewing limits

    Development specification
    In Cut & Sew, directional or matched prints use 10–15% more fabric. That extra fabric is included in your unit price, and the quote reflects both the extra consumption and the slower single-ply matching. Repeats reach 64 cm for screen printing or the full 150 cm width for digital. If you supply DXF patterns, they go through two sample rounds and adjustments before full regrading. Lightweight rayon gets 1 cm overlocked or French seams, tested to ISO 13936-2 with no more than 6 mm of opening. Fine fabrics can also take flat-felled sides or 0.6 cm French seams. Contrast fabrics stay within about 40 GSM and a shrinkage difference of no more than 2%. They go through wash and colour-bleed tests and keep shrinkage to no more than 3% in either direction after three home washes. Where a print crosses both bases, you approve the shared Pantone side by side.

Order Minimums, Lead Times and Delivery Terms

  • Order minimum

    Bulk orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can start at an accessible quantity.

  • Trial quantities

    We agree your first trial run case by case, and how far the quantity can flex is limited by what production allows.

  • Bulk timing

    Production takes 35 to 40 days from sample approval and receipt of your deposit. Where it falls in that range depends on your order size and the season.

  • Payment stages

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Ongoing collections

    We support quarterly capsules and forecast reorders, reserve base cloth for your bestsellers and refine designs based on your sales feedback.

In-House Development and Factory Oversight

One development team

If you're a startup, we handle development, sampling, sourcing, branding and manufacturing together, so the work isn't split between suppliers.

In-house design

Our designers handle pattern design, grading and fit development together, which cuts the rounds of design back-and-forth you have to manage.

Factory resources

Our factory team has been running since 2011. It has more than 290 people, including over 110 production sewers and 62 sampling staff: 7 pattern makers, 20 sewists, 4 artists, 11 development coordinators and 20 assistants.

Direct business orders

We serve business buyers worldwide only through this website and our team. We don't run an online shop or a third-party marketplace store. Production and export sit within one company that owns the factory.

Quality oversight

Our Quality Control covers everything from incoming fabric to final inspection, and we welcome SGS/BV checks.

It also includes OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GOTS for organic styles, BCI cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 quality management and WRAP social compliance.

From Kit Review to Your First Sample

  1. Share launch priorities

    Before any development decisions are settled, tell us your category, launch market, target price tier, intended product mix and artwork direction.

  2. Review kit materials

    Go through the swatches, catalogue, base styles and other planning materials with our production team.

  3. Narrow your range

    Pick a tighter first collection and decide which styles are worth developing into paid samples.

  4. Compare cost alternatives

    Before you choose or sample, we requote with rayon or cotton instead of silk or linen, digital printing instead of multi-colour screen printing, and resin buttons instead of coconut or shell.

  5. Agree the quote

    Under Our Process, we quote first and start making your sample only once you've agreed the price.

  6. Start mapped sampling

    When sampling begins, your artwork, trims, labels, packaging and shipping requirements are already mapped out.

Sample Costs, Timing and Sign-Off

  • Sample timing

    A standard sample takes us 10 to 15 days from your brief or tech pack.

  • Sample fee

    Each sample costs USD 50. This fee isn't refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  • Design fee

    Each design costs USD 50 for its pattern or print, covering all colourways. This fee isn't refunded or credited against bulk either.

  • Sampling options

    Sampling offers seven types: fabric test, first, fit, photo, revised, pre-production (PP) and repeat. There are quick-response lines for fit, photo and PP samples, and courier delivery, which you pay for, costs USD 50 extra.

  • Quote scope

    Your quote lists any applicable development charges separately. It excludes unlisted materials, trims, swatches, extra lab dips, decoration, setup, further revisions, extra samples, freight and testing, and any additional costs need your approval.

  • Bulk sign-off

    Bulk Production starts only once your specifications, quantities, sizing, materials, artwork, branding, packaging and schedule are confirmed. Your approved sample and pre-production record then govern how the order is made.

  • Packaging proposal

    Through Packaging, we send you a proposal within 24 hours, with digital mockups and clear pricing.

  • Label approval

    With Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we take the content, origin and tested care instructions from the actual materials, add the required RN/CA numbers and follow your destination's rules. Your artwork approval and QC are both required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the starter kit sent to me as a physical pack or digitally, and how long after I request it will I receive it?

You get both. We send the fabric swatches with print notes by DHL courier. The pack leaves us within 3 business days and takes 5 to 7 days to reach you. The base style catalogue, shipping guidance and launch guide come by email within 1 business day of your request.

At the end of development, do I receive the final print files and the tech pack for my styles?

The full tech pack documentation we prepare is yours to keep. Once you've paid for a design, we send you its print files whenever you ask for them. We keep the pattern files for reorders, and if you want those files yourself, there's a charge for each pattern.

Can I commission aloha shirt development and receive the resulting technical specifications without committing to bulk production with you?

Yes. We offer tech pack creation and sampling as standalone services. The tech pack documentation you get from them is yours to keep, and you have no obligation to place bulk production with us.
