Kids' Hawaiian Clothing Made in China

If you're looking for a private label kids' Hawaiian clothing manufacturer, you can start with 50 pieces per style in each colour, a starting quantity that suits small and growing labels.

We make kids' clothing in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and we agree first-trial quantities with you case by case within production limits. A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, with order size and season setting where it falls in that range.

OEM Kids' Hawaiian Clothing: Quotes and Sample Costs

Each sample costs USD 50, plus USD 50 per design for its pattern or print, which covers all colourways.

Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order. Our company has run its own factory since 2011, keeping production and export under direct control. Every B2B order is agreed directly with our team. We don't sell through an online shop or marketplace store.

Our product development team turns your design, picture or idea into OEM or ODM work and handles patterns, grading and fit in-house. For sampling, courier delivery costs an additional USD 50. We approve fit against a measurement sheet, and live fitting videos are available.

Our order process starts with a quote. Once you agree the price, we move on to making samples. Our English-speaking contact team replies within one business day, and you can reach them by phone or video call throughout your order.

For your quote request, send us your product categories, exact style IDs, quantities per style and colour, and your size range, so we can check minimum quantities before pricing. We also confirm artwork, print scale, labels, packaging and sample requirements.

We take your brief through sampling, approved production and inspection all the way to delivery.

Our Quality Control covers checks from fabric to finished garment, plus independent inspection. Coverage includes OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, certified plain-colour dyes, GOTS organic cotton styles, BCI cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 and WRAP. We confirm what each certificate covers and send copies on request.

Tests for markets outside the US are arranged after you approve them, and you pay for each test item. Girls' dresses in sizes 2T to 14 are designed to the EN 14682 and ASTM F1816 cord requirements, with no neck drawstrings or overlength ties.

For under-3s we leave out detachable small trims, and each US order for those sizes gets an accredited small-parts test at a lab such as SGS or BV.

Our fabric choices include natural, regenerated and synthetic fibres, with blends such as 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton. With Customisation, we match print, thread and fabric dye to your Pantone references.

Our Custom Print Design service creates tropical florals, foliage, beach scenes and novelty prints that are unique to your group.

For Matching Family & Group Sets, we carry your aloha print from toddler sizes up to 5XL and scale the artwork to each garment size so the whole set looks consistent. Our Cut & Sew service works from your DXF patterns.

It runs two rounds of pre-production samples with pattern and machine adjustments, followed by full-range regrading. Our Packaging service uses FSC-certified paper packs and GRS-certified recycled content in plastic packaging and poly mailers. Any packaging that touches the shirts directly is certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

Our Embroidery service gives you designer-prepared detailed embroidery, patches and labels, with logos that stand up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

For Garment Dyeing, we use certified dyestuffs and silicone softeners that are free from heavy metals, formaldehyde and banned azo compounds and that meet OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I requirements.

Before ordering, we confirm what the dye, softener and fabric certificates cover, and we supply current copies on request. Our Private Label service is available on every order. Label, tag and logo options include woven labels and heat-transfer artwork made in-house.

With Trims & Accessories, we help you compare button materials, sizes, colours and attachments, choose plackets and brand details, and develop colour-matched accessories or custom hardware. Under our after-delivery terms, you report any quality problem within 7 days with photos or videos.

We come back with a solution within 24 hours: repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit. For Custom Buttons, custom-dyed or logo-engraved designs need a separate lot of 1,000 pieces per design in each colour, and we store the spares for reorders.

Colour-matched stock buttons have no separate minimum and work at the 50-piece garment minimum.

Through Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we write fibre, origin and wash-tested care information from your actual materials so it complies with the rules where you sell, and we add RN or CA numbers where they're needed.

We get your approval on the artwork before production and check the labels on every private-label order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Apart from shirts, which kids' Hawaiian pieces can you make, such as girls' dresses, shorts sets or rompers?

We make girls' dresses, aloha shorts sets that pair camp-collar shirts with elastic-waist shorts, and short-leg rompers in matching prints. Shorts sets and rompers run from 6 months to 14 years, made to your design or one of our styles. For sizes under 3 years, we leave out detachable small trims such as beads and loose bows and use printed or flat-stitched decoration instead. Ties and cords follow EN 14682 for the EU and ASTM F1816 for the US.

Do your fabrics, buttons and trims meet children's safety rules such as CPSIA or EN 71?

Before shipment, we check buttons, snaps and ties against the applicable children's safety rules and pull-test each button and snap batch at 90 N for 10 seconds. EN 71 covers toys, not clothing, so garment cords and drawstrings follow EN 14682. For the US, our Quality Control service books CPSIA lead, phthalate and flammability tests at a CPSC-accepted laboratory on every order, at the lab's rate per item, and you, as importer, issue the Children's Product Certificate from the results.

What kids' size range do you cover, and can you grade the pattern to my own size chart?

Shorts sets and rompers cover 6 months to 14 years, and girls' dresses cover sizes 2T to 14. Our in-house development team handles pattern grading and fit development.

Can you scale my print down for the smaller sizes so the motifs don't look too big on a child?

Yes. For family and matching programmes, we scale the artwork to each garment size, so the print stays consistent from toddler sizes to 5XL and the same aloha design runs across your matching sets.
