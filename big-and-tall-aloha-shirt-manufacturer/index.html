Big and Tall Aloha Shirt Manufacturing in China

Our premium aloha shirts come in sizes XXS to 5XL, so you can stock larger sizes next to standard ones in the same collection.

They come in four cuts: unisex/men’s, men’s slim fit, women’s and women’s vintage. If you’re looking for an OEM big and tall aloha shirt manufacturer that makes its shirts itself, we produce every one in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Why Do Sizes 2XL to 5XL Cost More?

Each 2XL to 5XL piece costs more because it takes more fabric, has a larger print area and needs an extra button on the longer placket.

We show you a separate extended-size price tier before sampling. Pricing these sizes separately, at cost and retail, keeps their margins visible. Averaging them into one style price hides size-level margins and gets less accurate as your size mix shifts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer tall sizing with a longer body and sleeves, separate from the big sizes?

Yes. Our tall sizes run from LT to 4XLT. We grade them separately from our big sizes, so you get extra length without extra width. Each tall size adds 5 cm to body length and 3 cm to sleeve length over the matching regular size, and we also offer different length increments to match your size chart. Your customers' fit feedback tells you which range they need: tall sizing adds length, and big sizing adds room in the chest and shoulders. If you stock tall sizes instead of the matching big sizes, your customers get extra length but lose the additional chest, shoulder and waist room. Choose the wrong range and the shirt ends up too short or too baggy.

Is your big & tall range suited to specialty big & tall retailers and to uniform programmes that must cover every staff size?

Yes. We build the range for specialty big & tall retailers, for resort and surf shops that carry extended sizes, and for uniform or staff programmes that need XXS to 5XL in one matching print and fit. Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, and you can spread them across 3 to 4 sizes at no extra charge. That way you cover the larger sizes without ordering a full run of each one.

Are your 2XL–5XL shirts graded with their own proportions and construction details, or just scaled up from the standard pattern?

We grade 2XL–5XL shirts to their own big & tall proportions. We don't just scale up the standard pattern. We set chest, shoulder width, armhole depth, waist ease, body length and sleeve length separately for every size. From 2XL up, we lengthen the front placket and add one extra button. We also cut 6 cm side vents and place the chest pocket lower and further out to suit a larger frame.
