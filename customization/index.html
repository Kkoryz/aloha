Custom Hawaiian Shirts: Colours & Customisation

We make your aloha shirts in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and we produce custom colours only after you've approved them.

Before bulk production starts, we check your reference against the fabric you've chosen and the sample you've approved. Solid fabrics come in white, light blue, navy, black, gray and beige.

The fabric and dyeing method you choose change the final colour, so the same reference comes out differently on each base and with each method.

Colour Palettes & Minimum Orders

  • Custom palettes

    We build seasonal and campaign colour groups for your retail drops, events, uniforms and private label lines.

  • Physical colour standards

    Colour shifts from one dye lot to the next, so we keep your approved physical standards to hold your colours consistent across seasonal reorders.

  • Minimum order

    You can start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which gives small and growing labels a way in.

  • Trial quantities

    We agree your first-trial quantities case by case, and we are as flexible on quantity as production allows.

  • Direct factory ordering

    You order directly from our factory-owned production and export business, which keeps quality, cost and delivery under our direct control. We've made tens of thousands of custom aloha shirts.

Order Stages & Payment Schedule

  1. Brief and sampling

    We take your brief or tech pack through sampling until we reach the shirt you approve for production.

  2. Approval and deposit

    You approve the sample, and you pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order.

  3. Production and dispatch

    We finish bulk production and run a full final inspection, and you pay the remaining 50% before shipment.

Production Times & Worldwide Delivery

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season set where your order falls in that range.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Shipping quotation

    Shipping pricing depends on quantity and destination. Send us those details and we'll calculate the cost.

Your Shirt Brief & Design Options

For custom-tailored or unlisted requests, we make a prototype from your reference garment or tech pack for you to approve before bulk production, so fit, construction and material problems surface before they repeat across the order.

Our standard ordering process starts with a quotation, and sampling follows once you agree the price. In your quote request, send your fabric choice, artwork, colour direction, quantities and delivery schedule.

With Product Development, you choose custom design, internationally graded ready designs or a mix of both, and you don't need a tech pack. Custom Print Design covers tropical artwork, logos, solids, face prints and dog or cat prints from clear photos.

Our confidentiality and ownership terms give you an NDA and ownership of the patterns developed from your artwork, and we never resell your exclusive prints.

Shirt Cuts, Collars & Sizes

Collar shape and proportions

We adjust camp, spread or notch collars to suit your print scale. A camp collar gives a relaxed look, while a closed spread collar looks more structured and dressy.

Four shirt cuts

Our aloha shirt cuts are unisex/men’s, men’s slim fit, women’s and women’s vintage, and each one is graded over 47 measurement points. Women’s vintage shirts also come with mandarin-collar and cap-sleeve options.

Coordinated size runs

Matching Family & Group Sets run from toddler sizes up to 5XL. We scale the artwork to each garment size so the prints match consistently.

Woven Stripes, Checks & Jacquard

Yarn-dyed patterns show on both sides and stand up to repeated washing. The trade-off is that you lose the freedom printing gives you for detailed, many-coloured artwork such as florals.

  • Yarn-dyed stripes

    What we develop for you
    Our partner mills dye the yarn before weaving, and we adjust stripe width, spacing and colour combinations to your order.

  • Yarn-dyed checks

    What we develop for you
    We develop check, plaid and flannel-style patterns using your check size, repeat spacing and colour layout.

  • Jacquard

    What we develop for you
    Our partner mills weave subtle textures, mini geometrics, tone-on-tone dots and stripes right into the cloth. It takes extra loom setup and sampling time, so it suits confirmed collections and bulk programmes.

  • Matched construction

    What we develop for you
    With our Cut & Sew alignment, we match stripes and checks across the placket, pocket, camp collar and side seams to within 0.5 cm. We also place bulk chest, pocket and placket motifs within 0.5 cm of your approved sample.

All-Over Patterns & Print Methods

We develop floral, icon, geometric, seasonal and branded all-over prints for resort, event and promotional shirts. We choose the fabric before we finalise colours, and we make the strike-off on that same base.

  • Fabric response

    What it means for your shirts
    Cotton, rayon, polyester and blends each take ink differently, which changes the colour, texture and hand. Rayon gives a softer drape and richer colour than cotton, but it needs more careful washing and shrinkage control.

  • Fabric range

    What it means for your shirts
    Hawaiian Fabrics offers more than 580 bases, including yarn-dyed fabrics at 100 to 140 GSM and jacquards at 110 to 160 GSM. For a muted outer pattern, there's reverse-print cotton poplin at 110–130 gsm, approved through a two-sided strike-off and sample.

  • Material sourcing

    What it means for your shirts
    Through Fabric Sourcing, you choose library or custom materials from 60 to 200 GSM. Sampling swatches let you compare weights, drape and hand before you approve.

  • Printing specifications

    What it means for your shirts
    With Custom Printing, screen printing gives deep spot colours and solid white on dyed grounds, but overlapping heavy layers feel firmer and reduce airflow. Digital printing keeps the drape of natural fibres and suits shading or more than 8 colours. Digital detail reaches 0.3 mm lines and 6 pt lettering (about 2 mm cap height), and screen detail reaches 0.5 mm and 8 pt. Crisp logos on rayon need at least 0.5 mm and 8 pt. Each screen colour adds setup cost, while digital cost stays the same whatever the colour count. Sublimation needs light-coloured polyester or blends with at least 60% polyester. Screen repeats reach 64 cm, while digital and sublimation use the full 150 cm printable width. Large repeats or pattern matching use 10–15% more fabric, and that's included in your unit price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What limits on motif size, repeat size and fine detail should my designer follow when preparing a custom jacquard?

Motifs should be between 0.5 cm and 8 cm, and repeats no bigger than 16 cm wide by 20 cm high. Use no more than 4 yarn colours. Lines need to be at least 1 mm and gaps at least 1.5 mm, because anything finer blurs in the weave. We check your artwork and adapt it before the mill weaves the sample. Simpler designs come out sharper and cost less in setup and yarn.

Which choices in yarn-colour count, repeat size and weave complexity change the cost of custom yarn-dyed or jacquard fabric?

Every yarn colour after the first two adds cost. Longer repeats take more loom setup, and dense or multi-layer jacquard weaves cost more than plain stripes and simple checks.
