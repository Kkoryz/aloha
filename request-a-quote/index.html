Order Minimums and Trial Runs

Our garment orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can order with us.

We agree first trial runs case by case, and we flex quantities only as far as production allows.

To make the minimum-quantity calculation clear before we price samples or bulk, send us your product categories, exact style IDs, expected units for each style and colour, and your size range. Before quoting, we also confirm your print scale and what you need from the sample.

What We Need to Prepare Your Quote

We also need the following in your quote brief so we can account for your market, delivery requirements and shipping responsibilities.

  • Artwork or references

    Send your artwork or reference images so we have the design information to prepare an accurate quote.

  • Destination and channel

    As part of your product brief, tell us your destination country and market, along with your retail channel.

  • Delivery target

    Give us your target delivery date or delivery window so your brief states when you need the order.

  • Shipping term

    Tell us whether you want FOB, CIF or DDP, and we'll talk them through with you before quoting. The term you choose affects landed cost, carton planning, customs responsibility and the format of your quote.

  • Without freight support

    If you don't have a freight forwarder or customs broker in the destination market, DDP gives you one delivered price instead of freight arrangements to manage.

  • With freight support

    If you already work with a freight forwarder or customs broker in the destination market, FOB keeps those arrangements with your existing provider.

  • DDP trade-off

    Choosing DDP over FOB gets you one delivered price, and in exchange you give up your choice of forwarder and direct control of freight costs.

  • Overall cost comparison

    Who clears customs and pays destination duties decides which shipping term costs you less overall and which is simpler for you to manage.

Preparing Your Design and Planning Samples

Before we plan your samples, we separate the design decisions you've confirmed from the development questions that are still open.

  1. Identify confirmed details

    Mark your logo files, repeat artwork, label artwork, care-label wording, packaging references and retail requirements as confirmed wherever those decisions are already settled.

  2. Identify open decisions

    Let us know which choices are still open, including fabric, print method, trims and fit corrections, so we can work through them in sample rounds.

  3. Review design requirements

    Send us your fabric choice and colour direction. We then go through your brief with you, covering sizing, fabric, printing method, design direction, private labelling and packing.

  4. Lock settled items

    We lock the confirmed details first, then settle the open items one at a time. If several items change in one sample, nobody can tell which change fixed or caused a problem.

  5. Plan sample development

    Once we've reviewed the requirements with you, we map out a sampling route and bulk pricing options.

When Your Quote Arrives and What It Covers

Your quote is ready within 24 hours of us receiving your style information.

  • Accepted style information

    For each style, send spec sheets, sketches, a tech pack or detailed photos. A description of the garment type, sizes and quantities works too.

  • Quote calculation

    We work out your quote from the style, fabric, quantity, size range and customisation, based on the details you give us.

  • Enquiry reply

    Our reply includes fabric recommendations, a timeline and pricing broken down by style, so you have what you need to review your range.

  • Separate style prices

    Each style has its own price line, so you can drop, add or change one style without a full requote.

  • Price validity

    Your quoted price stays valid for 30 days from the date we issue it, and that is the period the quote covers.

  • Before sampling

    We always quote before making any sample.

How Bulk Prices and Payments Work

We price bulk after checking your design and fabric against real production needs.

Cost depends on construction, fabric availability, lining, print method, logo application, labels, packaging, size ratio and lead time, and we'll show which small tweaks reach your target without changing the core design. An itemised quote is obligation-free.

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Sample Charges and Other Costs

At the sampling stage, we'll point out which excluded extras apply to your style, so you can budget for development.

  • Sample charge

    Each sample is USD 50. We don't refund this fee or credit it against your bulk production order.

  • Design charge

    Each design is USD 50, which covers its pattern or print in all colourways. It isn't refunded or credited against bulk.

  • Separate development pricing

    If pattern and sample development charges apply, we quote them separately and don't fold them into the bulk price.

  • Materials and colour work

    Materials, trims and swatches aren't part of the quoted charges unless your quote lists them.

  • Decoration and setup

    Decoration and setup aren't included unless they're listed in your quote, and we'll point out the extras that apply during sampling.

  • Additional development costs

    Further revisions, extra samples, freight and testing are excluded too, unless your quote specifically lists them as included.

  • Approval before spending

    We always check with you before taking on any additional cost, so nothing extra is spent without your agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the sample fee refunded: when I place the bulk order, or only above a certain quantity?

We don't refund the sample fee or credit it against a bulk order, whatever the quantity. Each sample is USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design and covers its pattern or print in all colourways. It's also non-refundable and isn't credited against bulk.

Does the quoted price include shipping and duties, and which Incoterms do you quote on?

Our all-in DDP price includes shipping and duties. We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

How long is a quoted price valid before I have to ask for a new one?

Your quoted price stays valid for 30 days from the date we issue it.

When is the balance due after the deposit, and which payment methods do you accept?

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment. We invoice in USD and take T/T bank transfer for both payments. Sample and design fees are paid before sampling, and you can pay those through PayPal as well. Sampling starts as soon as the fee clears. For these fees, PayPal gets sampling started sooner than T/T but comes with higher transaction fees, and T/T suits larger amounts.
