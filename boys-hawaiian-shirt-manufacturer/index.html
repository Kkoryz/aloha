Boys' Hawaiian Shirt Production in China

If you're looking for a custom boys' Hawaiian shirt manufacturer that works with small and growing labels, our orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour.

We make every shirt in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree first trial quantities with you case by case, within our production limits. A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, and your order size and the season decide where in that range your order falls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which fabric do you recommend for boys' shirts that get washed a lot: rayon, cotton poplin or a blend?

For everyday boys’ shirts that go through the washing machine often, we recommend 110–130 gsm cotton poplin or a 65% polyester 35% cotton blend. Both hold their shape and print better than rayon. Rayon fibres swell and lose strength when they're wet, so frequent machine washing makes rayon shirts shrink, twist and lose their print brightness faster. If you choose poplin or poly-cotton, you give up rayon’s soft, fluid drape and cool feel. For dress-up styles that get gentle care, 100–120 gsm rayon is the right choice.

Can you make short and long sleeves, and camp collars as well as band collars?

Yes. We make short and long sleeves, with buttoned cuffs or roll-up sleeve tabs, and we make camp, spread, notch and band collars. We adjust the band-collar height and shape to suit your design, and we carry your print across collars, sleeves and cuffs. You can mix sleeve lengths in one order as long as each style reaches 50 pieces in each colour.

Do you offer slim and regular fits, and how do you grade boys' sizes from toddler to teen?

Yes. We offer regular and slim fits, plus a relaxed boxy fit. Our in-house design team develops each fit and grades it to your size specification, from toddler through to youth sizes. Regular fits give about 10–12 cm of ease at the chest, and slim fits give about 6–8 cm.

Can you line up the print across the front placket and the chest pocket on boys' shirts?

Yes. We print all-over patterns on the fabric roll before cutting, so the repeat runs continuously and matches across the collar, front placket, chest pocket and hem. For engineered or placement designs, we cut the panels first, print each one (for example, by sublimation transfer on polyester) and then sew them together, so the motif lands in the same spot on every shirt.
