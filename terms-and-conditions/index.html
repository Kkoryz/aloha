Our Factory and Minimum Orders

You can order aloha shirts from 50 pieces per style in each colour, a minimum we set with small and growing labels in mind.

We make them ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. For a first trial order, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within what our production requirements allow. We invoice in USD and take T/T bank transfer.

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment. You pay sample and design fees before sampling starts, and you can also pay these fees by PayPal.

Production Schedules and Delivery Options

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season decide where it falls in that range.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and DDP is available worldwide.

  • DDP costs and handling

    DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Direct ordering only

    We work as a B2B manufacturer and sell only through this website and our team. You agree every order with us directly. We have no online shop, third-party marketplace or platform store.

Sample Fees and Preparation Times

Sample and design fees aren't refunded, and we don't credit them against your bulk order.

  • Sample fee

    Each sample costs USD 50. This is charged separately from the design fee and the courier delivery charge.

  • Design fee

    You pay USD 50 per design. That covers its pattern or print, with all colourways included.

  • Preparation and courier

    Your standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days, and courier delivery costs you USD 50 on top of the sample fee.

Order Stages and Payment Schedule

Our order process has a 10 to 15 day sample stage and 35 to 40 days of bulk production.

  1. 01

    Brief and quotation

    You send us your brief or tech pack and we quote first. Once you agree to the price, we make the sample.

  2. 02

    Approval and deposit

    You approve the sample and pay the deposit to confirm the order, and then we start bulk production.

  3. 03

    Balance and shipment

    We do the final inspection before dispatch. You pay the remaining balance before we ship on the delivery terms you agreed.

Confidentiality and Ownership of Your Designs

We sign a non-disclosure agreement with you before you send any artwork, so your designs and business information stay confidential.

That agreement is in place before your designs reach our design and sampling teams. Any aloha pattern we develop from your artwork stays your intellectual property.

We keep a record of which patterns are exclusive to you, so they never appear in our catalogue and are never offered or sold to another client. Because that exclusivity record is clear, your print isn't reused by mistake.

Quality Checks and Support After Delivery

  • Checks throughout production

    Our quality checks cover incoming fabric, cutting, sewing, random checks and a full final inspection. You're welcome to bring in independent inspectors such as SGS or BV before shipment.

  • Inspection records

    We keep inspection records for every order. When you send us photos or videos, we compare them against those records to tell production defects apart from transit damage, without long back-and-forth.

  • After-delivery contact

    We stay in touch after delivery and act quickly if your goods arrive damaged or have any other issue.

  • Reporting a problem

    If you find a quality problem, let us know within 7 days of delivery and send photos or videos that show it.

  • Solution within 24 hours

    We review your case and come back to you with a solution within 24 hours. Depending on the issue, that's a repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit.

Discuss Your Order With Our Team

Send your enquiry to our factory team with your brief or tech pack. We quote first, then move on to sampling once you agree to the price.
