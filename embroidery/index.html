Custom Embroidery for Hawaiian Shirts

We make your aloha shirts in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and decorate them with flat embroidery, 3D puff embroidery or custom patches on our in-house multi-head machines.

For embroidered patches, we stitch your design onto a separate fabric base and then apply it to the shirt. This method suits thin fabrics. We also cut custom die-cut shapes and attach your patches by sewing or heat transfer.

Embroidery Techniques, Fabrics and Placement Options

Dense fills stiffen a lightweight shirt. Appliqué keeps the drape because the fabric is secured only at its edges. We cut and adhere your pattern and add cotton padding to give it a raised effect.

  • Fabric and technique

    We embroider directly onto cotton, rayon from about 100 gsm, linen and blends with up to 5% spandex, using cut-away backing. Silk and fabrics under 100 gsm need embroidered patches so they don't pucker.

  • Placement and alignment

    We embroider the flat panels before the shirt is assembled, so your design can go on the chest, pocket, back, sleeve, collar points or front hem. A design that crosses the pocket lines up within 3 mm, and collar stitching stays clear of the roll line. A design that crosses the opening splits at the placket edge, clear of the buttonholes.

  • Your manufacturing partner

    Our company has run its own factory since 2011 and handles production and export directly for business buyers worldwide. You agree your order with our team, not through an online shop or marketplace.

  • Developing your shirt

    Our Product Development service supports OEM and ODM work. You can bring your own custom designs, choose ready designs graded to international sizes, or combine the two. We do pattern, grading and fit work in-house, and you don't need a tech pack to start.

  • Wider fabric choices

    Our fabric range covers cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk and wool. It also includes blends such as 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton.

  • One coordinated process

    Our production process keeps fabric, embroidery, printing, sewing and finishing on one timeline with one contact. We agree your quote before sampling, and you approve the sample before bulk production starts.

  • Direct English-speaking contact

    You can contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call at any point in your order. We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

  • Individual uniform details

    Our Hawaiian uniform shirts pair shared prints and branding with individual names, titles, initials or numbers. We add these in embroidery or heat-transfer vinyl from your spreadsheet. This adds 3 to 5 production days, and then we pack by department, branch or name list.

Thread Colours Matched to Your Brand

  • Pantone matching

    What it means for your shirts
    We match your palette from hundreds of stock colours. If your colour isn't in our library, we source custom thread.

  • Stitched colour approval

    What it means for your shirts
    We judge each match on a stitched sample in daylight. Thread sheen reflects light differently from ink or a screen, so colours look lighter or shift in tone. This is strongest with glossy polyester or rayon.

  • Polyester

    What it means for your shirts
    This is our standard thread. It has a bright sheen and stands up well to frequent washing and bleach, so it's the choice for uniforms and shirts that get washed often.

  • Rayon

    What it means for your shirts
    We use rayon when a softer hand and a silk-like lustre matter most to you.

  • Matte cotton

    What it means for your shirts
    We use matte cotton to give your shirts a vintage look.

  • Metallic

    What it means for your shirts
    We keep metallic thread to small accent areas because it feels stiffer against the skin.

  • Reorder consistency

    What it means for your shirts
    We record your thread colours on your production specification, so your reorders use the same colours as your first run.

Embroidery Order Minimums and Production Capacity

Your order starts at 50 pieces per style in each colour.

Your test batch and the bulk run that follows use the same setup. That way the test shows how placement, fabric and stitching behave on real shirts before you commit to the full quantity. We keep thread tension, stitch density and Pantone matching consistent the whole way through.

We agree your first-trial quantity with you case by case, within our production capacity.

Before each run, we match sharp needles, cut-away backing, bobbin length and frame tension to your fabric.

Dull or unsuitable needles cut fine rayon and linen fibres, and the wrong hoop tension causes puckering or stitch misalignment. Our quality control covers incoming fabric, cutting, sewing, random checks and a full final inspection, and you're welcome to arrange independent SGS or BV inspection before shipment.

It also includes OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GOTS for organic cotton styles, BCI for cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance. Our Cut & Sew service uses size-specific panel templates.

We assemble, button, label, trim, press and measure your shirts with inline checks throughout, then pack each one in its own poly bag.

From Artwork to Approved Embroidery Preview

  1. Send your artwork

    We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PNG and JPG files, and we prefer AI or EPS vectors. We review your files for design issues before digitising.

  2. Check design dimensions

    Embroidery fields reach 30 x 30 cm, and chest, pocket and sleeve logos fit within 10 x 10 cm. Letters need to be at least 5 mm and lines at least 1 mm, and we flag any finer details for thickening.

  3. Digitise for placement

    Digitising is included. We set density, underlay and stitch sequence for your specific fabric and placement instead of scaling one file across chest, sleeve and back.

  4. Approve your preview

    We prepare your free digital embroidery preview within 24 hours of receiving your artwork. Production starts only after you approve it.

  5. Retain your file

    We archive your approved digitised design, so your future reorders run from the same stitch file.

  6. Protect your designs

    Our confidentiality terms include an NDA covering your designs and business information. You keep ownership of aloha patterns developed from your artwork, and the terms prohibit us from selling your exclusive print to another client.

Wash Performance and Combined Decoration Options

Printing limits

Custom printing limits sublimation to light-coloured fabrics with at least 60% polyester. Screen repeats reach 64 cm, and digital and sublimation widths reach 150 cm.

Large repeats or matching use 10–15% more fabric, and that's already included in your unit price.

Wash and care

The thread is stitched through the fabric, so it won't crack or peel in the wash. Wash your embroidered shirts inside out on a gentle cycle and iron them from the reverse.

Combined decoration

We combine embroidery on the same shirt with a screen-printed back graphic, an all-over sublimation print or heat-transfer elements.

Stitching over prints

We embroider straight over screen, digital or sublimation prints. For example, we outline a printed hibiscus or add texture to your printed logo.

Embroidery Costs and Design Choices

  • Price drivers

    Your price per shirt goes up with higher stitch counts, larger designs, more colours or placements, 3D puff instead of flat stitching, patches, appliqué and specialty threads.

  • Lower-cost design choices

    A single flat placement with fewer colours costs less. Swapping large fills for outlines cuts down the stitching, so you don't have to shrink your logo until it's no longer legible.

  • Sample fee

    Each sample costs USD 50. We don't refund this fee or credit it against your bulk order.

  • Design fee

    We charge USD 50 per design, which covers its pattern or print in all colourways. We don't refund this fee or credit it against your bulk order either.

  • Payment schedule

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Quotation scope

    Our quotation terms list any applicable development charges separately. They exclude materials, trims, swatches, decoration, setup, further revisions, extra samples, freight and testing unless these are listed, and any additional cost needs your approval first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide embroidery-only service on finished aloha shirts we supply, and what garment restrictions apply?

No. We only embroider, print, heat-press or garment-dye aloha shirts that we've cut and sewn ourselves. Send us your logo or artwork, and we'll manufacture and decorate your shirts in the same run.

Does adding embroidery add extra days to the sample or bulk production time?

Embroidered samples still take our standard 10 to 15 days. In bulk, embroidery adds 5 to 7 days to our standard 35 to 40 days of production, which depends on order size and season.

How many different thread colours can you use in one embroidered design?

We use up to 15 thread colours in one design, and we stitch a strike-off for you to check before bulk. Approve that stitched strike-off on your actual shirt fabric, not just from the digital preview.

Can you use our existing digitised embroidery file, and which stitch-file formats do you accept?

Yes. We accept DST, PES, EXP, JEF and EMB files. We check your file, adjust the density or underlay where lightweight aloha fabrics need it, and send you a digital preview to approve before production.

How do you stop embroidery from puckering thin rayon or linen, and will the backing feel stiff or scratchy inside the shirt?

On rayon, linen and stretch fabrics, we use a soft cut-away stabiliser sized to each embroidered area. On thin fabrics we also lighten the stitch density so they don't pucker. We trim the backing close to the stitches and finish the reverse neatly, so it feels soft rather than stiff or scratchy.
