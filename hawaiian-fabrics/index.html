Your Fabric, Your Collection — Start at 50 Pieces per Style and Colour

You can start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can order their own aloha shirts made with Hawaiian fabric.

We make them ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree first trial quantities case by case, and we are as flexible on quantity as production allows. A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

Once you approve your sample and pay the deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

Each sample costs USD 50, and design costs USD 50 per design, covering every colourway of its pattern or print. Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk orders.

  • Exclusive print development

    Send us your references, sketches or moodboard, and through our Custom Print Design we develop exclusive, production-ready seamless repeats, with motifs aligned across the collar, placket, pocket and hem.

  • Printing options

    Custom Printing covers reverse-print 100% cotton at 110–130 gsm, screen repeats up to 64 cm and digital up to 150 cm. Sublimation works only on light-coloured polyester or on blends with at least 60% polyester.

    Our factory-owned B2B business keeps production and export under our direct control, and you agree every order with our team, not through an online shop or third-party marketplace.

  • Design and fit

    OEM and ODM development starts from your design, picture or idea. We handle pattern design, grading and fit development in-house, which cuts down the design back-and-forth.

  • Sampling and courier

    Sampling confirms construction, fabric hand, print scale, labels, shrinkage risk and fit before bulk. Sample courier delivery costs you USD 50 on top of the sample fee.

  • Quote before sampling

    In our order process, we quote first, then make your sample once you agree the price.

  • Your brand alone

    With our Private Label service, we supply your approved labels, logos, trims, buttons, tags and coordinated packaging under your brand alone, and keep our name off the garments.

  • Project review and pricing

    Request a quote with your fabric, artwork, colours, quantities and schedule. We review the production requirements, map out sampling options and send back an itemised, no-obligation bulk quote within 24 hours.

Which Shirt Fabric Is Right for Your Collection?

The right shirt fabric is the one that matches the hand feel, drape, structure, stretch and care your collection needs.

  • Fabric range and preshrinking

    You can choose from more than 580 bases, including cotton, polyester, rayon, poly/cotton and silk, to match your hand feel, drape and price level. Every fabric is preshrunk, so your shirts keep their washed size and fit.

  • Linen weight and structure

    Our linen and linen blends run from 140 to 180 gsm, and we check the GSM in your tech pack before sending swatches. 140–160 gsm gives you cool, easy-draping aloha shirts, while 160–180 gsm gives you crisper, more structured resort shirts.

  • Finished pure rayon

    When the soft hand of pure rayon matters most, we apply a crease-resistant finish to cut down creasing. The fibre stays the same, and only the surface changes.

  • Rayon for hard wear

    For hard wear or travel, our rayon blend with 30% polyester recovers faster after sitting or packing. In return, you give up some of pure rayon's cool, fluid hand and hot-weather breathability.

  • Weft-only stretch

    When your shirt needs crosswise give, our cotton or rayon poplin with 3–5% spandex, including 95% cotton 5% spandex, gives about 10–15% stretch across the width.

  • Four-way or non-stretch

    Our four-way polyester or nylon with 8–20% spandex stretches 20–30% in both width and length. For classic non-stretch aloha drape, you can choose 100% cotton, rayon, linen or silk.

  • Linen and polyester care

    We specify cold, gentle machine washing, then hanging to dry or low tumble drying, with no bleach or high heat. Iron linen warm while it's slightly damp, and iron polyester only on low if needed.

  • Seams and cutting

    Our Cut & Sew uses 1 cm overlocked/French rayon seams tested to ISO 13936-2 (maximum 6 mm opening), or 0.6 cm French seams for fine fabrics. Matched, directional or large-repeat prints take 10–15% extra fabric, and that's included in your price.

Your Prints and Colours, on Fabric by the Metre or Roll

  • Fabric-only order minimums

    Besides finished shirts, you can buy our Hawaiian printed fabrics by the metre or by the roll. Prints from our existing library and custom prints both start at 300 metres per colourway.

  • Roll size and protection

    Each roll holds about 50 to 100 metres. Before shipment, we inspect every roll for shade, defects and width, then pack it on a core in sealed film for export.

  • One colourway, one run

    We print the full metreage of each colourway in one run, so every roll in your order comes from the same shade lot.

  • Separate-run shade differences

    When the same design is printed in separate runs, the shade varies slightly. You'll see that difference when panels from different runs are sewn side by side.

  • Fabric and detailing costs

    Rayon costs more per shirt than polyester. Your unit price also depends on your fabric choice, the complexity of your embroidery and print, and custom labels, and we itemise each of these in your quote.

  • Matching across fabric bases

    For Matching Family & Group Sets, each fibre renders Pantone differently. So we match every base to one reference, and you approve the strike-offs/samples side by side before bulk. We also scale the artwork from toddler sizes up to 5XL and sample the whole set together.

Feel Swatches and Review Quality Checks

  • Swatch pack

    What we provide and what you check
    You can order a separate swatch pack for USD 80 with DHL Express delivery included. We credit that amount against your first bulk purchase order.

  • Hands-on fabric assessment

    What we provide and what you check
    Handle, crumple and hand-wash the swatches before you choose your base. Photos and specifications don't show hand feel, drape, wrinkle recovery or true colour, and finding these differences at the sampling stage takes longer.

  • Comfort-stretch qualities

    What we provide and what you check
    Our comfort-stretch fabric is lightweight, breathable and wrinkle-resistant, and the swatches let you judge those qualities for yourself.

  • Fibre and blend choices

    What we provide and what you check
    We work with cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk and wool. Other blend options include 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton.

  • Materials for your factory

    What we provide and what you check
    Our standalone Fabric Sourcing starts at 300 metres per colour for stock or custom-dyed fabrics and keeps garment item minimums for trims. We deliver inspected materials to your factory anywhere in the world, with reports and photos.

  • Colour development

    What we provide and what you check
    With Garment Dyeing, you pick Pantone Coated, stock-card or custom shades. Custom minimums depend on the fabric and dye method, and we confirm feasibility during sampling. We keep colour consistent across fabric, lining and trims.

  • Quality standards and testing

    What we provide and what you check
    Our Quality Control covers OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics/trims, GOTS for organic cotton styles, BCI cotton sourcing, ISO 9001 quality management and WRAP social compliance, and we welcome SGS/BV inspection. We test printed/dyed rayon, cotton and polyester, plus printed silk, to minimums of ISO 105-C06 washing Grade 4, ISO 105-X12 rubbing Grade 3–4 (dry and wet for cotton/rayon) and ISO 105-E04 perspiration Grade 3–4. Rayon, cotton and polyester also meet a minimum of ISO 105-B02 light Grade 3–4.

  • Approved care labels

    What we provide and what you check
    Our Custom Labels & Hang Tags service prepares the fibre content, care instructions based on wash tests, origin and any required RN/CA details for you to approve. We follow the rules of your destination market, including US FTC, EU/UK fibre-name and Australian care-labelling requirements, and we check the labels on every private-label order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which lightweight fabric bases remain sufficiently opaque for unlined shirts with white or pale print backgrounds?

Cotton poplin at 120–140 gsm, rayon at 120–130 gsm and linen at 150 gsm and above stay light, and skin and pocket outlines don't show through them on white or pale print grounds. We choose the weight by garment type and climate, then check it against your background colour. A weight that suits a flowing muumuu or a breathable work shirt isn't automatically opaque enough for an unlined pale-ground shirt. Silk under 90 gsm and rayon under 110 gsm show through on pale grounds, so before sampling we send you swatches printed in your background colour so you can check the opacity.

What fabric weights are available for each uniform shirt fabric, and which weight suits all-day wear in hot weather?

Our uniform shirt fabrics come in cotton poplin at 110–140 gsm, rayon at 100–130 gsm, polyester microfibre at 100–130 gsm, silk at 60–90 gsm, cotton-polyester blends at 120–150 gsm and moisture-wicking polyester at 110–140 gsm. For all-day wear in hot weather, we choose 100–130 gsm rayon or moisture-wicking polyester. Both stay light and breathable and dry quickly between shifts. Before you commit, we check the chosen weight against your print's ground colour.
