Your Hawaiian Dress Factory in China

You can order Hawaiian dresses from 50 pieces per style in each colour, which lets a small or growing label start with a limited run.

For a first trial, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within our production capacity. A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days. Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit.

We have one factory of our own, in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and we make our aloha shirts there too.

Private Label Dress Finishing and Export Support

We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

Private label finishing

Our Private Label service is available at every order quantity. It covers woven, printed and heat-transfer labels, hang tags, metal tags, logos and silicone branding, and we make the woven labels and heat-transfer artwork in-house.

Packing for distribution

Our Packaging service covers custom bags, eco packs, retail boxes and neutral export packing. We also press, fold, barcode, sort by size and mark cartons, then pack by store, by programme or to your distribution requirements.

Shipping routes

Our Shipping service delivers to the United States, Hawaii, Canada, Australia, the Caribbean, the UK and Europe by express, air, sea or door-to-door, depending on your order size and timeline.

English-speaking support

You can contact our English-speaking team by phone or video call at any point in your order, and we reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Wholesale Dress Styles and Sizes for Your Range

  • Lengths and silhouettes

    We make mini, short, midi, long and maxi dresses, beach maxis and cover-ups included, in wrap, A-line, tunic, shirt-dress, loose-fit and oversized resort silhouettes built to your specifications.

  • Tops and decorative finishes

    You choose sleeveless, halter, off-shoulder or puff-sleeved tops, finished with smocking, tiers, ruffles or embroidery. Plus-size and modest versions let you build a varied range with us.

  • Warm-weather and event ranges

    We develop summer, casual, sundress, vacation and resort ranges, plus luau, party, cocktail, evening, wedding-guest and bridesmaid pieces, in vintage, boho, elegant, designer or tropical-print looks.

  • Matching family ranges

    Our Matching Family & Group Sets service carries the same aloha print from toddler sizes up to 5XL. We scale the artwork to each garment size, so the print lines up consistently across the whole set.

  • Our strongest sellers

    Luau, summer, sundress, casual, party, boho and vacation styles sell best for us, along with long, short and off-shoulder cuts.

  • Wholesale stock selection

    Our Hawaiian dress stock includes tropical-print maxi, sundress and off-shoulder styles in hibiscus, palm-leaf and floral prints. Ask us for the current stock list to see what's available.

  • Stock versus made-to-order

    Stock dresses ship sooner and let you test demand before you move proven sellers into made-to-order production. The trade-off is that with stock you accept existing prints, cuts and sizing, not the exclusive design and fit of your own specification.

Custom Dress Construction, Trims and Finishes

Construction or detailWhat we make and what it means for your range
Faux-wrap frontsFor wholesale lines across a full size run, we stitch the wrap front in place and tack the openings closed. That keeps the neckline, skirt opening and fit consistent, but you lose the adjustable waist and the relaxed, tied look of a true wrap.
True-wrap frontsThe fronts overlap fully and tie at the waist, so the wearer adjusts the dress to her body shape or between sizes. We cut at least 10 cm of overlap at the waist so it stays covered during movement, and we can add an inner tie or snap.
Smocked bodicesWe shirr the panels with 1 mm covered elastic thread in rows 8–10 mm apart, then steam-set them. Every sample is stretched 50 times to confirm it returns to within 1 cm of its relaxed width.
Tiers and rufflesWe gather on two rows of stitching, join the tiers with overlocked seams, topstitch the seams flat and finish with a narrow rolled hem.
Embroidered trimsWe stitch embroidered trims over a soft stabiliser, so they lie flat after washing.
Trims and accessoriesOur Trims & Accessories service helps you choose button material, size, colour and attachment, as well as plackets and brand details. Metal, plastic, resin or eco-friendly accessories are colour-matched to your fabrics and prints.
Custom button quantitiesWith our Custom Buttons service, custom-dyed or logo-engraved buttons need a separate lot of 1,000 pieces per design in each colour, and we store the spares for your reorders. Colour-matched stock buttons have no separate minimum and work at your garment minimum.

Hawaiian Print Dresses: Fabric and Colour Choices

We choose the fabric to suit your silhouette, then sample your print on that exact base, so you approve the drape and colour together before bulk production.

  • Lightweight non-stretch cover-ups

    For airy, non-stretch beach cover-ups, we supply polyester and high-twist chiffon at 60–90 GSM, cotton voile and lawn at 80–110 GSM and rayon challis at 100–130 GSM.

  • Cover-ups with stretch

    If you want a cover-up with more body and stretch, we use performance knits at 140–180 GSM in place of the lighter non-stretch bases.

  • Flowing dresses

    Drapey rayon or viscose challis suits flowing maxi and tiered dresses, and modal and Tencel give you a softer hand.

  • Structured and occasion dresses

    Crisper cotton poplin or linen holds the shape of a structured shirt-dress. For bridesmaid, cocktail and evening styles, we offer polyester or silk chiffon and satin-type polyester or silk fabrics.

  • Fabric changes print appearance

    The same print looks brighter, softer or less sharp on chiffon, rayon, cotton and satin, and each base hangs differently in tiered, flowing and structured cuts.

  • Tropical print themes

    Our Custom Print Design service creates designs exclusive to your group, using hibiscus, plumeria and other florals, monstera and palm foliage, beach scenes with sunsets or surfboards, and novelty themes.

  • Printing and colour control

    Our Custom Printing service screen- or digital-prints rayon, cotton and linen before cutting. We limit sublimation to light-coloured polyester or blends with at least 60% polyester, and we hold your approved specifications, with colour within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm.

  • Solid and custom shades

    Our Garment Dyeing service works from Pantone Coated or stock colour cards and custom shades. Custom minimums depend on the fabric and dye method, we confirm feasibility during sampling, and we coordinate colour across fabric, lining and trims.

How We Keep Dress Quality Consistent with Your Approved Sample

Once your dresses are pressed, we check them against your sealed approval sample, measuring hem level and key points. That catches any stretching or easing from sewing and pressing that cutting checks miss.

Quality checkpointWhat we control for your order
Level hemsWe cut preshrunk rayon and other lightweight fabrics on the straight grain. Bias-cut and circular skirts hang for 24 hours so they drop before hemming, and then we finish them with a 0.5 cm rolled hem or a 1 cm double-turned hem.
Quality control standardsOur Quality Control service checks fabric, cutting and sewing, and you're welcome to bring in SGS or BV for inspections. We hold finished chest, length and waist within ±1 cm and small points within ±0.5 cm. Residual length and width shrinkage stays within 3% after three home washes. Washing colourfastness is Grade 4 or above, high-twist chiffon included, and rubbing and light fastness are Grade 3–4 or above. Rayon gets cold or 30°C gentle washing, no tumble drying and cool reverse ironing. Tested fabrics and trims carry OEKO-TEX Standard 100, organic cotton styles carry GOTS, cotton comes through Better Cotton (BCI), and our factory holds ISO 9001 and WRAP.
Lightweight seamsOur Cut & Sew service uses 1 cm overlocked or French seams on lightweight rayon, tested to ISO 13936-2 with no more than 6 mm seam opening. For silk and other very fine fabrics, we offer flat-felled side seams or narrow 0.6 cm French seams.

Destination-ready care labelsOur Custom Labels & Hang Tags service prepares fibre content, origin, care instructions based on wash tests, and any required RN or CA details from the actual materials, all for your artwork approval. Frequently Asked Questions

What standard and plus-size ranges do your dresses come in, and what are the bust, waist, hip, bodice-length and finished-length measurements for each size?

Standard sizes on this dress chart run XS to XL, plus sizes 2XL to 5XL. Every quote we send you includes a chart with the bust, waist, hip, bodice length and finished length for each size. For example, size M measures 92 cm at the bust, 74 cm at the waist and 98 cm at the hip, with a 40 cm bodice length. Bust, waist and hip go up by 5 cm per size in the standard sizes and by 6 cm per size in the plus sizes. In size M, finished lengths are about 85 cm for a mini, 115 cm for a midi and 140 cm for a maxi, and we adjust these to match your own chart.

Which closures and fit details can you add to a dress, such as zips, button plackets, elastic waists, adjustable straps, pockets or built-in bra cups?

We can add any of these to any dress: concealed invisible zips at the centre back or side seam, button-front plackets, elastic or shirred waists, adjustable slider straps, tie straps and tie-backs. You can also ask for in-seam side pockets and built-in support, either a sewn-in shelf bra or removable foam cups in cup sizes A to D.

What relaxed and comfortably stretched bodice measurements can you give for each size of a smocked dress?

Your spec sheet shows both measurements for every size. In size S, the bodice measures 62 cm relaxed and 92 cm comfortably stretched. Both figures go up by 5 cm per standard size and 6 cm per plus size. We set a tighter or looser stretch ratio to match the fit you need.

Can you line my Hawaiian dresses, fully or just at the bodice, so light rayon or cotton prints aren't see-through?

Yes. You can choose full lining, bodice-only lining or a half lining to mid-thigh, in cotton voile, rayon or soft polyester, in matching or nude shades. With pale rayon or cotton prints, we check transparency during sampling, both in daylight and with backlighting. If the coverage isn't enough, we switch to a denser 120–140 gsm base or add a second bodice layer.

Which choices on a Hawaiian dress raise the cost most: length, tiers and ruffles, smocking, embroidery or lining?

Length has the biggest effect on cost, because a maxi uses roughly twice as much fabric as a mini. Lining comes next because it adds a second layer of fabric. After that come extra tiers or ruffles, smocking and embroidery, which all add sewing time. Concealed zips, button plackets and pockets add less. To hit your target price, cut length, lining or the number of tiers first. That way you keep functional details such as closures, pockets and built-in support.
