Bikini Sets, Separates and Top Styles We Make

We make bikini sets and two-piece swimsuits for you under OEM and ODM programmes. That covers string, micro, triangle, bandeau, underwire, high-waisted, tankini, plus-size, bikini-short and mix-and-match styles.

For tops, you can pick fixed or sliding triangles, bandeaus with removable straps, halters that tie or clasp closed, bralettes, longline tops and sport crops.

Custom Bikini Sets and Coordinated Prints

Coordinated silhouettes

We develop and grade triangle, bandeau, bralette and high-cut styles together around one shared print, so your line stays coordinated.

Seam alignment

During sampling, we map print placement onto the patterns so the design lines up at the side seam.

Matched cutting

We mark and cut matched panels one at a time instead of stacking them, which adds 1 to 2 days to a 300-piece order. We fix matched or random placement at sampling, because changing it later disrupts production.

Colour references

With Customisation, we match every colour in your print and thread to Pantone references for your approved design.

Family coordination

For matching family and group sets from toddler sizes to 5XL, each fabric base reproduces a print differently.

So we work from one Pantone reference, scale the artwork to each garment size and sample the pieces together, and you approve them side by side before bulk.

Printing limits

Our Custom Printing uses sublimation on light-coloured fabrics with at least 60% polyester. It handles repeats up to 150 cm, compared with 64 cm for screen printing.

Large repeats or matching use 10–15% extra fabric, and that cost is included in your unit price.

Swim Fabric Performance and Lining Options

Pool or sea

For pools we use chlorine-resistant polyester-spandex or PBT blends. For sea and resort wear we use nylon-spandex, because chlorine breaks down standard elastane faster than seawater does.

Sun and colour

A bikini fabric is sold as UPF 50+ only with its AS/NZS 4399 lab report. We aim for Grade 4 colourfastness to chlorinated water and seawater, but that's a target, not a guaranteed result.

Lining and hold

You can pick self-fabric lining or a 100–130 GSM nylon/spandex swim lining. Wherever your design needs firmer support, we add front power mesh.

Wet opacity

White and pale colours get a doubled front layer or a nude-tone lining. We test each colour wet during sampling so you can see how opaque it is.

Pilling resistance

For your swim fabric specification, we pick fabrics rated Grade 3–4 or higher for pilling resistance.

Precision Cutting and Reinforced Stress Points

Reinforced stress points

Straps, side seams and the crotch get extra stitching, because pulling the suit on and wearing it wet put the most strain there.

Chlorine exposure checks

For pools, shells use chlorine-resistant elastane and edges use encased rubber elastic. At sampling, we check that stretch recovery stays at 90% or more after 50 hours in chlorinated water.

Customer-supplied materials

For Cut & Sew, we cut your custom patterns precisely. Your materials arrive before cutting, with 5–8% extra fabric and 3% spare trims and labels.

We inspect what arrives and report any shortages or defects within 2 business days, before anything is used.

Clean Edges and Lined Bikini Gussets

  • Approved edge finish

    On your sample, we agree on visible fold-over elastic binding, lining-encased elastic, or bonded edges and seams, and that finish stays the same in bulk.

  • Ruching and hold

    For scrunch-back and ruched bottoms, we sew in elastic shirring as we go. Wherever your approved design needs more hold, we add power-net lining.

  • Enclosed gusset seams

    The crotch is fully lined in self fabric or nylon/spandex swim lining. We sew the front seam and enclose the back seam, so no raw edges touch the skin.

  • Period swim construction

    Period swim bottoms get an absorbent gusset with a leak-resistant barrier, set under a swim-grade polyamide/elastane or polyester/elastane shell.

Frequently Asked Questions

For mix-and-match bikinis, does the 50-piece minimum apply to tops and bottoms separately, and can I order tops and bottoms in different sizes?

Yes. Each top and each bottom counts as its own style, so you need 50 pieces per style per colour. You can spread each style across 3 to 4 sizes at no extra charge. The two size splits don't have to match. For example, tops can run S to XL while bottoms run XS to L, and each split follows your sales history for that piece.

Can you produce fully reversible bikinis with both faces wearable and seams, pads and labels concealed?

Yes. We make both faces wearable, using either a solid and a print or two prints. The seams are enclosed, so no stitching or overlock shows. The pads sit in an internal pocket between the layers. Labels are either heat transfers or hidden inside the pad pocket.

Can you apply removable hygiene liners to bikini bottoms that stay attached through handling without leaving adhesive residue?

Yes. Every bottom comes with a removable hygienic liner. Its low-tack adhesive stays in place through folding, packing and try-ons, and then it peels off without leaving residue. On orders over 3,000 units, we add your logo.

Can you build bikini tops around our supplied cups and underwires, and what compatibility limits apply to the pattern and size range?

Yes. We adapt the cup patterns and channelling to the B to E cups and underwires you supply. The wires need to fit an 8 to 10 mm channel, and you need to send every size you want graded.

How do you check that bikini tops and bottoms retain their fit when wet and during swimming, rather than only in a dry fitting?

We fit every top and bottom dry, then fit it again after 30 minutes in water. While the model swims and moves, we check for strap slip, cup gaping and leg-line ride-up. Before pattern approval, we also check how the fabric and elastic stretch and recover when wet.
