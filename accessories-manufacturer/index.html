Accessories Made in China

We make textile resort accessories printed to match your aloha shirt line: tote bags, bucket hats, caps, scarves, bandanas, scrunchies and smaller add-ons. We make them ourselves, alongside the shirts they match, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Custom Accessories for Your Range

Accessory or optionWhat we make for you
TotesWe cut and sew your totes from cotton canvas or linen, with double-stitched side seams and bar-tacked joins where the handles meet the bag. You can choose flat shoulder-strap shoppers or gusseted beach totes with webbing or self-fabric handles. Large gusseted totes suit beach and holiday ranges.
Hats and capsYou can choose 5-panel or 6-panel caps, soft strap-back dad caps, structured snapbacks with flat or curved brims, or bucket hats with short or wide brims. We build them with multi-row stitched brims, taped inner seams and fused front panels. Dad caps and bucket hats suit relaxed resort lines, and structured snapbacks suit collections that lean towards streetwear.
Scarves and bandanasWe finish rayon, silk, cotton or polyester with narrow rolled hems on every edge. Squares work as neck and head wear. Long rectangles work as wraps and suit beach and holiday ranges.
Scrunchies and add-onsFor scrunchies, we close fabric tubes over covered elastic. We also make zip pouches, drawstring bags, pocket squares, hair bows and eye masks, and we develop other fabric accessories linked to your apparel range or brand from your brief, sketch or reference picture.
Factory ownershipOur factory-owning business has operated since 2011 and controls production and export directly for B2B buyers worldwide. You agree orders with our team, not through an online shop or third-party marketplace.
Fabric choicesOur fabric range covers cotton, polyester, spandex, nylon, rayon, viscose, modal, Tencel, linen, silk and wool, plus blends including 95% cotton 5% spandex, 80% nylon 20% spandex and 65% polyester 35% cotton.
Material developmentThrough Fabric Sourcing, you can pick from our fabric library or have a custom material developed.

Private Label Accessories and Branding

Branding by price

On lower-priced accessories, we keep branding to woven labels or branded packaging. Full prints and patches suit items whose retail prices cover the extra cost.

Printing choices

Our Custom Printing service gives you screen printing in up to 12 colours, soft-feel DTF in unlimited colours, embroidery, heat transfer, silicone printing and sewn-on labels. On aloha shirt blanks, you can have screen printing, DTF or embroidery.

Embroidery and patches

Our Embroidery service covers detailed shirt embroidery prepared by designers, plus patches and labels. Embroidered logos hold up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Private label collections

With our Private Label service, your brand is the only one on your aloha shirt collections. We coordinate approved labels, trims, tags and retail-ready packaging to your specifications.

Buttons and hardware

Our Custom Buttons service develops buttons and hardware branded with your logo. We quote tooling separately before you approve it, and we keep the moulds for 3 years so identical reorders reuse them.

Care label wording

Our care and content labels are based on wash tests of the fabric and print and on destination rules, including US, EU and Australian requirements. You approve the wording before production, and we run QC checks on every private-label order.

Matching Accessory Prints and Colours

  • Artwork scale

    We rescale your shirt motif for the smaller surfaces of hats, scarves and scrunchies so the design reads clearly on each finished accessory.

  • Printing before sewing

    We print accessory fabric flat, then cut and sew it into shape, so the finished construction determines where motifs meet.

  • Visible seam alignment

    We line motifs up at the seams people see first, including the front of a hat crown and the joins where a tote's front meets its sides and base.

  • Pattern continuity limits

    A pattern cannot run unbroken from brim to crown, across cap backs and top buttons, or along tote gussets and bottom corners. We mark placement on your sample for you to approve before bulk.

  • Matching across fabrics

    For matching family and group sets in sizes from toddler to 5XL, we scale and sample everything together. Different bases never print identically, so we match them to a shared Pantone reference and you approve each base side by side.

OEM Accessories: Design, Samples and Timing

  1. 01

    Develop your starting point

    Through Product Development, we take on OEM and ODM projects starting from your design, a picture or just an idea. Our pattern design, grading and fit development are all done in-house, which means less back-and-forth on the design.

  2. 02

    Confirm accessory specifications

    You choose which apparel prints or logos carry over. Before sampling, we agree each accessory's style, fabric, print scale, labels, patches and packaging with you.

  3. 03

    Agree the quote

    Under our process, we quote first and start making your sample only after you've agreed the price.

  4. 04

    Confirm development fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per design, meaning its pattern or print with all colourways included. Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk.

  5. 05

    Make your samples

    Through Sampling, standard samples take 10 to 15 days and have no minimum. Courier delivery costs you an extra USD 50, and we can rush a sample only when our schedule allows.

  6. 06

    Approve the finished result

    When colour matching matters, we sample the accessories alongside your key shirts. We release bulk only after you've approved the side-by-side comparison.

Materials, Quality Checks and Delivery

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Where your order falls in that range depends on its size and the season.

  • Payment schedule

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Freight terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and DDP is available worldwide.

  • Door delivery

    DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Compatible components

    Our sampled components need scrunchie elastic 3 to 10 mm wide, tote webbing 2 to 4 cm wide and no more than 3 mm thick, and brim inserts that match the pattern.

  • Buyer-supplied materials

    For Cut & Sew, your materials have to arrive before cutting, with 5–8% extra fabric and 3% spare trims and labels. We inspect them and tell you about any defects or shortages within 2 business days.

  • Quality verification

    Our quality control confirms the certification that applies, including OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and ISO 9001. It also checks every production stage up to final inspection, and independent SGS or BV inspectors are welcome.

  • Combined shipping plan

    Through our Shipping service, we agree bulk quantities, the packing method and a combined shipping plan with you before anything ships.

Frequently Asked Questions

My shirts are made by another factory. Can I order matching accessories from you alone?

Yes. We take accessory-only orders as long as the accessories connect to your apparel range or brand, even if another factory makes your shirts.

Which choices change the price of an accessory the most?

Item type and construction have the biggest effect on price. A structured 6-panel cap with buckram and an adjustable strap costs more than a flat hemmed bandana. Fabric is next: silk or linen costs more than polyester or cotton. Print method and coverage come after that, and all-over multi-colour printing costs more than a single placed logo. Moving up from branded packaging to woven labels, patches and custom hang tags adds to the unit cost. When we look for ways to cut cost, we go through these choices in order and leave branding until last.

Does your textile-only scope allow zippers, snaps, buckles and adjustment hardware within bags and hats?

Yes. We fit colour-matched zippers, snaps, buckles, eyelets and cord locks into textile bags and hats, including zip-top totes, snapback closures and adjustable buckle straps.

What sizes do your totes, scarves, bandanas and hats come in, and can I set my own dimensions?

Our standard totes are 38 x 42 cm with a 10 cm gusset. Scarves are 90 x 90 cm or 70 x 180 cm, and bandanas are 55 x 55 cm. Scrunchies come in fabric widths of 1 cm, 3.5 cm and 6 cm. Bucket hats come in S/M (56 cm) and L/XL (59 cm), and adjustable caps fit 56 to 60 cm. You can set your own dimensions for any item, including custom hat head sizes from 52 to 62 cm, and we confirm the final size on your sample.

Can you use the same fabric batch as my shirts for the accessories, and which accessory constructions would require reinforcement or a different fabric?

Yes. We can cut your accessories from the same fabric batch as your shirts.
