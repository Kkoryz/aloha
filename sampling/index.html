Hawaiian Shirt Samples: Your Options

Before you sign off on bulk production, our aloha shirt sample lets you check construction, fit, fabric, print scale, labels, trims and packing all at once.

We make the shirts ourselves in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. If you start from one of our base styles, your sample covers fit, fabric and construction, plus a first check of your labels.

Custom Samples From Our Factory

Custom print checks

We go through motif scale, colour, seam alignment, shrinkage risk, fabric hand, packaging and label placement with you, and we confirm the fit suits your customers.

Recorded decisions

We look over the physical sample with you and note every agreed point on a sample comment sheet, which stays with your style into bulk.

Direct factory relationship

You deal directly with our factory-owned business. We handle production and export ourselves, and you agree orders with our team, not through an online shop or third-party marketplace.

Development starting points

Our Product Development service handles OEM and ODM sampling from your design, picture, idea, measurements, base block or reference shirt, and we do the pattern, grading and fit work in-house.

Your Approved Sample Sets the Bulk Standard

The sample you approve becomes the standard for bulk.

We sample on the bulk fabric base with the bulk print method, so a screen-printed design gets a screen-printed sample, not a digital stand-in. Bulk follows the same colour reference, with printed colour held within ΔE ≤ 2.0 and placement within 0.5 cm of the approved sample.

Because the ink and fabric stay the same, the hand feel stays the same too. We keep a signed counter-sample and compare bulk fabric and finished shirts under the same lighting. Any change needs your written approval.

Sample Types and Lead Times

Send your final artwork, fabric direction and the sample types you need at the same time, and we'll plan all the rounds on one schedule.

  • Standard sample timing

    Your sample is ready 10 to 15 days after you confirm the artwork and fabric direction.

  • Seven sample types

    You can choose fabric test, first, fit, photo, revised, pre-production (PP) and repeat samples. Our quick-response lines handle fit, photo and PP work.

  • Fabric and trims

    Confirming your fabric and trims takes 1 to 3 days.

  • Print plate preparation

    Making the print plates for your sample takes 3 to 5 days.

  • Your approval window

    Once the sample is delivered, your fit review and sample approval take 3 to 7 days.

  • Garment dyeing allowance

    For Garment Dyeing, lab dips add about 5 to 7 days to the standard sample schedule.

Sample Fees and Delivery

  • Sample and design fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per pattern or print, and that covers all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  • Courier cost and timing

    Courier delivery of your sample costs USD 50 on top of the sampling fees. Tracked DHL delivery reaches your door in 5 to 7 days.

  • Dispatch information

    Before we dispatch, we need your full delivery address, a contact phone number and any import details.

  • Quoted development scope

    Our quotation lists any development charges separately from bulk and leaves out extras that aren't listed. We don't commit to any additional cost until you approve it.

  • Upfront sample payment

    Under our payment terms, we invoice sample and design fees in USD. You pay them before sampling by T/T bank transfer or PayPal.

  • Export shipping paperwork

    Our Shipping service packs your goods securely and handles all the export paperwork, including the commercial invoice, packing list and customs documents.

Fit Checks and Sample Testing

  • Fit sample

    What we do
    We make one sample per style in size S or your chosen fit size to test fit, stretch and construction. Then we correct anything that needs it.

  • Measurement review

    What we do
    Before dispatch we measure the sample against your measurement sheet, and the results go out with the sample. Fit approval is based on measurements, not photos, and we offer live fitting videos.

  • Fabric testing

    What we do
    Before sampling, our Quality Control service wash-tests the fabric for shrinkage and colour bleed. After three home washes, dimensional change has to stay within 3% in both length and width.

  • Full size range

    What we do
    On Cut & Sew orders, we add a sample in every size you order before bulk, each at the sample fee. You can review the sewing and finishing before sampling or production.

When to Request a Sample

Compare fabric choices

If you haven't chosen a base fabric yet, sampling lets you compare cotton, rayon, nylon-spandex, quick-dry polyester or a linen blend on your actual shirt.

Check print scale

For a new custom print, we sample on the real silhouette so you can see how the motif works on the finished shirt.

Prepare marketing samples

We finish photo samples with final labels, buttons and retail packaging so they match your production shirts. You can supply these items, or we source them to your artwork.

Feel fabrics first

Through Hawaiian Fabrics, you can order a separate swatch pack for USD 80, DHL Express delivery included. We credit that amount against your first bulk purchase order.

Choose your cut

Our aloha shirt range covers men, women and children in 4 cuts: relaxed men's/unisex, shaped women's, men's tailored slim and women's vintage.

Approve matching sets

For Matching Family & Group Sets, different fibres render Pantone colours differently. So we match each base to one reference and get your approval with the pieces side by side.

We also sample the artwork scaled from toddler sizes up to 5XL together.

Your Sample Brief and Materials

  • Brief without a tech pack

    How it supports sampling
    You don't need a tech pack. Send us a reference shirt or base style code, artwork references, size targets, fabric direction and trim notes. High-resolution or layered artwork leaves less to interpretation.

  • Your printed fabric

    How it supports sampling
    For one aloha shirt, send about 2 metres at 140–150 cm width. A large repeat, or a print matched across the front placket and pocket, needs 3 metres.

  • Collar direction

    How it supports sampling
    Our Customisation service offers camp, spread or notch collars, and we adjust the size and shape to suit your design.

  • Questions and discussion

    How it supports sampling
    Contact our English-speaking team and you'll get a reply within one business day. We're available for phone or video calls throughout your project.

From Quote to Sample Approval

  1. Agree the quote

    Our ordering process begins with a quotation, and we start work on your sample once you agree the price.

  2. Preview your artwork

    With Custom Print Design, you get a 3D mockup from us within 48 hours of sending your artwork.

  3. Confirm sample requirements

    We confirm the fabric, print method, sample and design fees, label requirements and timing with you. Before we cut, you approve the artwork scale, placement, colour direction and construction.

  4. Approve colour proofs

    You approve either a lab dip for plain grounds, which is fabric dyed to your Pantone reference or swatch, or a strike-off, which is a trial print on the actual shirt fabric that shows the ink, colours and hand feel.

  5. Review the garment

    You see the finished sample on a body and mark your corrections point by point, and we work through each one.

  6. Choose the next stage

    You either approve the style for bulk or ask for another sample round. We carry out a full final inspection before shipment.

Bulk Order Minimums and Timing

  • Sample quantity and rush

    There's no minimum for samples, whether you're using a base style or a custom print. A single sample piece confirms your style before you commit to bulk, and rush work depends on our schedule.

  • Bulk order minimum

    Bulk orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which gives small and growing labels a way in.

  • First trial quantities

    We agree your first trial quantity case by case, and we flex as far as production allows.

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  • Bulk payment stages

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

  • Delivery terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • Fabric preparation allowance

    With Fabric Sourcing, stock fabric arrives within 3 to 5 days. Custom-dyed or custom-developed fabric adds 15 to 20 days before bulk starts.

  • Reorder colour approval

    On Bulk Production reorders, a new colourway keeps the bulk minimum and needs a strike-off rather than another full sample. The strike-off adds 5 to 7 days.

Label, Trim and Packaging Samples

Send your final label, hangtag and packaging artwork with your sample request, and we'll make the mockups alongside the garment so we can check them all together.

Private label options

Our Private Label service is available at every order quantity. We make woven labels and heat-transfer artwork in-house, along with other branding options.

Care label approval

Through Custom Labels & Hang Tags, we work from the actual materials to prepare fibre, origin and wash-tested care information for your destination. We add any required RN or CA details and get your approval on the artwork.

Embroidered branding

Our Embroidery service develops detailed designs, patches and labels, and the embroidered logos hold up to repeated washing and hard wear without fraying.

Packing reference

Our Packaging service puts each shirt in its own polybag as standard, or packs to your specification. Before bulk, a PP reference fixes the folding, hangers, tissue, bags, label placement and carton pressure.

Custom trim timing

With Trims & Accessories, woven labels take 7 to 15 days alongside shirt sampling, and custom buttons add 7 to 10 days to bulk production.

Button minimums

Custom Buttons need 1,000 pieces per design per colour, and we store the spares for your reorders. Colour-matched stock buttons have no separate minimum and work for a 50-piece shirt run.

Frequently Asked Questions

What deviations from my measurement sheet are acceptable for the sample's chest, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length?

Chest and body length stay within ±1 cm of your size chart. Shoulder width stays within -0.5 to 0.5 cm, and sleeve length within -1 to 1 cm. If any point falls outside these limits, we correct it before you approve the style.

If the fabric I choose isn't available in time for the sample, do you sample on a substitute, and how is that handled when bulk fabric arrives?

Yes. If you agree, we use the closest fabric we have in stock for the first fit sample. Bulk approval is based on the pre-production sample made with your bulk fabric and bulk trims, not on the substitute.

If I'm buying from your in-stock styles, can I get a sample of those faster than a custom sample?

Yes. An in-stock wholesale sample comes straight from stock and ships within 3 business days of payment, while a custom sample takes 10 to 15 days to make. Ask us for our current stock list, and you can pick styles, colours and sizes and check our workmanship before you pay for custom sampling.

A substitute fit sample does not become the bulk reference.

  1. Agree substitute fabric

    If the chosen fabric cannot arrive in time, agree to use the closest fabric held in the factory.

  2. Make first fit sample

    Use the agreed substitute fabric.

  3. Receive bulk fabric

    The chosen bulk fabric arrives.

  4. Make pre-production sample

    Use the bulk fabric and bulk trims.

  5. Check bulk against reference

    The pre-production sample becomes the reference for checking the bulk order.
