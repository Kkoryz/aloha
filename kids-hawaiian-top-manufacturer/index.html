Kid Hawaiian Top Production in China

If you're looking for an OEM kids' Hawaiian top manufacturer that works with small and growing labels, our orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour.

We make every top in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. For a trial run, we agree the quantity case by case within our production limits. A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days.

Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, and your order size and the season decide where it falls in that range.

Custom Kids' Tops: Fits, Collars & Sleeves

Pick sizes by body height, not age. Children of the same age vary in height, so our height-based bands give you a more reliable fit.

Toddler height bands

Our toddler tops fit body heights of 86–92 cm (2T), 92–98 cm (3T) and 98–104 cm (4T).

Youth height bands

Our youth sizes cover 110–122 cm (5/6), 122–134 cm (7/8), 134–152 cm (10/12) and 152–164 cm (14/16).

Kids' pricing

Your quote lists each kids' size band on its own line. Kids' tops are priced separately because they use less fabric and rescaled prints.

Size allocation

You can split the minimum order for each style and colour across 3 to 4 kids' sizes at no extra charge.

Collar options

You can choose a camp collar (also called Cuban, revere or cabana), mandarin, button-down, athletic V-neck or classic crew neck. If you need a custom collar, we develop it and finalise it during sampling.

Sleeve lengths

You can choose short, 3/4 or long sleeves. Going with long sleeves doesn't change your print, fit or fabric weight.

Gentle neckline finishes

At sampling, you choose soft cotton seam tape, gentle self-fabric or jersey binding, or a seam fully enclosed inside the collar, so no raw edges touch the neck.

Collar structure

With Cut & Sew, you choose at sampling between standard lightweight fusible interlining for crisp cotton and soft sewn-in or no interlining for drapey rayon. Each option shapes camp collars and plackets without making them stiff.

Kids' Top Safety Standards & Market Testing

Safety requirementWhat happens on your order
Destination marketsTell us your markets at the start of development, so we choose compliant trims and construction before sampling. If you change buttons, snaps or neck construction after approval, it adds time and a risk of re-testing.
Fastener constructionWe lockstitch every button with a thread-wrapped shank and run pull and tear-strength checks on all functional trims. For babies and toddlers, you can choose snaps or sewn buttons with reinforced backing to secure small parts.
Dressing trade-offSnaps make dressing babies and toddlers quicker, but they replace the classic look of real aloha-shirt buttons.
Pull testingWe pull each batch of buttons and snaps at 90 N for 10 seconds during sewing and again at final inspection; any detachment or damage counts as a failure.
Nickel releaseOur metal buttons and snaps meet the EU REACH limit of 0.5 µg/cm²/week. Nickel-free plating is standard on baby styles, and you can request it for other ranges.
Market rulesDuring development, we check your US orders against CPSIA lead and phthalate limits, 16 CFR Part 1501 small-parts rules and 16 CFR Part 1610 flammability requirements, and your EU orders against EN 14682 cord and drawstring rules.
Certificates and US documentationWe confirm certificate coverage for every trim before you order. A CPSC-accepted laboratory carries out US lead, phthalate and flammability testing, and we prepare supporting paperwork based on your order so you can issue your Children's Product Certificate, along with permanent tracking labels worded the way you specify.
Other markets and feesWe book destination-specific tests with accredited third-party laboratories. US children's tests are required and always quoted; other markets' tests follow your approval, all paid in advance at the lab's rate per test item, with each report sent to you.
Quality controlOur Quality Control runs from incoming fabric through full final inspection, and SGS or BV inspectors are welcome. We hold chest, length and waist within ±1 cm and small points within ±0.5 cm, keep residual shrinkage of preshrunk fabric within 3% in both directions after three home washes, and reach washing colourfastness of Grade 4 or above—including high-twist chiffon—with rubbing and light fastness at Grade 3–4 or above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do toddler and older-child sizes use different proportions and movement allowances, or are all children's sizes graded from one base fit?

We develop two base patterns rather than one. Our toddler block for 2T to 4T has a shorter body, a rounder chest and about 8 cm of chest ease for crawling and climbing. Our children's block for 5/6 to 14/16 has a longer body, longer sleeves and about 10 cm of ease. If you're ordering both ranges, approve a sample from each block, because one block's fit doesn't establish the other's.

How do your crew-neck and V-neck tops provide enough head-opening room for younger children, particularly when made in non-stretch fabric?

On woven, non-stretch crew-neck and V-neck tops, we add a 6–8 cm opening at the back neck or shoulder with one button or snap, or we deepen the V, so the neckline opens to at least 52 cm. Knit or stretch tops use a rib neckband that stretches over the head without an opening.

For tops intended for children under three, are the selected fabrics, print materials and trims covered by OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Product Class I certification?

Before you order, we confirm which certificate covers each fabric, trim, dye or ink in your style, kids' items included: our OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Product Class I certificate for children up to 36 months or our Class II certificate for sizes over 36 months.
