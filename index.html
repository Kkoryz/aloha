Your Aloha Shirt Factory in Guangzhou

You can order aloha shirts from 50 pieces per style in each colour, a minimum set for small and growing labels.

We make every shirt ourselves in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. If you want a first trial run, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within our production limits.

Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

From Sample to Shipment

Your order moves from development through production checks to dispatch, and sampling is charged separately from your bulk order.

  • Sample and design fees

    For Sampling, each sample costs USD 50, design is USD 50 per design and courier delivery adds USD 50. Sample and design fees are not refunded or credited against bulk.

  • Quotes within 24 hours

    To Request a Quote, send spec sheets, sketches, a tech pack or photos. You get the quote within 24 hours, valid for 30 days.

  • One direct team

    Through Our Process, the same team develops and sews your order, and a dedicated account manager is your contact until shipment.

  • Quality checks and certificates

    Our Quality Control covers incoming fabric, cutting, sewing and random checks, backed by OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, BCI, ISO 9001 and WRAP.

  • Printing and embroidery

    Custom Printing combines cut and sew with in-house screen, DTF and sublimation printing, and Embroidery covers logos, patches and labels.

  • Your brand only

    Private Label keeps our name off the garments, and Custom Labels & Hang Tags drafts fibre, care and origin labels for your approval.

  • Worldwide DDP shipping

    DDP delivery is available worldwide, with duties, customs and freight in one price.

  • After-delivery support

    Under our Terms and Conditions, report quality problems within 7 days with photos or videos. We give a solution within 24 hours: repair, remake, replacement, discount or credit.

An Aloha Shirt Manufacturer That Grows With Your Brand

Early on, we ask whether your shirts are for resort retail, a souvenir programme, matching family sets or a private-label holiday collection, then suggest cuts, fabrics, labelling and packing for that channel.

Resort retail needs a full size spread and swing tags, while family sets need the same print across adult and children's sizes.

Our company and factory background puts production and export in one business, so quality, cost and delivery have stayed under our direct control since the factory opened in 2011. You agree every order with our team directly, with no online shop or third-party marketplace store.

Our Product Development supports OEM and ODM projects from your design, a picture or an idea, with in-house pattern design, grading and fit development. For Bulk Production, our factory makes up to 34,500 pieces a month, and runs above 57,500 units can use our expanded production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you print the Hawaiian fabrics yourselves, or do you buy printed fabric from mills?

We do screen, DTF and sublimation printing ourselves, in our own factory. You can also choose DTG and specialty printing, printed or embroidered logos, digital and hand embroidery, and finishing treatments such as dye washes and garment washing.

Can you turn my own artwork into an original aloha print, and will that print belong only to my brand?

Yes. We turn your artwork into an original aloha print that stays your intellectual property, and we never sell a print made exclusively for you to another client. We take your sketches, moodboards or references and develop them into production-ready repeat artwork. That means we separate the colours, set up the repeats, scale them to the garment type, refine the placement and prepare 3D mockups. We also sign a non-disclosure agreement to protect your designs and business information. We plan pattern matching at the artwork stage. Before sampling, we set the repeat size and panel placement so the print runs continuously across the collar, placket, pocket and hem. Misalignment can't be fixed once the panels are cut, so matching has to be built into the repeat and cutting plan from the start.

How do you check the goods during sewing and before they ship, and what happens if a batch fails?

Our own QC team inspects your shirts in-line while they're being sewn, and our multi-stage checks finish with a 100% final garment inspection. You're welcome to bring in independent inspectors, including SGS or BV, before shipment. You book and pay the agency directly and give us at least 3 business days' notice of the inspection date. If the goods fail because of our workmanship, we rework them and pay the re-inspection fee.
